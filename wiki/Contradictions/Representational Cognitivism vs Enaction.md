# Representational Cognitivism vs Enaction
> Is cognition the recovery of a pregiven world by a pregiven subject, or the enactment of a world and a mind through a history of embodied action?

## The Positions

### Cognitivism / strong representation

In *The Embodied Mind* (1991), [[Thinkers/Francisco J. Varela]], [[Thinkers/Evan Thompson]], and [[Thinkers/Eleanor Rosch]] isolate a research program that crystallized in 1956 (Simon, Chomsky, Minsky, McCarthy) out of cybernetics:

> "cognition can actually be defined as computations of symbolic representations." "The only way we can account for intelligence and intentionality is to hypothesize that cognition consists of acting on the basis of representations that are physically realized in the form of a symbolic code."

**Strong representation** (ch. 8): (1) the world is pregiven; (2) cognition is *of* that world; (3) we represent its features and act on those representations. Success is accurate recovery — Marr's "shape from shading," color as surface reflectance, problem-solving in a crystalline task domain. Connectionism can drop discrete symbols and still keep this success-criterion: a network's global state "is said to represent the learned item." Adaptationism is "the precise homologue" in biology: optimal fit to a pregiven environment.

Vault inhabitants of this pole (they are not identical): Newell & Simon physical symbol systems; [[Thinkers/Marvin Minsky]] frames and societies of mind as still-representational architecture; Fodor / Pylyshyn systematicity; [[Thinkers/Douglas Hofstadter]] isomorphism; [[Concepts/Strong AI and Computational Sufficiency (Chalmers)|Chalmers on computational sufficiency]]. [[Contradictions/Dreyfus vs Computationalism - Skilled Coping vs Formal Rules]] is the neighboring fight about *formal rules and facts*. This page is the fight about *pregiven world plus recovery*.

### Enaction

> "Cognition is not the representation of a pregiven world by a pregiven mind but is rather the enactment of a world and a mind on the basis of a history of the variety of actions that a being in the world performs."

The enactive approach: perception is perceptually guided action; cognitive structures emerge from recurrent sensorimotor patterns. Autonomous systems have operational closure; they "enact a world as a domain of distinctions." Color is "neither pregiven nor represented but rather experiential and enacted." Evolution is [[Concepts/Natural Drift (Varela)|natural drift]] (viability, not Mount Fitness). Brooks: "use the world as its own model."

They do not take the subjectivist "egg" (the system just projects a world). "World and perceiver specify each other"; arising together is "not arbitrary." They do not, in ch. 8, deny a physical milieu of perturbations. What they deny is a world of *ready-made features* recovered by a mind that exists independently of that world.

The first-person partner is [[Concepts/Mindfulness-Awareness as Method (Varela)|mindfulness/awareness]]. The philosophical close is Madhyamaka [[Concepts/Groundlessness and the Middle Way (Varela)|groundlessness]]: objectivism and nihilism share a grasping root. See [[Concepts/Cartesian Anxiety (Varela)]].

## Where They Disagree

| Issue | Cognitivism / strong representation | Enaction |
|---|---|---|
| What is cognition? | Computation over representations of a pregiven world | Embodied action that enacts a world |
| Success criterion | Accurate recovery / task correspondence | Viability; entering a shared world of significance |
| Self | Not needed for the mechanism; ignored in life | Unfindable in experience; clinging is the problem |
| Body | Implementation, or a sensor package | Condition of the domain that is enacted |
| Evolution | Adaptation / optimal fit | Natural drift / proscriptive viability |
| Experience | Often epiphenomenal (Jackendoff) | Needs a disciplined method; not leftover |
| Foundations | World or subject as ground | No ground beyond the history of coupling |

## Third Voices

**[[Thinkers/Hubert Dreyfus]]** is an ally against recovery-of-facts and world-models, and a critic of the book's method. He doubts cognitive science when it is equated with cognitivism (1991 intro). He does not adopt enacted-color metaphysics or Madhyamaka. His 1993 review rejects mindfulness as inward observation (Thompson 2016 accepts). See [[Concepts/Embodied Intelligence and the Role of the Body (Dreyfus)]].

**[[Thinkers/Andy Clark]]** lists this book as kin (1998) and later mechanizes coupling with predictive processing. He keeps *models* (generative models, precision). Extension of *belief* into a notebook is not enactment of a *world*. Do not treat 4E as the 1991 thesis.

**[[Thinkers/Jakob Hohwy]]** keeps prediction-error recovery behind a sensory boundary — a sophisticated inner ground. That is the Cartesian anxiety restaged, not escaped.

**[[Thinkers/John Searle]]** is named in the book (Chinese Room; intentionality). His biological-causal-power objection is a different axis ([[Contradictions/Hofstadter vs Searle - Strange Loops vs Chinese Room]]). Enaction locates the failure in recovery-of-a-pregiven-world, not in syntax-versus-semantics.

**[[Thinkers/Anil Seth]]** later takes Maturana-style life-first continuity. His "controlled hallucination" still recovers/predicts bodily variables. Neighbor, not identity.

## The Deepest Clash

Both sides can drop von Neumann symbols. The remainder is whether a world of ready-made features is *there to be recovered*. Cognitivism (and adaptationism, and much connectionism, and much later deep learning) says yes. Enaction says the cognitive world is brought forth, and that treating either that world or the subject as a foundation is the same anxiety. Groundlessness is the cost of the second answer; nihilism is what happens if you take the cost without a path.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- Connectionism sits on the fence: right architecture (emergence, no CPU), still a representational success-criterion in ch. 5.
- Whether later "embodied cognition" (Barsalou, Shapiro, Clark) kept the anti-recovery claim or domesticated it as "the body helps representation" is Thompson 2016's worry.
