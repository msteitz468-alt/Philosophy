# Hohwy vs Clark — Secluded Inference vs Extended Mind

> Same Helmholtz/Friston mechanism; opposite geography of the mind. [[Thinkers/Jakob Hohwy]]: prediction-error minimization is optimized relative to the nervous system, behind a veil of sensory input. [[Thinkers/Andy Clark]]: the prediction machine is an action-oriented engagement machine, extension-friendly, not a secluded inference engine. Hohwy names the split in 2013; *Surfing Uncertainty* (2016) is Clark's systematic reply.

## The Positions

### Hohwy: Secluded Inference

From *The Predictive Mind* (2013), now primary ([[Sources/The Predictive Mind - Jakob Hohwy (2013)]]):

1. The brain has only sensory effects and must infer hidden causes ([[Concepts/Perception as Causal Inference (Hohwy)]]). "Without helping ourselves to the answer by going beyond the perspective of the skull-bound brain" (p. 15).
2. Causes "remain hidden behind the veil of sensory input and can only be inferred" (p. 50). We are "trapped inside our skulls with only sensory data to go by" (p. 224).
3. Perception and action are both inferences on the statistics of sensory input. Action does not buy embodiment or externalism. "It is better… to live with the risk of radical scepticism than to belie the core idea" (p. 221).
4. Tools, language, and built environments are real and useful. They "optimiz[e] the information channel from the world to the senses" (p. 238). That *accentuates* indirectness: we tidy the world *because* the mind–world link is fragile.
5. Clark is right that situatedness makes environment and brains "genuinely continuous" — "but only in the sense that it makes the causal link from the senses to the world more reliable… the mind remains secluded from the hidden causes of the world" (p. 239).
6. A wholly extended mind would require PEM "relative to a distributed system taking input from hidden causes extraneous to it." Conceivable, not the best explanation of Clark's cases. PEM is optimized "relative to just the nervous system" (p. 241).
7. The sensory boundary "is not arbitrary, but rather a principled, indispensable, and epistemically critical element for identifying what is inferring what" (p. 240).

Held in: [[Concepts/Secluded Mind and the Sensory Boundary (Hohwy)]], [[Concepts/Prediction Error Minimization (Hohwy)]], [[Thinkers/Jakob Hohwy]].

### Clark: Action-Oriented Engagement (2016) and Extended Prediction Machines

From "The Extended Mind" (1998), *Surfing Uncertainty* (2016), and *The Experience Machine* (2023), all now primary.

**2016 is the systematic reply Hohwy was waiting for** (Ch 6, "Beyond Fantasy"):

1. Hohwy's term **"evidentiary boundary"** is reported, not adopted. Clark quotes Hohwy: "the mind appears to be secluded from the world, it seems to be more neurocentrically skull-bound than embodied or extended."
2. The tension lives in an over-demanding notion of **inference**. "‘Inference’, as it functions in the PP story, is not compelled to deliver internal states that bear richly reconstructive contents." It can pick frugal, action-involving procedures.
3. Two mistakes in the fantasy/VR reading: (i) treating inference as a "representational veil"; (ii) ignoring action. "It is the guidance of world-engaging action, not the production of 'accurate' internal representations, that is the real purpose of the prediction error minimizing routine itself" (6.1).
4. **"Don't Fear the Demon."** Hohwy ties seclusion to Cartesian/Matrix global skepticism and a "veil of transduction." Clark: "red herring." The mere possibility of a fixed sensory play does not refute non-reconstructive embodied strategies. Limited seclusion (world as specified by partially self-induced sensory flow) ≠ a rich inner simulacrum.
5. Preferred label, if needed: **"not-indirect perception."** A working system "disperses the fog of surface statistics"; it does not interpose a virtual reality (6.14).
6. On extension itself, 2016 is more cautious than 2023: PP "does not materially alter" the 1998 arguments pro or con; it supplies an "extension-friendly" neural story (8.9). See [[Concepts/Action-Oriented Engagement Machine (Clark)]], [[Concepts/Productive Laziness and Cognitive Niches (Clark)]].

**1998 / 2023** (still load-bearing):

7. [[Concepts/Active Externalism (Clark and Chalmers)]]: the environment drives cognitive processes in the here-and-now ([[Concepts/Parity Principle (Clark and Chalmers)]]).
8. [[Arguments/Otto and Inga (Clark and Chalmers)]]: Otto's notebook *is* the memory. Beliefs extend; experiences "may be determined internally."
9. 2023 slogan: predictive brains are "the perfect internal organs for the creation of extended minds"; we are "natural-born cyborgs."

Held in: [[Concepts/Action-Oriented Engagement Machine (Clark)]], [[Concepts/Extended Mind (Clark and Chalmers)]], [[Concepts/Predictive Processing (Clark)]], [[Thinkers/Andy Clark]].

## What they share

- Helmholtzian unconscious inference; hierarchical generative models; prediction error as the currency; expected precision as attention; action as the other direction of fit; Friston as the technical source.
- Rejection of the passive "smart camera" / wax-imprint picture.
- Rejection of a homuncular sense-data screen.
- Agreement that we stack environments (words, tools, buildings) to make prediction cheaper.

## Where they actually disagree

The disagreement is not "does the environment matter?" It is **where PEM is computed** and **whether the sensory boundary is the mind's boundary**.

| | Hohwy | Clark |
|---|---|---|
| Location of PEM | Nervous system | Organism–world coupling; notebooks can be in the loop |
| Sensory boundary | Principled, identifies the agent | "Magical membrane" (Hurley); not sacred |
| Tools | Raise channel precision | Can *be* cognitive vehicles |
| Scepticism | Live risk, accepted | World-contact via error correction; less interested in the veil |
| Consciousness | Hard problem declined, conceivability kept | Later deflationism (2023); 1998 already keeps experience internal |

A third, smaller split on **psychosis**: Hohwy 2013 treats delusions as *low* sensory gain / overweight priors (exaggerated expectation of noisy PE). Clark 2023 treats psychosis as *high-precision prediction-error*. Clark **2016** is closer to Hohwy than 2023 is: high-precision false PE *or* reduced higher-level precision / failed sensory attenuation are treated as Bayesian-equivalent and clinically distinct (Ch 7). Autism: 2016 uses Pellicano & Burr hypo-priors *and* Van de Cruys inflexible precision, fused as weakened hyperpriors — not yet the single 2023 slogan. See [[Concepts/Precision-Weighting (Clark)]].

A fourth split on **the hard problem**: Hohwy will not touch it. Clark 2016 only "creeps up" (interoceptive incrementalism; "too early to say"). Clark 2023 dissolves it. Same mechanism does not force the same metaphilosophy of consciousness.

A fifth split on **the slogan**. Hohwy 2013 does not say "controlled hallucination." Clark 2016 discusses the slogan and *resists* it as a metaphysics of what we perceive. Clark 2023 popularizes it. Seth claims it independently.

## Why this is a major contradiction

It is not a verbal dispute about "the word *mind*." Hohwy's PEM story *uses* the sensory boundary to say what is inferring what. Collapse the boundary and, on his account, the agent disappears. Clark's parity principle *uses* functional role to say that location is not a primitive reason to withhold the label. They can both be right about the neuroscience and still not both be right about Otto.

Dreyfus is in the background. Clark tried to keep a world-involving mind and add a mechanism. Hohwy kept the mechanism and rebuilt a sophisticated internalism. See [[Contradictions/Dreyfus vs Computationalism - Skilled Coping vs Formal Rules]].
