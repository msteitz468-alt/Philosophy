# Kierkegaard vs Hegel - Faith vs Sittlichkeit

> Can the single individual, as particular, stand higher than the ethical whole — or is that "moral evil" to be annulled in *Sittlichkeit*?

## The Conflict

### Position A: Hegel — the ethical is the universal
The individual's moral task is to identify with the totality of social life. Public morality and the principles of political cohesion are the expression of universal human goals. If there is a human *telos*, that is where it finds expression. "The State in and by itself is the ethical whole" (Hannay quotes *Philosophy of Right*, Knox p. 279). In "Good and Conscience" the individual seen merely as the single individual is a "moral form of evil" to be annulled in the teleology of ethical life. The outer (*Entäusserung*) is higher than the inner. Faith, as immediacy, is a provisional form of consciousness; philosophy goes further and transcribes it into shareable concepts. Mediation is how thought works, and mediation is by virtue of the universal.

**Key anchors:** [[Thinkers/Hegel]], [[Concepts/Geist (Absolute Spirit)]], [[Concepts/Dialectical Method (Hegel)]], [[Concepts/Cunning of Reason (Hegel)]]. The *Philosophy of Right* itself is not a vault primary; Johannes's Hegel is the Hegel of Copenhagen Hegelians (Martensen) plus the *Sittlichkeit* formula Hannay cites.

### Position B: Johannes *de silentio* — if that is right, Abraham is a murderer
Faith is the paradox that the single individual as the particular is higher than the universal and stands in an absolute relation to the absolute. That cannot be mediated. Abraham's act has no social intention, no higher ethical *telos* inside the ethical, no disclosure. The temptation *is* the ethical. Either there is a teleological suspension, an absolute duty to God, and a justified concealment — or Hegelians must stop praising Abraham and remit him as a murderer. They want both: the System *and* the father of faith. Johannes will not let them have both.

Going further than faith is the Hegelian cheapening the Preface and Epilogue attack. Faith is not immediacy; it is a later interiority after infinite resignation. Socrates already went further than cheap faith. Philosophy cannot give an account of faith and should not cheat people by making it nothing.

**Key anchors:** [[Thinkers/Søren Kierkegaard]], [[Sources/Fear and Trembling - Kierkegaard (Hannay 1985)]], [[Concepts/Teleological Suspension of the Ethical (Kierkegaard)]], [[Concepts/The Single Individual (Kierkegaard)]], [[Arguments/Three Problemata (Kierkegaard)]].

## What Is Not Settled

- Johannes is a pseudonym who says he does not have faith and still treats the ethical-as-universal as the only going principle. The contradiction is between Hegelian ethics and *Abraham as Johannes reconstructs him*, not yet between Hegel and a Kierkegaardian ethics stated in Kierkegaard's own name. Hannay thinks that ethics is reserved for *Postscript*.
- The vault's Hegel is strongest on logic, phenomenology, and history, weakest on *Sittlichkeit*. A *Philosophy of Right* ingest would make Position A primary rather than reported-from-Johannes-plus-Hannay.
- A third pole already in the vault: [[Thinkers/Kant]] also makes the ethical universal, but as a law of reason, not as the State. Johannes's hoop catches him too; that fight is [[Contradictions/Kierkegaard vs Kant - Absolute Duty vs Universal Law]].

## Implications for the Vault

This is the first primary attack on Hegel that is not Popper's historicism charge or Marx's inversion. It is an attack on the *ethical* Hegel: whether becoming human is conforming ever more successfully to a general specification of the human. Sartre's legislator and Heidegger's *das Man* both inherit pieces of this fight without inheriting Abraham's second movement.
