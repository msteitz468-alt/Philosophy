# Seth vs Chalmers — Real Problem vs Hard Problem

> Same datum — there is something it is like. Opposite agendas. [[Thinkers/David Chalmers]]: after the functions are explained, *why* they are accompanied by experience remains, and materialism fails. [[Thinkers/Anil Seth]]: explain, predict, and control the *properties* of experience until that residual *why* evaporates — dissolving, not solving, the hard problem.

## The Positions

### Chalmers: the hard problem is residual and load-bearing

From *The Conscious Mind* (1996), now primary ([[Sources/The Conscious Mind - David J. Chalmers (1996)]]):

1. Easy problems (discrimination, integration, report, access) yield to standard cognitive science. The hard problem is why their performance is *accompanied by experience*.
2. Phenomenal consciousness fails to supervene logically on the physical ([[Arguments/Zombie Argument (Chalmers)]], inverted spectra, Mary, absence of analysis).
3. Materialism is therefore false. [[Concepts/Naturalistic Dualism (Chalmers)]] — property dualism plus fundamental psychophysical laws — takes its place.
4. Constraint: **take consciousness seriously** (no deflationary redefinition). NCC catalogues and workspace theories explain *awareness*, not experience.
5. Constructive side: coherence with awareness, organizational invariance, double-aspect information. Substrate-neutral machine consciousness follows ([[Concepts/Machine Consciousness and Mind Uploading (Chalmers)]]).

Held in: [[Concepts/Hard Problem of Consciousness (Chalmers)]], [[Thinkers/David Chalmers]].

### Seth: the real problem dissolves the residual why

From *Being You* (2021), now primary ([[Sources/Being You - Anil Seth (2021)]]):

1. Accept that consciousness exists. Do not start with *why it is in the universe*. Do not sweep it under the carpet.
2. The primary goals are to *explain, predict, and control* phenomenological properties — level, content, self — via mechanisms in embodied, embedded brains ([[Concepts/Real Problem of Consciousness (Seth)]]).
3. The historical model is vitalism. Life looked irreducible until its properties were mechanized. "The fatal flaw of vitalism was to interpret a failure of imagination as an insight into necessity. This is the same flaw that lies at the heart of the zombie argument."
4. Zombies become less conceivable as we know more about brains. The hard-problem intuition should "vanish in a puff of metaphysical smoke."
5. IIT is the neuroscientific theory that tries to *solve* the hard problem head-on by identifying consciousness with Φ. That ambition makes its distinctive claim untestable. Keep I+I as properties; drop the identity ([[Concepts/Phi and Integrated Information (Seth)]]).
6. The same strategy, run through controlled hallucination and the beast machine, is supposed to reconcile the physical with the phenomenal without dualism. Metaphysics: functionally agnostic physicalism.

Held in: [[Concepts/Real Problem of Consciousness (Seth)]], [[Thinkers/Anil Seth]].

## What they share

- Nagel's "something it is like" as the mark of the explanandum.
- Dissatisfaction with mere NCC lists.
- Refusal of mysterianism (McGinn) as a stopping point.
- Interest in a mapping between structure of experience and structure of mechanism (Chalmers: structural coherence / mapping problem, which Seth notes; Seth: non-arbitrary explanatory bridges).

## Where they split

| | Chalmers | Seth |
|---|---|---|
| After the functions are explained | experience is still left over | the leftover *why* was a failure of imagination |
| Zombies | conceivable, therefore materialism false | less conceivable with more knowledge; conceivability is not insight |
| Vitalism analogy | the easy/hard split is *not* like vitalism; experience is a further fact | it *is* like vitalism, and we should run the same playbook |
| Machine consciousness | fading qualia ⇒ substrate-neutral | functionalism unjustified; life probably required |
| Take consciousness seriously | forbids deflationary redefinition | accepts experience, redefines the *question* |

## Nearby voices

- **[[Thinkers/Andy Clark]]**: same dissolve neighbourhood via the *meta-problem* and self-opacity ([[Concepts/Predictive Processing and the Hard Problem (Clark)]]). Not Seth's vitalism/properties route.
- **[[Thinkers/Daniel Dennett]]**: more aggressive deflation ([[Concepts/Qualia Disqualified (Dennett)]]). Seth names *Consciousness Explained* as a turning point but keeps experience as what is to be explained.
- **[[Thinkers/Jakob Hohwy]]**: declines the hard problem and keeps conceivability open. Neither Chalmers's dualism nor Seth's dissolution.
- **Dehaene**: vitalism analogy plus "the easy problem is the hard one." In-progress ingest; a third dissolve-by-access voice, not yet a primary page.
