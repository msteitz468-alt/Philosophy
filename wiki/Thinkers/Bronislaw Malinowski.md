# Bronislaw Malinowski

> Field anthropologist whose supplement to *The Meaning of Meaning* coins **[[Concepts/Context of Situation and Phatic Communion (Malinowski)|context of situation]]** and **phatic communion**, and treats primitive speech as a *mode of action*, not a mirror of thought.

## Profile

- **In the source**: Ph.D., D.Sc.; Reader in Social Anthropology, London School of Economics. Ogden and Richards thank him in the first-edition preface: his return to England as the book went through the press gave them "many years of reflection as a field-worker in Ethnology on the peculiarly difficult border-lands of linguistics and psychology." The essay is Supplement I, "The Problem of Meaning in Primitive Languages."
- **Material**: Trobriand canoe-boasts, fishing commands, tense/aspect that will not translate (*boge laymayse* ≈ "already moving hither," not "have arrived").
- **In the vault**: first appearance. Not a mere endorser of Ogden–Richards: he *says* his context of situation is their sign-situation, then immediately marks non-identity and adds ethnographic method, action-embedding, and a non-referential social use the triangle does not cover.

## Core Positions (from Supplement I)

- **Utterance has no meaning except in the context of situation.** Word-for-word translation of native social, ritual, and grammatical terms fails because meaning is not contained in the utterance.
- **Language in its primitive function is a mode of action**, not an instrument of reflection. Fishing commands are links in concerted work, not condensed thought.
- **Four speech-types**: speech-in-action; narrative (social/emotive first; referential "subordinate"); ritual/magic (words as handles); **phatic communion** — gossip and greetings whose word-meanings are almost irrelevant; ties are created by the exchange itself.
- **Genetic explanation of word-magic**: infantile and "savage" meaning is pragmatic efficiency (the name summons the mother/object). This "substantiates" Ogden–Richards Ch. II.
- **The triangle is valid for developed speech.** In early stages he *collapses it to the base*: a real connection of sound-reaction and situation. The dotted base of the Ogden–Richards diagram is then a surviving primitive bond, not a fallacy only.

## How This Relates to Prior Thinkers

- **[[Thinkers/C. K. Ogden]] / [[Thinkers/I. A. Richards]]**: agreed on no meaning-entity in the word, on sign-situations, and on Symbolic Relativity. Disagreed in emphasis: ethnographic "context" is behavioural and situational, not only the psychological context of Ch. III.
- **[[Thinkers/Ludwig Wittgenstein]]** (later): [[Concepts/Language-Games (Wittgenstein)|language-games]] and [[Concepts/Forms of Life (Wittgenstein)|forms of life]] will occupy neighboring ground — meaning as use in activity — without citing this essay. Do not identify them; Malinowski is genetic/ethnographic, Wittgenstein therapeutic.
- **[[Thinkers/Locke]]**: Locke's words-as-signs-of-ideas is the mentalist picture Malinowski's "mode of action" refuses for primitive speech.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] Period language ("savage," "primitive") is his. Quote it; do not launder it into later speech-act jargon or treat "primitive" as a completed theory of all non-literate speech.
