# C. S. Peirce

> American logician whose **semeiotic** — sign, object, interpretant — is the most elaborate rival triangle in the vault. This page is a *first dossier*, not a Peirce primary: it is what [[Thinkers/C. K. Ogden]] and [[Thinkers/I. A. Richards]] excerpt in Appendix D of *The Meaning of Meaning*.

## Profile

- **In this source**: "By far the most elaborate and determined attempt" to treat signs; terminology "so formidable" the work was unfinished. Appendix D §6 is almost entirely quotation (*Monist* 1905–06; letters to Lady Welby, 1904–09). A later footnote says the Harvard *Collected Papers* (then in course of publication) "has so far brought to light nothing which necessitates a modification."
- **Already in the vault as a reported source**: [[Thinkers/William James]] credits Peirce's "How to Make Our Ideas Clear" (1878) as the originating principle of [[Concepts/Pragmatic Method (James)|pragmatism]]. That is James's Peirce (conduct as sole significance). This page is Ogden–Richards's Peirce (the sign-trichotomies). They are not yet stitched by a Peirce primary.
- **In the vault**: first dedicated thinker page. Do not treat this as a completed ingest of the *Collected Papers* or of "How to Make Our Ideas Clear."

## Core Positions (as excerpted)

- **A sign** is anything so determined by an Object that it determines an effect upon a person (the **Interpretant**); "upon a person" is "a sop to Cerberus." More strictly: Object determines Sign determines Interpretant, the latter mediately determined by the former.
- **Two objects**: Immediate (as the sign represents it — the "hint" inside the sign) and Dynamical / Dynamoid (the Reality outside that determines the sign).
- **Three interpretants**: Immediate ("ordinarily called the 'meaning'"), Dynamical (actual effect), Final (the full would-be effect).
- **Icon / Index / Symbol** — relations to the Dynamical Object: icon by internal nature (likeness); index by real relation (weathervane, smoke); symbol only in that it *will be so interpreted*.
- **Type / Token / Tone** (and Instance; also legisign / sinsign / qualisign, replica). Twenty printed *the*s are tokens of one type.
- **Rheme / Dicent / Argument** (later Seme / Pheme / Delome). Further trichotomies (Suggestive / Imperative / Indicative; Instinct / Experience / Form) are sketched, not completed. Six trichotomies would give 28 classes, not 729, because they are not independent.
- **Logic** = the conditions for symbols' reference to objects. Aligned, not identified, with Welby's Sense / Meaning / Significance.

## How This Relates to Prior Thinkers

- **[[Thinkers/C. K. Ogden]] / [[Thinkers/I. A. Richards]]**: they *reproduce* this apparatus and do **not** adopt it. Their [[Concepts/Triangle of Reference (Ogden-Richards)|triangle]] has a causal reference, not an interpretant-series. Peirce's Immediate Interpretant is what they would call "meaning" and then refuse as a hypostatization.
- **[[Thinkers/William James]]**: James's Peirce is the 1878 pragmatic maxim. O&R's definition IX of meaning ("practical consequences") is James, not this Appendix. Two different Peirces until a primary is ingested.
- **[[Thinkers/Edmund Husserl]]**: Appendix D's other modern. Husserl hypostatizes ideal *Bedeutungen*; Peirce multiplies trichotomies. O&R leave both.
- **Frege** (no thinker page): *Sinn* / *Bedeutung* is reported in the same appendix via Russell — thinner than this Peirce dossier.
