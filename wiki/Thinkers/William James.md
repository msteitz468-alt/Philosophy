# William James

> American philosopher and psychologist (1842–1910), the leading popularizer and systematizer of **pragmatism** and a founder of modern psychology. Two primaries now in the vault: *The Varieties of Religious Experience* (1902) studies personal religion from documents and ends in **piecemeal supernaturalism**; *Pragmatism* (1907) turns C. S. Peirce's principle into both a method for dissolving idle metaphysical disputes and a "genetic" theory of truth, and champions a pluralistic, "unfinished," meliorist universe. Do not read 1902 as a delayed chapter of 1907.

## Profile

- **Dates/place**: 1842–1910; American (Harvard). Brother of the novelist Henry James. Trained in painting, then medicine; became Harvard's central figure in psychology and philosophy.
- **Key works** (now ingested): *The Varieties of Religious Experience* (Gifford Lectures, Edinburgh 1901–02; Longmans 1902/1905) and *Pragmatism: A New Name for Some Old Ways of Thinking* (1907). *Pragmatism* refers back to *Varieties* as "regarded as making for the reality of God" and to *The Will to Believe* (an essay he says he "unluckily" titled — still uningested). Still not in the vault: *The Principles of Psychology* 1890, *A Pluralistic Universe* 1909, *The Meaning of Truth* 1909, *Essays in Radical Empiricism*.
- **Lineage he claims**: dedicates the book to **J. S. Mill**; credits **C. S. Peirce** with the originating principle ("How to Make Our Ideas Clear," 1878), and **John Dewey** + **F. C. S. Schiller** with the instrumental/humanist theory of truth. Names **Socrates, Aristotle, Locke, Berkeley, Hume** as pragmatism's "forerunners… preluders only."
- **Self-distinction**: pragmatism is "no logical connection" with his separate doctrine of "radical empiricism" — "one may entirely reject it and still be a pragmatist" (Preface).

## Core Positions (from *Pragmatism*, 1907)

- **Pragmatism as mediator**: between the "tender-minded" (rationalist/idealist/religious/monist) and "tough-minded" (empiricist/materialist/sceptical/pluralist) temperaments — keeping "the richest intimacy with facts" while staying open to religious hypotheses. See [[Concepts/Tender-Minded and Tough-Minded (James)]].
- **The pragmatic method**: settle disputes by tracing each alternative's *practical consequences*; "no difference anywhere that doesn't make a difference elsewhere." Theories are "instruments, not answers… in which we can rest"; their meaning is their "cash-value" in experience. See [[Concepts/Pragmatic Method (James)]].
- **The pragmatic theory of truth**: "Truth *happens* to an idea. It *becomes* true, is *made* true by events" — by verification. "The 'true'… is only the expedient in the way of our thinking." Truth lives "on a credit system." Truth is *in rebus* (a name for verification-processes), like health/wealth/strength — not a static essence *ante rem*. See [[Concepts/Pragmatic Theory of Truth (James)]].
- **The one and the many**: the deepest "classification with the maximum number of consequences." Oneness and manyness are "absolutely coordinate"; pragmatism sides with **pluralism** and may replace the "Absolute" with the "Ultimate." See [[Concepts/The One and the Many (James)]].
- **Common sense as inherited hypotheses**: the common-sense categories (thing, kind, cause, one time/space, etc.) are not eternal intuitions but "extraordinarily successful hypotheses" frozen into language; three stages of thought (common sense / science / critical philosophy), none "absolutely more true."
- **Humanism**: "to an unascertainable extent our truths are man-made products"; "the world stands really malleable… Man engenders truths upon it." We "carve the statue" from the marble of sensation. See [[Concepts/Humanism - Reality as Malleable (Schiller and James)]].
- **Meliorism + pluralistic theism**: salvation as a *possibility* whose probability grows with the actual conditions (incl. our acts); God as "*primus inter pares*," one helper in a still-growing universe. See [[Concepts/Meliorism (James)]].

## Relations to other vault thinkers

- **[[Thinkers/Hume]]** (and Locke, Berkeley): James places himself in the empiricist line and praises their pragmatic moves (e.g., Berkeley on matter, Locke on personal identity reduced to consciousness, Hume on the soul as "verifiable cohesions"). He *radicalizes* the naturalist, anti-rationalist temper — but where Hume stops at description of custom and ends in [[Concepts/Mitigated Scepticism (Hume)]], James turns the same materials toward a constructive, world-making, hopeful philosophy. Hume's "Epicurus" critique of the **design argument** is essentially James's ("design is a blank cartridge").
- **[[Thinkers/Nietzsche]]**: striking kinship — truth as perspectival and "man-made," "untruth as a condition of life" ≈ "the true is the expedient," anti-dogmatism, a "yes" to a dangerous/unfinished world. Differences of tone: James is meliorist, democratic, religion-friendly; Nietzsche is aristocratic, anti-Christian, revaluing. *The Gay Science* **§347** now supplies the nearest *primary* contrast with the will to believe: how much faith one needs is a measure of *weakness*; the free spirit *par excellence* would leave all faith and dance near abysses. Do not flatten James into that verdict, or Nietzsche into James's "right to believe." See [[Concepts/Perspectivism (Nietzsche)]], [[Sources/The Gay Science - Nietzsche (Kaufmann 1974)]]. *Human, All Too Human* III is a second contrast, not with the will to believe but with the saint: 1878 chemistry (compound vanity, inner enemy, optical inflation, "never has a religion… contained a truth") vs. James's fruits test. Do not flatten either into the 1887 [[Concepts/Ascetic Ideal (Nietzsche)]]. See [[Sources/Human, All Too Human - Nietzsche (Zimmern, Collection excerpt)]].
- **[[Thinkers/Kant]]**: James rejects categories "fulminated before nature began" in favor of "categories gradually forming themselves in nature's presence" ("the whole chasm between rationalism and empiricism yawns"); treats the "categorical imperative" and "the Truth" as rationalist idols.
- **[[Thinkers/Spinoza]]**, **[[Thinkers/Plato]]**, **[[Thinkers/Plotinus]]** and the **Absolute** (Royce/Bradley/Hegel, Vedanta): the monistic, "edition de luxe, eternally complete" universe James pragmatically resists — granting it a real "moral-holiday" value but denying its necessity. Leibniz's *Théodicée* is his stock example of rationalism's "feeble grasp of reality."
- **[[Thinkers/Aristotle]]/[[Thinkers/Thomas Aquinas]]**: the adequation/correspondence theory of truth and the omnipotent, simple God are exactly what James's "truth happens" and "primus inter pares" theism depart from.

## The Psychologist of Religion (via *Varieties*, 2026-08-12)

*Varieties* is the vault's first James primary that is not a theory of truth. Gifford Lectures on Natural Religion; the planned second course ("Satisfaction through Philosophy") was postponed, so the twenty lectures stay descriptive until Lecture XX and the Postscript "suggest rather than state" his own conclusions. Method: personal documents, extreme cases, **fruits not roots**. Medical materialism (Paul = lesion, Teresa = hysteric) is refused as a value-verdict. Working definition: "the feelings, acts, and experiences of individual men in their solitude, so far as they apprehend themselves to stand in relation to whatever they may consider the divine." Two religious families — [[Concepts/Healthy-Mindedness and the Sick Soul (James)|healthy-mindedness / sick soul]] (Newman's once-born / twice-born) — are *not* the 1907 tender/tough table. [[Concepts/Conversion and the Subliminal Self (James)|Conversion]] unifies a divided self; suddenness is idiosyncrasy; the **subliminal** (Myers) is a door, not a substitute deity. [[Concepts/Mystical States - Four Marks (James)|Mystical states]] have four marks and bind only the mystic. Lecture XVIII already applies Peirce's "principle of *pragmatism*" to God's metaphysical attributes ("destitute of all intelligible significance"). Lecture XX's nucleus: uneasiness + deliverance via a **MORE** continuous with the higher self; hither side = subconscious; farther side = James's [[Concepts/Over-Beliefs and Piecemeal Supernaturalism (James)|over-belief]] that a larger-than-conscious power produces real effects and need not be infinite or unique. See [[Sources/The Varieties of Religious Experience - William James (1902)]].

## The Psychologist James (via Damasio, 2026-08-12)

The vault's James is *Pragmatism*. [[Sources/Descartes' Error - Antonio Damasio (1994)]] is the first source to bring in *The Principles of Psychology* (1890) as a theory of emotion. Damasio: James "seized upon the mechanism essential to the understanding of emotion and feeling" — feeling as readout of a body change, particular stimuli exciting "a specific pattern of body reaction" without evaluation. He then revises James on three counts: no weight to mental evaluation (Othello, Hamlet, Lady Macbeth); no supplementary route to the body-sense (Damasio's as-if loop); no role for emotion in cognition. Primary emotions are "Jamesian"; secondary emotions are the adult overlay. See [[Concepts/Emotions vs Feelings (Damasio)]]. *Principles* itself remains uningested.
