# Isaiah Berlin

> Riga-born Oxford political theorist (1909–1997). The vault now has a **Berlin primary** — *The Crooked Timber of Humanity* (essays 1959–1988; collected 1990; 2nd ed. 2013) — and a **2013 reception** of the 1958 lecture “Two Concepts of Liberty.” The lecture itself, the 1969 Introduction to *Four Essays on Liberty*, and *Liberty* (Hardy 2002) remain uningested.

## Profile

Born 1909 in Riga; witnessed both 1917 revolutions in Petrograd; to England 1921; Corpus Christi and All Souls; Chichele Professor of Social and Political Theory; founding President of Wolfson; President of the British Academy. Died 1997. Banville (foreword) and Hardy (preface) give the life; do not invent beyond them.

**Two source-layers. Keep them distinct.**

1. **Primary (this ingest).** *The Crooked Timber of Humanity: Chapters in the History of Ideas*, ed. Henry Hardy, 2nd ed. Princeton 2013. History-of-ideas method: monism → utopia → terror; value pluralism; Counter-Enlightenment; Romantic will; nationalism as bent twig; Maistre as proto-fascist in temperament. Title from Kant (1784), Berlin’s rendering: “Out of the crooked timber of humanity no straight thing was ever made.”
2. **Reception (already in).** Baum and Nichols (eds.), *Isaiah Berlin and the Politics of Freedom* (2013), quoting Hardy’s *Liberty*: **TC** = 1958 lecture; **IN** = 1969 Introduction. Negative vs positive liberty; liberty ≠ its conditions. Tully vs Crowder. Not a Berlin primary.

Autobiographical turning point (Pursuit): Tolstoy/Russians → Oxford Platonism → Machiavelli, Vico, Herder. Crowder’s biographical claims (Labour jig, social democrat, anti-Thatcher, Zionism) were **reported** in 2013. Bent Twig now supplies the Zionism/Palestine pair from Berlin’s own essay.

## Core Positions (from Crooked Timber)

- **Value pluralism.** Ultimate goods are many, objective, and clash. “Some among the Great Goods cannot live together. That is a conceptual truth.” Discoverers: Machiavelli, Vico, Herder. See [[Concepts/Value Pluralism (Berlin)]].
- **Not relativism.** Relativism is taste (“coffee / champagne”). Pluralism is many ends still “fully men,” imaginatively enterable, inside a **human horizon**. See [[Concepts/Pluralism versus Relativism (Berlin)]].
- **Utopia is incoherent.** The three-legged stool (one true answer; a path; compatibility of all truths) makes a perfect society look possible. It is “not merely unattainable… but conceptually incoherent.” The search for perfection is “a recipe for bloodshed.” Equilibrium, not a final solution. See [[Concepts/Utopianism and the Pursuit of the Ideal (Berlin)]].
- **History of ideas as method.** Ideas in people’s heads become armies. Study visions of life, not only impersonal forces. Versus Lovejoy: unit-ideas vs value-collision. See [[Concepts/Counter-Enlightenment (Berlin)]].
- **Romantic will.** Ends are made, not found (Fichte). One trunk, two branches: man-as-end *and* human-material politics. See [[Concepts/Romantic Will (Berlin)]].
- **Nationalism.** Inflamed national consciousness, usually from wounds. Herderian belonging is “OK”; nationalism is not. Zionism and Palestinian nationalism as **mirror** wounds. See [[Concepts/Bent Twig Nationalism (Berlin)]].
- **Maistre.** Terrifying prophet of violence and throne-and-altar; precursor of totalitarian repression, not Mussolini. See [[Thinkers/Joseph de Maistre]].

Appendix clarifications (do not overwrite the essays): Kant and Herder are not contradictory in the bits he takes (choice vs variety of ends); “non-teleological developmentalist” accepted only as Kocis’s loose label; Wittgenstein’s family resemblance for cultures; “there is not a fixed, and yet there is a common, human nature.”

## Core Positions (from the 2013 reception of Two Concepts)

Keep these as **reception**. They are not established from a Berlin primary in this vault.

- **Two questions, two concepts.** Negative liberty = area of non-interference / open doors. Positive liberty = who governs me? See [[Concepts/Negative and Positive Liberty (Berlin)]].
- **Liberty is not its conditions.** “Everything is what it is: liberty is liberty, not equality…” See [[Concepts/Liberty versus the Conditions of Liberty (Berlin)]].
- **The slide of positive liberty.** Higher self → tribe/church/state → compulsion as liberation. Rousseau’s “forced to be free.” See [[Contradictions/Berlin vs Rousseau - Negative Liberty vs Forced to Be Free]].
- **1969 concessions.** The two questions “cannot be kept wholly distinct.” Welfare-state case can be built from negative liberty. Tully vs Crowder on whether the lecture narrows or expands liberalism — still open.

## How This Relates to Prior Thinkers

- **[[Thinkers/Arthur O. Lovejoy]]:** predecessor in the History of Ideas leg. Lovejoy tracks unit-ideas; Berlin tracks value-collision. This book is the method Outstanding Sources named. Not an application of Lovejoy.
- **[[Thinkers/Kant]]:** epigraph and argument (crooked timber blocks perfection); also the self-legislation the 1958 reception fears; also the opener of Romantic Pandora’s box who slammed it with universal reason. Three uses, not one Kant.
- **[[Thinkers/Plato]]:** ancestor of one-answer politics and Utopia.
- **[[Thinkers/Giambattista Vico]] / [[Thinkers/Johann Gottfried Herder]] / [[Thinkers/Joseph de Maistre]]:** Berlin’s Counter-Enlightenment cast (this book’s readings, not their primaries).
- **[[Thinkers/Jean-Jacques Rousseau]]:** “born free” inverted by Maistre; “forced to be free” inverted in the 1958 reception. Two fronts.
- **[[Thinkers/Hegel]] / [[Thinkers/Karl Marx]]:** historicise conflict, restore a happy ending; miss nationalism as more than false consciousness.
- **[[Thinkers/Nietzsche]] / [[Thinkers/Arthur Schopenhauer]]:** Romantic will lineage (Carlyle, *Kraftmenschen*; blind cosmic will).
- **[[Thinkers/John Stuart Mill]]:** experiments in living; ally against uniformitarian despotism. Not the same as incommensurable Great Goods.
- **[[Thinkers/Bertrand Russell]]:** 1947 review of *A History of Western Philosophy* — Encyclopedist self-portrait, not the ideal historian of thought. See that source page.
- **[[Thinkers/William James]]:** metaphysical pluralism (one and the many) is a cousin.
- **[[Thinkers/Robert Nozick]]:** later libertarian hardening; framework-utopia is not Berlin’s Utopia. Do not identify them.
- **[[Thinkers/Ludwig Wittgenstein]]:** family resemblance used analogically for cultures (Polanowska letter).

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] The 1958 lecture is still outstanding. Do not mark TC/IN ingested. Reception quotations stay on the 2013 pages.
- > [!warning] Tully vs Crowder (narrows vs expands liberalism) is a debate *about the lecture*. Crooked Timber does not close it.
- > [!warning] Conflicts with [[Thinkers/Jean-Jacques Rousseau]] on whether obedience to a collective will can *be* freedom. See the contradiction page.
- > [!warning] Romantic “values are created” vs Berlin’s “world of objective values.” Historical diagnosis vs his own view.
- Does pluralism entail liberalism? Crowder vs Gray — still open.
