# Johann Gottfried Herder

> German critic of Enlightenment uniformitarianism. This page is **Berlin’s reading** in *The Crooked Timber of Humanity*, not an ingest of the *Ideen* or the *Briefe*. Famous champion of the uniqueness and **incommensurability** of national cultures; not, on Berlin’s mature view, a relativist.

## Profile

[[Thinkers/Isaiah Berlin]] treats Herder as the public face of an insight Vico first conceived in method: each society has its own **centre of gravity** (happiness, standards, gods). Walter Scott first *shows* in fiction what Herder preaches. Hamann is the named friend/influence; *Three Critics of the Enlightenment* is a different book, not ingested.

## Core Positions (as Berlin reads them)

- **Cultures are incommensurable.** Each nation/age has its centre of happiness. A garden of many flowers, not a single peak. “The idea of a single, perfect society of all mankind must be internally self-contradictory, because the Valhalla of the Germans is necessarily different from the ideal of future life of the French.” This is “the beginning of the modern attack on the notion of Utopia, Utopia as such.”
- **Einfühlen.** Sympathetic insight into another form of life. Allied to Vico’s *fantasia*.
- **Not a relativist.** Values of other cultures are **objective** facts about human possibilities, not mere tastes. Herder can denounce Alexander. Outlooks we cannot imaginatively enter fall outside the human range. See [[Concepts/Pluralism versus Relativism (Berlin)]].
- **Harmony-hope (essay 3) vs uncombinable peaks (essay 4).** In “Vico and Cultural History” Berlin still notes that Herder hoped dissimilar contributions need not conflict and would enrich a God-given harmony. “Alleged Relativism” treats him as a pluralist of uncombinable objective peaks, with no linear amelioration. Keep both.
- **National belonging, not yet “nationalism.”** Herder’s *Volk* is a source of [[Concepts/Bent Twig Nationalism (Berlin)|later nationalist fire]] and also of Berlin’s benign **national consciousness**. Do not make Herder a fascist or a liberal cosmopolitan.

## How This Relates to Prior Thinkers

- **Voltaire:** target — essentially identical “enlightenment” across ages; peaks-only history.
- **[[Thinkers/Giambattista Vico]]:** method-partner. Herder is the official discoverer of uniqueness; Vico of the concrete method.
- **[[Thinkers/Kant]]:** Kocis (appendix) alleges a contradiction in Berlin between Kantian agency and Herderian cultural variety. Berlin denies it: ability to choose belongs to men as such; cultures differ in what they choose. Do not turn Herder into anti-Kant.
- **[[Thinkers/Hegel]]:** historicises and then *resolves*. Herder (as Berlin wants him) does not restore a happy ending.
- **Fichte:** melts objective truth into will and *Volk* — the Romantic slide, not Herder’s pluralism.
- **[[Thinkers/Isaiah Berlin]]:** “To Herder I owe the notion that men’s” cultures are many (Kocis reply). Primary debt for value-pluralism’s cultural half.

## Contradictions / Open Questions
