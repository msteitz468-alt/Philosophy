# Karl Friston

> Karl Friston, the computational neuroscientist [[Thinkers/Jakob Hohwy]] treats as the principal source of the prediction-error minimization mechanism. This page is Hohwy's Friston, not a primary ingest of Friston's papers.

## Summary

In *The Predictive Mind* Hohwy says Part I "relies on the work of researchers in neuroscience and computational theory, in particular that of Karl Friston and his large group of collaborators" (p. 3). Friston read and commented on every chapter. Hohwy presents the formal framework as Friston's and the philosophical application as his own; he "remain[s] responsible for any shortcomings."

What Hohwy takes from Friston, as named in the book:

- **Hierarchical predictive coding and message passing.** Cortical hierarchies generate sensory data from high-level causes. Friston's slogan, quoted at p. 47: "forward connections are the feedback connections" (Friston 2005: 825).
- **Free energy / surprisal.** Prediction error is a tractable upper bound on surprisal (negative log probability of sensory input relative to a phenotype). Hohwy uses this "obliquely" and prefers the epistemic PEM vocabulary; "fundamentally however there is no difference between these formal frameworks" (p. 4). Named sources: Friston and Stephan 2007; Friston 2010.
- **Attention as precision optimization.** "Attention is just optimization of precisions in hierarchical prediction error minimization" (Friston, cited pp. 194–95).
- **Active inference.** Action as fulfilling (especially proprioceptive) predictions rather than issuing motor commands.

Stanislas Dehaene, quoted by Hohwy at the opening of ch. 12, calls the framework "the first time that we have had a theory of this strength, breadth and depth in cognitive neuroscience… This one falls much closer to a grand theory."

The vault still lacks a Friston primary (Outstanding Sources lists "a review paper, or *The Predictive Mind*" as the Friston/Hohwy hole). This page should be rewritten from Friston when that source is ingested.

## How Friston Relates to Prior Thinkers in the Vault

- **[[Thinkers/Jakob Hohwy]]**: translator into philosophy of mind; keeps the mechanism, adds seclusion, the Bayesian room, and the courtroom of perception.
- **[[Thinkers/Andy Clark]]**: the other main philosophical consumer. *Surfing Uncertainty* names Friston first in the acknowledgements; Appendix 2 is "The Free-Energy Formulation"; active inference, precision, and the "single active inference machine" are quoted from Friston papers throughout Chs 4–5. *The Experience Machine* names him in notes. They share the mechanism and split with Hohwy on where the mind is.
- **[[Thinkers/Anil Seth]]**: third philosophical consumer, now primary (*Being You*). Credits Friston with coining active inference; uses the free-energy principle as the physical grounding of the [[Concepts/Beast Machine Theory (Seth)|beast machine]] ("I predict myself therefore I am"). Treats FEP as "mathematical philosophy," a theory *for* consciousness science, not *of* it. Co-author of Seth & Friston 2016 on interoceptive inference.
- **Helmholtz**: Hohwy's historical root for the hypothesis-testing brain; Friston is the modern formalization.
