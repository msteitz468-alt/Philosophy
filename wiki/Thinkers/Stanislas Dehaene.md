# Stanislas Dehaene

> French cognitive neuroscientist whose *Consciousness and the Brain* (2014) turns Descartes's two tests — open-ended speech, flexible reason — into a laboratory program: consciousness is [[Concepts/Conscious Access (Dehaene)|conscious access]], implemented as [[Concepts/Global Neuronal Workspace (Dehaene)|global neuronal workspace]] broadcasting, indexed by reproducible [[Concepts/Signatures of Consciousness and Ignition (Dehaene)|signatures]] (late P3, ignition, long-distance "brain web").

## Profile

- **In the source**: professor at the Collège de France; research supported by INSERM, CEA, Université Paris Sud, and the European Research Council; based at NeuroSpin (CEA, south of Paris). Earlier books named on the flyleaf: *The Number Sense*, *Reading in the Brain*. As a student in the late 1980s he found the C-word banned in lab meetings.
- **Circle named in the book**: mentor [[Thinkers/Jean-Pierre Changeux]] (not yet a vault thinker page) first proposed in the early 1990s that consciousness was empirically attackable; Laurent Cohen pointed him to neuropsychological cases; Lionel Naccache became the close collaborator on subliminal depth and the 2001 "subjective reports as data" manifesto. Wife Ghislaine Dehaene-Lambertz leads the infant-language work in Ch. 7. Bernard Baars is credited with the first psychological global-workspace theory; Dehaene's contribution is the *neuronal* architecture plus the signatures.
- **In the vault**: the missing primary Outstanding Sources named for the reductive program Chalmers files as explaining *awareness*, not experience, and the ignition Hohwy already borrowed. Lands with [[Thinkers/Daniel Dennett]] and [[Thinkers/Andy Clark]] against a leftover phenomenal residue; more anatomically committed than either — there *is* a late, all-or-none broadcast.

## Core Positions (from *Consciousness and the Brain*, 2014)

- **Consciousness = conscious access.** Vigilance, attention, self, and metacognition are neighboring phenomena. What can be studied is the fact that some attended information becomes reportable. A distinct "phenomenal consciousness" (Block, Chalmers) is "highly misleading and leads down a slippery slope to dualism."
- **Method**: [[Concepts/Minimal Contrast Method (Dehaene)|minimal contrasts]] (masking, rivalry, blink, inattention) plus introspection as *raw data*, not as a theory of how the mind works. The 2001 Naccache–Dehaene line: subjective reports *define* the phenomenon.
- **Unconscious depth is vast.** Binding, meaning, approximate arithmetic, value, sleep statistics, even partial executive control can run without access. Consciousness is not needed for those operations; it is needed for duration, chaining, and sharing.
- **Function**: unconscious processors are parallel statisticians; consciousness *samples* one brief, holds it, and routes it — a biological Turing machine plus, in humans, a social clipboard. See [[Concepts/Functions of Conscious Broadcasting (Dehaene)]].
- **Signatures**: late P3 (~300–500 ms), parietal–prefrontal ignition, late gamma burst, long-distance bidirectional "brain web." Early activity (P1/N1, early gamma, local recurrent loops) is not sufficient. "Consciousness lives in the loops."
- **Theory**: GNW — giant layer II–III pyramids interconnecting prefrontal, parietal, cingulate, and temporal hubs. Ignition is a phase transition. Spontaneous ignitions generate the stream of consciousness; Darwinian selection inside the workspace replaces Descartes's organ metaphor.
- **Clinic**: the same signatures detect residual awareness (Owen imagery; local–global P3b; Massimini TMS–EEG ping). Tests are asymmetric: positive ≈ conscious; negative proves nothing. "Cortico ergo sum" is operational, not a joke.
- **Scope**: infants have a slow workspace by two months; mammals (and probably birds) share access; humans add a compositional "language of thought." Schizophrenia and anti-NMDA encephalitis are candidate workspace diseases.
- **Machines and free will**: no logical bar to *in silico* workspace. Qualia will go the way of vitalism; Chalmers swapped the labels on easy/hard. Free will is autonomous deliberation, not Lucretius/Penrose/Eccles quantum swerve.

## How This Relates to Prior Thinkers

- **[[Thinkers/Descartes]]**: the book is a reply to the [[Concepts/The Two Tests and the Animal-Machine (Descartes)|two tests]]. Descartes is praised as the first synthetic biologist of the body and blamed only for stopping at speech and flexibility. Ch. 7 grants him the composition-of-signs point as still uniquely human.
- **[[Thinkers/Daniel Dennett]]**: "fame in the brain"; reports as data; qualia as prescientific. Unmarked tension: GNW *does* have a finish line (ignition / "conscious perception is complete when they converge"), which Multiple Drafts denies.
- **[[Thinkers/David Chalmers]]**: named in Ch. 7 as the hard-problem theorist. Dehaene's reply is the vault's first primary counter from the NCC/GNW side. See [[Contradictions/Dehaene vs Chalmers - Access Consciousness vs the Hard Problem]].
- **[[Thinkers/Jakob Hohwy]]**: already used ignition as the perceptual→active-inference switch. Dehaene's primary is anatomical and clinical, not PEM.
- **[[Thinkers/Andy Clark]]**: same deflation of the hard problem; Dehaene's "clever sleuth" brain and Fletcher–Frith schizophrenia story are PEM-adjacent without the PEM label.
- **Baars, Changeux, Crick & Koch, Lamme, Tononi**: Baars and Changeux are co-architects; Crick & Koch's 40 Hz is rejected as too strong; Lamme's local recurrence is necessary but not sufficient; Tononi is cited for differentiation, not as IIT.
