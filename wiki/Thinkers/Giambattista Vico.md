# Giambattista Vico

> Neapolitan philosopher of history. This page is **Berlin’s reading** in *The Crooked Timber of Humanity*, not an ingest of the *Scienza nuova*. Berlin calls him “the true father both of the modern concept of culture and of what one might call cultural pluralism.”

## Profile

[[Thinkers/Isaiah Berlin]] meets Vico through R. G. Collingwood, who “pointed” him to the *Scienza nuova*. Michelet rediscovered Vico for the nineteenth century; Marx (letter to Lassalle) saluted his genius on social evolution. Berlin grants that Vico’s etymologies are “fanciful” and details “wrong, even absurd.” The method is what matters.

## Core Positions (as Berlin reads them)

- **What men have made, other men can understand.** Unlike Spengler or Westermarck, Vico does not encapsulate men in windowless epochs.
- ***Fantasia*.** Historical understanding is imaginative insight: “entering into” / “descending to” primitive minds. Later Germans oppose *verstehen* to *wissen*. It is knowledge of what it is to belong to a society, not Caesar’s death-date or how to ride a bicycle. Needed *plus* verification.
- ***Verum / factum*.** Man understands (fully) only what he makes. Critics (unconscious drives, unintended consequences, Providence as Hegel’s “cunning of reason,” Wilamowitz’s “dead drink our blood”) are granted; something still survives: rounded societies vs antiquarian compilation. At p. 91: we can reach *certum*, even *verum*, in some spheres; categories are not hopelessly culture-bound.
- **Homer.** Primitive men are “sublime poets.” Homeric virtues are not Plato’s or Versailles’. **No true progress in the arts.** “Homer cannot coexist with Dante; nor Dante with Galileo.” Loss is permanent. This makes the perfect society **intrinsically incoherent**.
- **One truth of facts.** Vico is not a relativist of knowledge. Fable is poetically not literally true. Ideology / false consciousness waits for Hegel and the early Marx.
- **Not a relativist of values.** See [[Concepts/Pluralism versus Relativism (Berlin)]]. Berlin recants his own earlier contribution to Momigliano’s relativist reading.

## How This Relates to Prior Thinkers

- **[[Thinkers/Plato]]:** Vico’s Homer is the foil to Platonic virtue. Primitive sublimity vs philosophical ethics.
- **[[Thinkers/Spinoza]]:** target — any truth findable at any time.
- **[[Thinkers/Hegel]]:** Providence as cunning of reason is a *criticism* of *verum/factum*, not Vico’s view.
- **[[Thinkers/Karl Marx]]:** receiver (social evolution); also: men make history but not out of whole cloth — a limit on *factum*.
- **[[Thinkers/Johann Gottfried Herder]]:** official champion of uniqueness; Vico first conceives the method in concrete terms.
- **[[Thinkers/Arthur O. Lovejoy]]:** Lovejoy’s unit-ideas are not Vico’s cultures. Berlin uses Vico for **value-collision**, not for tracking plenitude.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] Reception, not primary. Do not mark the *Scienza nuova* ingested.
- > [!warning] Vico is not a German Counter-Enlightenment figure and not a Romantic. Berlin’s grouping is retrospective.
- Complete translation is “in principle impossible” (p. 63) — linguistic variety as a model of self-expression — without collapsing into unintelligibility.
