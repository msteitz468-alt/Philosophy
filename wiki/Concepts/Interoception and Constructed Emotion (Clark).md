# Interoception and Constructed Emotion (Clark)
> The prediction machine's first job is staying alive: hunger, mood, and emotion are anticipatory constructs built from predictions about the body, not passive readouts of present bodily states.

## Summary

Clark extends [[Concepts/Predictive Processing (Clark)]] inward. *Surfing Uncertainty* Ch 7 already runs the predictive twist on James–Lange: felt emotion is multilevel intero/proprio/extero inference, one process, not two-factor. Seth is the load-bearing source for **presence** = successful top-down suppression of interoceptive prediction error (AIC/ACC); depersonalization = pathologically imprecise interoceptive predictions; body ownership = intero–extero PE minimization (cardiac rubber-hand). Pezzulo's "fear in the night": high-precision interoceptive arousal tilts inference toward the thief hypothesis. "This multilayered texture, in which a world of external causes and opportunities for organism-salient action is presented to a creature in a way constantly intermingled with a grip upon its own bodily condition may lie at the very heart of… 'conscious experience'" (7.16).

The 2023 popularization sharpens the survival slogan: "The primary task of all the prediction machinery in our heads is to help us stay alive." The **Dark Room puzzle** is answered in 2016 Ch 8 by creature-defining "expectations" plus cultural novelty-seeking ([[Concepts/Productive Laziness and Cognitive Niches (Clark)]]), and in 2023 by self-fulfilling foraging predictions. Cousin answers, different grain.

**Interoception** is the inward-looking sensory system (heart, gut, viscera, blood sugar, temperature), distinct from exteroception and proprioception. Its prediction errors drive corrective action. The regulatory pillars are **homeostasis** (returning to a set point; term coined by Walter Cannon) and **allostasis** (preemptively *altering* the set points to meet anticipated demands). "Feelings of hunger and tiredness are for the most part signals not of present needs but of predicted, impending challenges."

## Key Claims / Positions

- **Body budgeting** (Lisa Feldman Barrett): the brain anticipatorily tracks and replenishes resources (water, salt, glucose). "Every prediction you make... is always in relation to the activity of your heart and lungs, your metabolism, your immune function." Every emotion-related brain region is a body-budgeting region.
- **Emotion is constructed, not fingerprinted.** Against a naive reading of William James ("our feelings and emotions are... nothing other than perceptions of our own varying physiological responses"), Clark holds there are "no neat, recurrent 'bodily fingerprints' for the different emotional states." Emotion is **interoceptive predictive processing**: bodily arousal integrated with higher-level predictions of its cause (the same racing heart read as fear, excitement, attraction, or a heart attack). Framing therefore alters feeling *itself* — "raw feelings" are already "highly informed guesses" (anterior insular cortex central).
- **Error dynamics** — how well error-minimization is going *relative to expectation* — drive curiosity, mood, and a "Goldilocks zone" of optimal learning (supported by "artificial curiosity" simulations and infant studies).
- **Depression as a disorder of allostasis** / "locked-in brain": inefficient energy regulation plus **cognitive immunization** (high precision on prior negative beliefs robs positive evidence of updating power).
- **Aesthetic chills** ("salience detection hypothesis"): frisson marks the sudden resolution of important uncertainty; art and music are "probability designs" (Karin Kukkonen) — artifacts engineered to interact with predictive brains.

## How This Relates to Prior Thinkers

- **[[Thinkers/Daniel Dennett]]**: constructed emotion parallels Dennett's deflation of qualia into reactive dispositions — feelings are informed guesses, not intrinsic given data.
- **[[Thinkers/Martin Heidegger]]**: Clark's interoceptive attunement is a naturalist cousin of Heideggerian *Befindlichkeit* / mood (see [[Concepts/Disclosedness — Attunement, Understanding, Discourse (Heidegger)]]) — mood as a way the world is disclosed, here recast as body-budgeting prediction.
- **[[Thinkers/Aristotle]]**: contrasts with [[Concepts/Theory of Perception (Aristotle)]] — perception (including of the body) is active construction, not reception of form.
- **[[Thinkers/Jakob Hohwy]]** (2013, earlier): already recodes James–Lange as interoceptive PEM — arousal is ambiguous prediction error; emotion is explaining it away by priors/context; the bridge study is an emotional illusion (pp. 242–44). Clark is richer on Barrett, allostasis, body-budgeting, depression, and aesthetics. Hohwy is earlier on privacy-of-mind and the [[Concepts/Self as Sensory Trajectory (Hohwy)|trajectory-self]]. Dark Room: Hohwy answers with phenotype priors ("we are not dark-room phenotypes"); Clark answers with self-fulfilling foraging predictions.
- **[[Thinkers/Antonio Damasio]]** (1994, now primary): the lesion ancestor this page was waiting for. Feeling as perception of a body landscape; emotion in the loop of reason; body as ground reference. **Disagreement on fingerprints:** Damasio keeps "largely preorganized" profiles for happiness/sadness/anger/fear/disgust (Jamesian), while denying stereotyped replicas; Clark/Barrett deny "neat, recurrent 'bodily fingerprints.'" Same body-first commitment; opposite on whether a characteristic *profile* remains. See [[Concepts/Emotions vs Feelings (Damasio)]], [[Concepts/Somatic Marker Hypothesis (Damasio)]], [[Concepts/Body-Minded Brain and Neural Self (Damasio)]]. Hohwy's cited source for the recoding is already Seth 2012 / Seth, Suzuki et al.
- **[[Thinkers/Anil Seth]]** (*Being You*, 2021, now primary): names **interoceptive inference** (Seth 2013; Seth & Friston 2016) as the mechanism of the [[Concepts/Beast Machine Theory (Seth)|beast machine]]. Emotions and moods are "*control-oriented perceptions which regulate the body's essential variables*" (allostasis). Affect has no objecthood ("Anxiety doesn't have a back") because it is a *controlling* hallucination, not an epistemic one. Barrett constructivism is a close ally; appraisal theory's two-step is rejected. Clark popularizes the same cluster for body-budgeting and psychiatry; Seth claims it as the *root of consciousness and self*. Heartbeat-evoked potentials and cardio-visual ownership are, he admits, only "suggestive."
