# Affirmation and Denial of the Will-to-Live (Schopenhauer)
> Affirmation is procreation. Denial is *nolle*, not annihilation of a substance. Suicide is not that act. The one inborn error is that we exist in order to be happy.

## Summary

The systematic primary is *WWR* Volume 2, Chapters XLV and XLVIII–XLIX. *Parerga* II Chs. XIII–XIV are additional remarks that presuppose this.

**XLV.** Affirmation of the will-to-live is not mere self-preservation but *sexual impulse* (endless generations). “The life of a man, with its endless care, want, and suffering, is to be regarded as the explanation and paraphrase of the act of procreation.” The act of generation is the *punctum saliens* of the world-egg. Only in man does the will reach *reflection* and decide affirmation or denial.

**XLVIII.** Original sin = existence itself / affirmation. *Operari sequitur esse*: works do not justify. Salvation = regeneration, denial. Genuine Christianity = Brahmanism/Buddhism (world-denial, celibacy); OT/Judaism, Islam, Greek paganism = optimism. Philosophy states *sensu proprio* what religions give in myth. Two roads: the elect (knowledge + others’ suffering) and sinners (own suffering, the *δεύτερος πλοῦς*). “We have to hope for our salvation and deliverance rather from what we suffer than from what we do.”

**XLIX.** “There is only one inborn error, and that is the notion that we exist in order to be happy.” Life is arranged to refute it. That sentence is the system-side of [[Concepts/Eudaemonology as Accommodation (Schopenhauer)]].

**Denial** “does not in any way assert the annihilation of a substance, but the mere act of not-willing”: *velle et nolle*. For us, denial is “a passing over into nothing.” Same as Vedic *magnum Sakhepan*, Buddhist Nirvana, Neoplatonic *ἐπέκεινα*.

Greek/Roman paganism vs Christianity = affirmation vs denial of the will-to-live; “Christianity is fundamentally right.” His ethics stands to prior European systems as NT to OT: theirs “mere Judaism (plain despotic theism)”; his, with the denial of the will as “the path to redemption,” is “Christian philosophy proper.” The entire will-to-live is “detestable”; the world’s horrors are “the mere commentary to the affirmation of the will-to-live.” Death proves existence implies guilt.

**Generation is the kernel of the will-to-live** — the “world-knot”: “The will-to-live has affirmed itself anew.” Father gives will (binding); mother intellect (redeeming). Unnatural sexual satisfaction affirms the will while suppressing propagation, “which alone keeps open the possibility of the denial.” Monastery = poverty, chastity, obedience. Suffering is needed for salvation (*δεύτερος πλοῦς*). “A happy life is impossible”; the best is a heroic life whose will is “extinguished in Nirvana.” Animal suffering: “the will-to-live must devour its own flesh because in the phenomenal world absolutely nothing exists besides it, and it is a hungry will.”

**Suicide is not denial.** Only monotheistic/Jewish religions treat it as a crime; Scripture gives no prohibition. Man has an “indisputable right” over his person and life. The **only valid moral reason** (*WWR* I §69) is that suicide “is opposed to the attainment of the highest moral goal since it substitutes for the real salvation… one that is merely apparent.” It is “still a very long way from this aberration to a crime.” That reason is *ascetic* and applies only at a standpoint higher than European moral philosophy. The body is “the phenomenon of the will-to-live”; hence terror at destroying it.

See [[Concepts/The Will (Schopenhauer)]], [[Concepts/Vanity and Suffering of Existence (Schopenhauer)]], [[Concepts/Indestructibility of Our True Nature (Schopenhauer)]].

## Key Claims

- Denial = *nolle*, not annihilation of a substance.
- Affirmation’s kernel is generation. The world-knot is the sexual act.
- Suicide is an apparent exit that blocks the real one.
- Christianity is “fundamentally right” as denial; paganism as affirmation. This is his mapping, not a church history.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Arthur Schopenhauer]]** — *WWR* II is the system; *Parerga* II Chs. XIII–XIV are additional remarks. Volume 1’s fate essay already said “the will’s turning away from life is the ultimate aim of temporal existence” ([[Concepts/Transcendent Fatalism (Schopenhauer)]]).
- **[[Thinkers/Nietzsche]]** — *Twilight*: “negation of the will to life” is “the very instinct of decadence” ([[Concepts/Morality as Anti-Nature (Nietzsche)]]). *Gay Science* §99: followers take denial and skip the intellectual conscience. Do not import that into 1851. This chapter is the *nolle*, not a decadence diagnosis.
- **[[Concepts/Amor Fati (Love of Fate)]]** — **do not weld**. The aim is turning away, not loving necessity.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] Suicide ≠ denial. Do not file Ch. XIII as a proto-Camus or as a crime-pamphlet.
- > [!warning] “Christian philosophy proper” is his self-placement against “plain despotic theism,” not an endorsement of church dogma. Volume 1 already called moral theology a sop.
