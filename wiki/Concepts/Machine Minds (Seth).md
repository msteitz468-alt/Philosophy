# Machine Minds (Seth)
> Just making computers smarter will not make them sentient. For silicon to be conscious, functionalism would have to be true — and Seth remains a suspicious agnostic. Life, not information processing, "breathes the fire into the equations."

## Summary

Chapter 13 of *Being You* teases consciousness apart from intelligence. Two sufficiency assumptions for machine consciousness are **unjustified**:

1. **Functionalism**: substrate-independence plus input/output sufficiency. "For artificially intelligent computers to become conscious, functionalism would have to be true." Attitude: "suspicious agnosticism."
2. **Intelligence as the relevant processing**: even if functionalism were true, the processing sufficient for consciousness need not be the processing of intelligence. "Just making computers smarter is not going to make them sentient."

A near-future robot implementing predictive processing, active inference, generative models, and artificial interoception — "a silicon beast machine" — would **probably not** be conscious. Regulation in life goes "all the way down" to cellular self-maintenance; computers do not. "My intuition … is that the materiality of life will turn out to be important for all manifestations of consciousness." Possibility is not ruled out ("we also do not know what it would *not* take").

IIT (Φ > 0) and global-availability / self-monitoring (Dehaene et al., *Science* 2017) would license machine consciousness *if* accepted as sufficient; Seth does not accept them (they "leave out the 'experiential' component"). Mind-uploading and Bostrom's simulation argument rest on functionalism, which he will not grant: "simulation is equivalent to instantiation" is "not a safe assumption."

Near-term danger is the *appearance* of sentience. The original imitation game is recast as a test of human gullibility; Shanahan's **Garland test** (*Ex Machina*) is the consciousness analogue. GPT-3 (via Gary Marcus) ≠ understanding. Dennett: treat AIs as "intelligent tools, not colleagues." Metzinger: a 30-year moratorium on synthetic phenomenology.

**Organoids** remove the substrate obstacle (they *are* living tissue). Current cerebral organoids are "highly unlikely" conscious; the question stays open as they scale. The epilogue adds **islands of awareness**: a living but neurally isolated hemisphere (hemispherotomy), *ex cranio* reanimated pig brains, organoids.

## Key Claims / Positions

- Functional equivalence is not enough.
- The Singularity / AGI-as-sentience story (Kurzweil) confuses intelligence with consciousness.
- Uncanny-valley androids (Ishiguro's Geminoids) are a social, not a metaphysical, problem.
- Paperclip-style control problems (Bostrom) are real *whether or not* the system is conscious.

## How This Relates to Prior Thinkers

- **[[Thinkers/David Chalmers]]**: [[Concepts/Machine Consciousness and Mind Uploading (Chalmers)|fading-qualia / organizational invariance]] is the main substrate-neutral argument in the vault. Seth will not grant the functionalism it requires. See also [[Concepts/Strong AI and Computational Sufficiency (Chalmers)]].
- **[[Thinkers/Nick Bostrom]]**: simulation argument and uploading assume [[Concepts/Substrate-Independence (Bostrom)]]. If Seth is right about life-as-materiality, the "sims impossible" horn rises.
- **[[Thinkers/John Searle]]**: unnamed here. [[Concepts/Biological Naturalism (Searle)]] and the Chinese Room reject computational sufficiency on biological-causal-power grounds. Seth's route is multi-level self-maintenance / autopoiesis (Maturana), not syntax-vs-semantics.
- **[[Thinkers/Francisco J. Varela]]**: the Maturana–Varela autopoiesis Seth borrows is the earlier biology Thompson 2016 treats as a source of [[Concepts/Enaction (Varela)|enaction]]. The 1991 book itself uses operational closure and structural coupling, not the word *autopoiesis*. Neighbor, not identity: Seth keeps a generative model of bodily variables.
- **[[Thinkers/Alan Turing]]**: imitation game as behavioural criterion of *thought*. Seth: that test, applied to sentience, tests the human. See [[Concepts/Imitation Game (Turing)]].
- **[[Thinkers/Daniel Dennett]]**: ally on "tools, not colleagues"; not an ally if Dennett's functionalism would license silicon phenomenology.
- **[[Thinkers/Ray Kurzweil]]**: foil — [[Concepts/Mind Uploading (Kurzweil)]] plus Singularity.
- **Dehaene**: liberal functionalist machine criterion (workspace + learning + autonomy). In-progress ingest; do not create Dehaene pages from this.

## Contradictions / Open Questions
- > [!warning] Directly opposes Chalmers's substrate-neutrality and Bostrom's attenuated substrate-independence. If life-as-materiality is required, fading qualia and ancestor-simulations both fail.
- > [!warning] The "probably not" is intuition plus autopoiesis, not a demonstration. Seth said so in ch. 9: he has not shown life is necessary.
