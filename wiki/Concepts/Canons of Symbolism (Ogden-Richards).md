# Canons of Symbolism (Ogden-Richards)

> Six regulative postulates of prose communication. Logic is "the science of the systematization of symbols." Breach of the canons is sometimes called philosophy.

## The Six Canons (Ch. V)

1. **Singularity.** "One Symbol stands for one and only one Referent." Apparent two-referent words are two symbols. Structural particles (`the`, `not`) have no specific referents — grammar's job.
2. **Definition.** "Symbols which can be substituted one for another symbolize the same reference." Same *referent* is not enough (`King of England` / `owner of Buckingham Palace`). This is "the guarantor of mathematics."
3. **Expansion.** "The referent of a contracted symbol is the referent of that symbol expanded." Contractions and pseudo-expansions people the universe with spurious entities. Universals and classes are symbolic fictions; "universal 'qualities' arise, phantoms due to the refractive power of the linguistic medium." "No argument about the world is valid if based merely upon the way a symbol system behaves."
4. **Actuality.** A symbol refers to what it is *actually* used to refer to — not to good usage, not to the interpreter, not even to the speaker's intention.
5. **Compatibility.** No complex symbol may contain constituents claiming the same "place."
6. **Individuality.** All possible referents form an order in which each has one place only. The traditional Laws of Thought (identity, contradiction, excluded middle) become a "triad of minor Canons" and then collapse, via Canon II, into VI. "There is no difference between a referent and its place."

A recap at p. 107 lists Expansion before Definition; the numbered I–VI order above is the one they introduce. They explicitly say **six**.

**Mathematics.** Not homogeneous. They quote [[Thinkers/Ludwig Wittgenstein]]: mathematical propositions are "equations, and therefore pseudo-propositions" (*Tractatus* 6.2, 6.24). Alternatives: Rignano (imagined physical experiments); a third school (commands for a mental "thinking machine"). The likely answer is a combination.

## Technique of Definition (Ch. VI)

There is no usable traditional theory (genus/differentia, connotation, name-magic). Four obstacles: we define *words* (substitute symbols), not things; grammar makes definitions look "about things"; all definitions are *ad hoc* to a **universe of discourse**; intensive vs extensive is later shown artificial.

**Starting-points**: pointables or familiar experience. Survival routes: Similarity, Causation, Space, Time. Formal list of ten: Symbolization/naming; Similarity; Spatial; Temporal; Causation physical / psychological / psycho-physical; Object of a mental state; Common complexes; Legal. Deductive sciences need one "proper" definition; aesthetics, ethics, politics do not.

**Three subterfuges**:
- **Phonetic**: like-sounding words treated as analogously expandable (Mill's `desirable` / `visible`).
- **Hypostatic**: contractions reified (`Virtue`, `Liberty`, `Democracy`) — control by Canon III.
- **Utraquistic**: one term used for both object and act (`perception`, `knowledge`; `beauty` is often both hypostatic and utraquistic).

**Dangerous words**: Irritants (emotive, e.g. Appearance/Reality); Degenerates (too many associated referents); Mendicants (Arnold: terms thrown at an ungrasped object); **Nomads** ([[Thinkers/Locke]]: vagrants with no settled ideas).

Moorean indefinable `good` "stands for nothing whatever" and has no symbolic function.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Bertrand Russell]]**: Canon III is the solvent of the World of Pure Being. Theory of Types is filed as an *ad hoc* patch for neglected canons.
- **[[Thinkers/Ludwig Wittgenstein]]**: cited with approval on mathematics as pseudo-propositions; rejected (Appendix A) when the *Tractatus* turns the same instinct into picture-isomorphism.
- **[[Thinkers/Locke]] / [[Concepts/Words as Signs of Ideas and Language (Locke)]]**: Nomads are Locke's abuse-of-words, renamed. Remedies (definition, attention to ideas) are the same family as this technique.
- **[[Thinkers/A.J. Ayer]]**: "definitions in use" (Russell's descriptions as paradigm) is a later, more formal cousin of substitution-along-a-route.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] Canon IV (actual use, not intention) sits in tension with Ch. IX's careful split of meaning as what the user *actually* refers to (XIII), *ought* to refer to (XIV Good Use), and *believes* himself to refer to (XV). The canons govern prose; the sixteen govern the word "meaning."
