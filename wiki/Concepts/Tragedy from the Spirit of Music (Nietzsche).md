# Tragedy from the Spirit of Music (Nietzsche)
> Tragedy is a Dionysian chorus that discharges itself, again and again, in Apollinian images.

## Summary

The 1872 book’s historical claim, as distinct from the duality itself: Attic tragedy is *born from music*, dies when music is driven out, and can be reborn only from music.

**Origin (§§7–10).** Tragedy “arose out of the tragic chorus and was, to begin with, nothing but chorus.” Origins are purely religious; a political reading “may have been suggested by a phrase in Aristotle” and is rejected. Schiller is kept: the chorus is a living wall insulating tragedy from actuality. The **satyr chorus** stands on a fictitious state of nature. The satyr is to cultured man as Dionysian music is to civilization. Civic divisions give way to “unity leading back into the heart of nature.”

Primary dramatic fact: **enchantment**. The reveler sees himself as satyr, *and as satyr sees the god*. That new vision is the Apollinian completion of the Dionysian state. Formula: “Greek tragedy as a Dionysian chorus which again and again discharges itself in Apollinian images.” “Tragedy is an Apollinian embodiment of Dionysian insights and powers.” Earliest tragedy imagines Dionysus and is not yet “drama.” When the god is *shown*, drama begins. Dialogue and scene = Apollinian dream-illusion; chorus = Dionysian music.

Sophoclean heroes are luminous **Apollinian masks** after a look into nature’s horror — that is “Greek cheerfulness.” Oedipus = glory of passivity; Dionysian wisdom is “an unnatural crime.” Aeschylean Prometheus = glory of action; *Moira* above gods and men; Janus face of Dionysian mask + Apollo of justice. Up to Euripides, stage heroes are **masks of Dionysus** (Zagreus: dismemberment = individuation as suffering). Tragic doctrine: individuation is “the source of all suffering”; art hopes to break that spell. “It was the Heraclean power of music… that… endowed myth with a new and profound significance.”

**Death (§§11–15).** See [[Concepts/Socratism and the Theoretical Man (Nietzsche)]]. Euripides kills myth and music; deserting Dionysus, he is deserted by Apollo. Optimistic dialectics whip music out. Tragedy as “music made visible” is annihilated.

**Metaphysics of music (§16).** Schopenhauer endorsed: music “is not a copy of the phenomenon… but an immediate copy of the will itself.” It is the Dionysian art; plastic art is Apollinian. Music can give birth to *myth*, especially *tragic* myth. Tragic joy is joy in the annihilation of the individual: the hero is phenomenon; “the eternal life of the will is not affected.” What tragedy needs is metaphysical comfort, not a *deus ex machina*.

**Opera vs music-drama (§19).** Opera is the naïve art of Alexandrian / Socratic culture: *stilo rappresentativo*, “text-word lords it over counterpoint like master over servant,” idyll of the “good primitive man,” “the birth of the theoretical man, the critical layman, not of the artist.” Opposite of true musical tragedy.

**Hoped German rebirth (§§19–25).** “*German music*… from Bach to Beethoven, from Beethoven to Wagner,” plus German philosophy (Kant, Schopenhauer) as “Dionysian wisdom comprised in concepts.” Luther’s chorale as first call. Goethe/Schiller/Winckelmann did not reach the Hellenic core. *Tristan* III is the test: tragic myth is the noble deception that lets us endure pure music; “music is the real idea of the world, drama is but the reflection of this idea.” In the total effect Dionysus predominates: “Dionysus speaks the language of Apollo; and Apollo, finally the language of Dionysus.” The *aesthetic listener* is reborn with tragedy; the “critic” is the Socratic stand-in. Without myth a culture loses creative power. Close: music and tragic myth are inseparable expressions of a people’s Dionysian capacity; man is “dissonance become man”; sacrifice “in the temple of both deities.”

This is the early Wagnerism *The Case of Wagner* presupposes and recants. Do not close the tension. Do not create a Wagner thinker page. Do not import 1888 *décadence* or theatrocracy into the 1872 hope.

## Key Claims

- Chorus first; drama later; satyr, not the modern shepherd.
- Tragedy = Dionysian chorus discharging Apollinian images.
- Music gives myth its depth; dying myth is systematized into history.
- Music is immediate copy of the Will (Schopenhauer’s word), not of phenomena.
- Opera is theoretical-layman art; music-drama is the opposite.
- German music + Kant/Schopenhauer = hoped rebirth. Wagner is named inside that hope.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Nietzsche]] (1872)** — philological-aesthetic history serving an artist’s metaphysics. *Ecce Homo* later says people heard only a formula for Wagner and missed “Hellenism and Pessimism.” Reception; not this primary.
- **[[Thinkers/Arthur Schopenhauer]]** — §16’s long endorsement of music as immediate copy of the Will. The *use* of that thesis is un-Schopenhauerian: not resignation, but rebirth of tragic myth.
- **[[Thinkers/Aristotle]]** — chorus not political (§7); tragic effect not catharsis and not moral world-order (§24).
- **1888 Nietzsche.** *The Case of Wagner*: Wagner is Cagliostro, *histrio*, Hegel’s heir; *Parsifal* a masterpiece of seduction. *Twilight* Ancients: *Birth of Tragedy* was “my first revaluation”; psychology of the orgiastic as the key to tragedy — *against* Aristotle’s catharsis *and* Schopenhauer’s resignation. The late books keep the anti-catharsis claim and drop the Wagner-as-rebirth claim.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] Early / late Wagner. 1872: Bach–Beethoven–Wagner as German Dionysus; *Tristan* as the test. 1888: Wagner belongs only to the diseases. Both are primaries. Do not reconcile.
- > [!warning] The 1886 “Attempt at a Self-Criticism” is **not in this collection text**. Do not fill *Genealogy* III’s pointer to “the preface to *The Birth of Tragedy*” from this ingest.
