# Hierarchy of Existence, Life, and Intelligence (Dionysius)

> One God under more and less universal names — Existence, Life, Wisdom — whose *titles* rank by breadth (Being first) while *creatures* rank the other way (intelligences include the lower gifts and stand nearer the Good).

## Summary

*Divine Names* V–VII takes up Being, Life, and Wisdom as modes of one Providence, not as stacked godheads. "We do not regard the Good as one thing, the Existent as another, and Life or Wisdom as another; nor do we hold that there are many causes and different Godheads… one God is the universal Source of the emanations" (DN V.2).

**Extent of the titles.** "Good" reaches beings *and* non-beings (and beyond). "The title of 'Existent' extends to all existent things and is beyond them. And the title 'Life' extends to all living things and is beyond them. And the title of 'Wisdom' extends to the whole realm of Intuition, Reason, and Sense-Perception, and is beyond them all" (DN V.1).

**Name-universality vs creature-rank.** Dionysius poses the apparent paradox: "How is it, since Existence transcends Life, and Life transcends Wisdom, that living things are higher than things which merely exist… and intelligences than those which have only reason?" (DN V.3). Answer: as *titles*, the more universal name "transcends" the less — first participated gift is Being; "Very Being is above Very Life, Very Wisdom"; "all creatures that participate must participate first of all in Being Itself" (DN V.5). As *creatures*, the higher rank *includes* the lower gifts in a surpassing mode: intelligences "exist… live… understand and know" beyond lower grades, and so "especially participate" in the Beautiful and Good (DN V.3). Everything that lives exists; everything that knows lives and exists.

God "is not an Attribute of Being, but Being is an Attribute of Him… all attributes may be affirmed at once of Him, and yet He is No Thing" (DN V.8). He "embraces and anticipates all existence" and "doth not even exist, but is the Essence of existence." **Exemplars** are "those laws which, pre-existent in God as an Unity, produce the essences of things: laws which are called… 'Preordinations' or Divine and beneficent Volitions" (DN V.8). All things, ages, time, eternity, souls, angels pre-exist and are maintained in the Pre-Existent.

**Life** (DN VI): Supra-Vital Life vitalizes Very Life and every mode — angels, souls, animals, plants, even demons *qua* living. "When this is taken away… all life fades"; those that turn to It again revive (DN VI.1). Resurrection is "supernatural" versus visible nature, not versus Divine Life.

**Wisdom** (DN VII): God transcends Reason, Intelligence, Wisdom ("foolishness of God"). Angelic knowledge is simple intuition; human souls are discursive; sense is an "echo." "The Mind of God gains not Its knowledge of things from those things; but of Itself and in Itself It possesses, and hath conceived beforehand in a causal manner, the cognizance and the knowledge and the being of them all" (DN VII.2). "God is known in all things and apart from all things… He is All Things in all things and Nothing in any, and is known from all things unto all men, and is not known from any unto any man" (DN VII.3). Highest knowledge is Unknowing: the mind "turning away from all things and then leaving even itself behind" (DN VII.3).

This is the Christian rewriting of the Neoplatonic **Being–Life–Intelligence** triad (Iamblichus, Proclus): not three hypostases but three names of one Cause, plus a participation-scale of creatures.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Iamblichus]] / [[Thinkers/Proclus]]**: Logical Realism made Being prior to Life, Life prior to Intelligence as *ontological* levels. Dionysius keeps the order for *names* and inverts it for *creaturely rank*.
- **[[Thinkers/Plotinus]]**: Intellect contains Being and Life as moments of one hypostasis. Dionysius's "one God… not many causes" (V.2) is the Christian insistence against a ladder of deities.
- **[[Concepts/Great Chain of Being (Lovejoy)]]**: DN IV.2 already gave the ladder (angels → souls → beasts → plants → lifeless). V–VII give the *logic* of the rungs: more universal name, higher gift included.
- **[[Thinkers/Thomas Aquinas]]**: grades of participation in *esse*; God as *ipsum esse*; angels as purely intellectual. The vault's Treatise on Man already assumes this scale.
