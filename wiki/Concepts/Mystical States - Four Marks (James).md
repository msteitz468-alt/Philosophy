# Mystical States - Four Marks (James)

> A psychological classifier, not a metaphysics of union. Two marks suffice to call a state mystical — **ineffability** and **noetic quality**. **Transiency** and **passivity** usually come with them. Such states are authoritative for the person who has them, bind no outsider, and break the exclusive authority of waking rational consciousness.

## Overview

Lectures XVI–XVII of *Varieties* treat mystical states as the "root and centre" of personal religion. James speaks of them "at second hand." The working definition is four marks:

1. **Ineffability** — "it defies expression… cannot be imparted or transferred… more like states of feeling than like states of intellect."
2. **Noetic quality** — "states of insight into depths of truth unplumbed by the discursive intellect… a curious sense of authority for after-time."
3. **Transiency** — "half an hour, or at most an hour or two" (less sharply marked).
4. **Passivity** — "his own will were in abeyance… grasped and held by a superior power" (less sharply marked).

The series runs from "deeper significance" and déjà vu through alcohol and nitrous oxide ("the anaesthetic revelation") to yoga, *dhyāna*, Sufism, and Christian orison. "Our normal waking consciousness… is but one special type." Majority drift: **optimism** + **monism**, "pantheistic… opposite of pessimistic," fittest to twice-born and other-worldly minds. Fruits, not pathology, decide value. There is also **diabolical mysticism**; unanimity is a "privileged case," not a premise.

Three conclusions on authority:

1. Mystical states "have the right to be, absolutely authoritative over the individuals to whom they come."
2. "No authority emanates from them which should make it a duty for those who stand outside… to accept their revelations uncritically."
3. They "break down the authority of the non-mystical or rationalistic consciousness… They open out the possibility of other orders of truth."

Lecture XVIII then ranks **feeling** as religion's deeper source; dogmatic and idealist constructions are **over-beliefs**. Philosophy cannot coerce universal warrant. What it *can* do, if it "abandon[s] metaphysics and deduction for criticism and induction," is become a **science of religions**.

## How James Uses the Tradition (without becoming it)

- **[[Thinkers/Pseudo-Dionysius]]**: "The fountain-head of Christian mysticism." James quotes the negative + *super-*luxuriance ("*super*-lucent, *super*-splendent, *super*-essential") as an *instance* of the union/negation drift. The four marks are not [[Concepts/Divine Darkness and Unknowing (Dionysius)|Divine Darkness]] and not [[Concepts/Cataphatic and Apophatic Theology (Dionysius)|apophasis as method]]. Ineffability is a psychological fact; noetic quality is claimed *knowledge*, not silence.
- **[[Thinkers/Plotinus]] / [[Schools/Neoplatonism]]**: quoted — "Absorbed in God, he makes but one with him, like a centre of a circle coinciding with another centre." Listed with Hinduism, Sufism, Christian mysticism, and Whitmanism as a "recurring note" of union — not as the definition of the four marks. James then *withdraws* classic unanimity (Spanish personalists; dualistic Sāṃkhya; diabolical cases).
- **[[Thinkers/Al-Ghazali]]**: the difference between knowing "in what drunkenness consists" and "*being* drunk effectively"; what remains "could be learned neither by study nor through the ears, but solely by giving one's self up to ecstasy." "This incommunicableness of the transport is the keynote of all mysticism." Lecture XX will reuse the drunkenness contrast against a merely theoretic science of religions.
- **[[Concepts/Beatific Vision - Last End (Aquinas)]]**: not named in XVI–XIX. Do not file James's "cosmic consciousness" (Bucke) as *visio Dei*.

## Contrasts / Lineage

- **Vs. [[Concepts/Faith and Reason - Enthusiasm (Locke)]]**: Locke's enthusiast claims private revelation as public rule. James grants the mystic private authority and *denies* the public duty — a stricter limit than Locke's "reason must be our last judge," because James also denies that waking reason is the only order of truth.
- **With [[Concepts/Conversion and the Subliminal Self (James)]]**: passivity and automatism already appeared as conversion-marks; here they are marks of a distinct region of consciousness, not of conversion as such.
- **Kant** is used against the old proofs and to block the Hegelian leap from *Bewusstsein überhaupt* to a concrete World-Soul. The Ego of Apperception has "no theological implications."

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- The monistic/optimistic *drift* of mystic reports is a statistical tendency James himself undercuts (diabolical mysticism; personalist and dualist counter-cases). Do not treat "mysticism = monism" as his conclusion.
- Authority-for-the-subject + no-duty-for-outsiders leaves a science of religions with documents it cannot coerce into a creed. That is the job [[Concepts/Over-Beliefs and Piecemeal Supernaturalism (James)]] takes up.
