# Compassion and Justice (Schopenhauer)
> The only genuine moral incentive is compassion. Right is the negation of injury. The world has a moral significance, or the mind is perverse.

## Summary

Chapters VIII–IX of *Parerga* II are paralipomena to *WWR* Book IV and the 1841 prize essays (*On the Basis of Morals*, *On the Freedom of the Will* — cited, not this ingest). They are not the eudaemonological accommodation of Volume 1’s Aphorisms.

“That the world has only a physical and not a moral significance is a fundamental error… the real perversity of the mind.” The **moral incentive** is **compassion** (*Mitleid*), not Kantian “dignity of man” (circular: dignity from morality, morality from dignity). Practical rule: do not estimate another’s worth; attend only to sufferings, need, anxiety, pain. “The standpoint of sympathy or compassion is the only one suitable for curbing hatred or contempt.”

The fork of character is **envy vs. sympathy**. Envy thickens the wall You/I; sympathy thins it and can abolish I / not-I. **Malicious joy** (*Schadenfreude*) is the devilish opposite of sympathy; sympathy is “the true source of all genuine righteousness and loving kindness.”

That source is metaphysical: *tat tvam asi* — the same **will-to-live** whole in every being. Disinterested benefit is a *mysterium*: one recognizes oneself in the other and contradicts the phenomenal separateness of individuals. “To support morality by means of theism is equivalent to reducing it to egoism.” See [[Concepts/The Will (Schopenhauer)]], [[Concepts/Affirmation and Denial of the Will-to-Live (Schopenhauer)]].

**Freedom** is only metaphysical: the **intelligible character** is a free act of will; empirical deeds are as necessary as a clock. *Velle non discitur*: precept and example can yield civil/legal improvement, not inner moral change. Responsibility needs **aseity** of the will (already in Volume 1 Fragments §13).

**Wrong** is positive (*laesio*: injury to person, property, honour). **Right** is negative: to do what injures no one. Original rights are equal because the same will-to-live appears at a like stage in each. The **State** is only a protective machine; it exists because of acknowledged human injustice. “In itself right is powerless; by nature might rules. The problem of statesmanship is to associate might with right.” Misery belongs to existence itself, not chiefly to governments. See [[Contradictions/The Highest Good - Pleasure vs Virtue]].

## Key Claims

- Compassion, not duty-form or dignity, is the moral incentive.
- *Tat tvam asi*: one will, many appearances. Theism as moral prop is egoism.
- Character is a free act; deeds are necessary. *Velle non discitur*.
- Right = negation of injury. The state yokes might to right. No political heaven-on-earth.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Arthur Schopenhauer]]** — ethics as seeing through individuation. Eternal justice: *malum poenae* tallies with *malum culpae*. Isolated honesty/kindness/genius are a pledge of a hidden redeeming principle in *Samsāra*, not a worldly highest good.
- **[[Thinkers/Kant]]** — dignity is dismissed as hollow. Do not weld to the categorical imperative.
- **[[Thinkers/Nietzsche]]** — later files *Mitleid* as the *technic* of nihilism ([[Concepts/Pity as Practice of Nihilism (Nietzsche)]]). Do not import that diagnosis into 1851. Here compassion is the only genuine moral source.
- **[[Concepts/Eudaemonology as Accommodation (Schopenhauer)]]** — do not flatten Ch. VIII into the Aphorisms. One is a lower art of living under a false standpoint; this is the moral significance of the world.
