# Inside-Out Perception (Seth)
> Perceptions do not come from the bottom up or the outside in. They come primarily from the top down, or the inside out: a neuronal fantasy reined in by sensory prediction error.

## Summary

Chapter 4 of *Being You* is Seth's Copernican inversion of the "how things seem" picture of perception. The rejected view treats the senses as transparent windows and the visual system as a bottom-up feature detector (Marr's cookbook, hierarchical cortex, deep nets). "What we see, hear, and feel is nothing more than the brain's 'best guess' of the causes of its sensory inputs." Contents of consciousness are "a kind of waking dream — a controlled hallucination — that is both more than and less than whatever the real world really is."

Three ingredients (Seth's own list):

1. Top-down predictions cascade through perceptual hierarchies.
2. Bottom-up signals are *prediction errors*; perception is **prediction-error minimisation**.
3. "Perceptual experience … is determined by the content of the (top-down) predictions, and not by the (bottom-up) sensory signals." "We never experience sensory signals themselves, we only ever experience interpretations of them."

Hallucination is a degree, not a kind: "If perception is controlled hallucination, then — equally — hallucination can be thought of as uncontrolled perception." Colour is the working example of a secondary quality: it tracks inferred invariants ("discount the illuminant"), not spectral facts. "There is no redness-as-such in the world or in the brain." Locke's primary/secondary split is kept (trains still kill you); *appearance* is constructed. Accurate, "veridical" perception "is a chimera." Evolution designed the controlled hallucination for **utility, not accuracy**.

Chapter 5 supplies the Bayesian machinery: prior × likelihood → posterior; beliefs as Gaussians (mean = content, precision = confidence); yesterday's posterior is today's prior. Predictive processing is a theory of *mechanisms*; controlled hallucination is that theory applied to *phenomenology*. Both rest on PEM. PEM *approximates* Bayes ("GoodEnough Bayesianism"). Three components: generative models, perceptual hierarchies, precision-weighting. Attention *is* inferred precision. Active inference (Friston's coinage): action quenches error by changing the data; "actions *are* perceptions" — self-fulfilling proprioceptive predictions.

## Key Claims / Positions

- "Top-down predictions do not merely bias our perception. They *are* what we perceive."
- "We don't perceive the world as it is, we perceive it as it is useful for us to do so."
- Objecthood is richness of counterfactual/sensorimotor predictions (Seth 2014; O'Regan and Noë). Time has no dedicated sensor. "Reality" itself is inferred (substitutional reality). "Seeming-to-be-real" is what fuels the hard problem.

## How This Relates to Prior Thinkers

- **Helmholtz**: unconscious inference; scientific Kant. "We sense the effects, never the external objects."
- **[[Thinkers/Kant]]**: the noumenon is what inside-out perception is designed *not* to deliver. Experience is structured by the mind's contribution.
- **Richard Gregory**: perception as neural hypothesis-testing.
- **[[Thinkers/Andy Clark]]** / **[[Thinkers/Jakob Hohwy]]** / **[[Thinkers/Karl Friston]]**: the contemporary PEM family. Clark is named as champion of action-oriented PP (*Surfing Uncertainty*). Hohwy's *Predictive Mind* is a history source. See [[Concepts/Predictive Processing (Clark)]], [[Concepts/Prediction Error Minimization (Hohwy)]].
- **[[Thinkers/Locke]]**: primary/secondary qualities reused, not refuted.
- **[[Thinkers/Hume]]**: the mind "gild[s] and stain[s]" the world; we project causality.
- **[[Thinkers/Daniel Dennett]]**: ally on "double transduction" / no figment in the brain.
- **Marr**: foil — the bottom-up computational cookbook.

## Contradictions / Open Questions
- > [!warning] Utility-not-accuracy sharpens the realism question already live in [[Concepts/Controlled Hallucination (Clark)]] and [[Concepts/Secluded Mind and the Sensory Boundary (Hohwy)]]: if veridicality is a chimera, what secures world-contact? Seth's answer is evolutionary usefulness plus error-correction, not Hohwy's seclusion and not Clark's extended mind.
