# Bent Twig Nationalism (Berlin)

> “Nationalism is an inflamed condition of national consciousness,” usually caused by **wounds**. Humiliated peoples lash back “like the bent twig of the poet Schiller’s theory.” Berlin is neither a nationalist nor a simple anti-nationalist.

## Summary

“The Bent Twig: On the Rise of Nationalism” (*Foreign Affairs*, 1972) is [[Thinkers/Isaiah Berlin]]’s primary on nationalism. Nineteenth-century historical consciousness forecast technocracy, capitalist concentration, backward-country revolution, German metaphysical fury — and missed the movement that “dominated much of the nineteenth century.”

**National identity ≠ nationalism.** Consciousness of national identity “may well be as old as social consciousness itself.” Nationalism is not tribal feeling, xenophobia, or mere patriotism. As a **coherent doctrine** it is dated to the last third of the eighteenth century in Germany, especially [[Thinkers/Johann Gottfried Herder]]’s *Volksgeist* / *Nationalgeist*.

Herder treats **belonging** as elemental (like food or communication). He wanted **peaceful coexistence** of diverse national forms. Napoleonic invasion turned the plea for cultural autonomy into “embittered and aggressive nationalist self-assertion.”

> “Nationalism is an inflamed condition of national consciousness, which can be, and has on occasion been, tolerant and peaceful. It usually seems to be caused by wounds, some form of collective humiliation.”

The **bent twig**: humiliated Germans, after provincialism, the Thirty Years War, and French cultural-military dominance, lashed back, inventing a deeper inner *Geist* against “smooth, heartless” France. Hardy’s editorial note: the Schiller attribution is **probably Berlin’s image**, not Schiller’s. The same pattern: backward or patronised societies turn to real or imaginary past glories, or convert backwardness into messianic youth (Slavophils; Poland; Balkans; Asia; Africa).

The 1996 letter to van Cleemput restates the line: **national consciousness** is “OK” (belonging, solidarity, patriotism); **nationalism** is that consciousness inflamed (superiority, chosenness, mission, xenophobia).

## Why Liberals and Marxists Missed It

After mid-century, nationalism was thought to be waning. Hegel opposed “wild, violent, emotional nationalism.” Liberals and early socialists treated it as “immaturity.” Marx/Engels: like religion, a bourgeois, temporary **false consciousness** that evaporates with class war. 1914 socialist war-loyalty and later Fascism/Nazism were systematically underestimated.

After the 1920s, no movement succeeds in Asia/Africa without **anti-imperialism plus pronounced nationalism**. Liberation often follows the bent-twig reaction. “Passionate nationalism appears to be the sine qua non of contemporary revolutions.” It is also a world reaction against nineteenth-century liberal rationalism’s *calculemus* and “engineers of human souls.” The young and the ex-colonial refuse to be counters in utilitarian games. *Fare da se* — “to be one’s own master” — and the *se* today is typically **collective**.

“Freedom is only one value among others, and cannot be realised without rules and limits” — forgotten “in the hour of revolt.” See [[Concepts/Value Pluralism (Berlin)]].

## Zionism and Its Mirror

No minority that keeps cultural, religious, or racial distinctness can “indefinitely tolerate” permanent minority status. “Wounded pride” or “collective injustice” animates “**Zionism or its mirror image, the movement of the Palestinian Arabs**,” and analogous cases (African Americans, Ulster Catholics, Nagas). Not a Zionist programme and not a dismissal — **symmetric injury-politics**.

The 2013 Baum/Nichols reception reported Berlin’s Zionism and later sympathy for Palestinian nationalism from biographies. This essay is the primary those reports were pointing at.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Johann Gottfried Herder]]:** belonging and unique cultures; then hijack into aggressive self-assertion. Gastaldo letter: *Blut und Boden* “does to some degree… descend from Herder” though Herder has no *Blut* and preached tolerance.
- **[[Thinkers/Hegel]]:** *Anerkennung* — hunger for recognition; its lack drives excess. Also historic vs unhistoric nations.
- **[[Thinkers/Karl Marx]]:** nationalism as disguise / false consciousness — the miss.
- **[[Thinkers/Jean-Jacques Rousseau]]:** Poles should cling to clothes and habits — “pathological form of a self-protective resistance.”
- **[[Thinkers/Joseph de Maistre]]:** “integralism” named in the cast; not this essay’s theory.
- **[[Concepts/Negative and Positive Liberty (Berlin)]]:** this essay does **not** use the dual. Adjacent only: “not to be interfered with,” *fare da se*. Do not close Tully vs Crowder on colonial remarks in “Two Concepts.”

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] Berlin condemns genocide and “brutal and destructive” excess while insisting the motive is a “profound and natural need” of “‘the decolonised.’” Pathological *and* natural. Do not pick one.
- > [!warning] This essay does not adjudicate colonial readings of the 1958 lecture. Two Concepts remains outstanding.
- Van Cleemput letter: liberal states need a *central* culture/language to which minorities adjust; partition when friction is intolerable. That is a late hedge, not the 1972 essay’s centre.
