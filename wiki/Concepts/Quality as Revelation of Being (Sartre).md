# Quality as Revelation of Being (Sartre)
> A material quality is not a projected image but a mode in which being lets itself be possessed. The slimy (*visqueux*) is the trap in which the in-itself sucks the for-itself — an Antivalue.

## Summary

The last section of *Being and Nothingness* Part Four, Chapter Two treats qualities (yellow, snow, the slimy) as **modes of being's self-giving**, not as subjective projections (BN 599–603). Tastes are choices of being. This continues the analysis of **possession**: "to possess is to wish to possess the world across a particular object" (BN 597). Knowing itself is appropriative (Barnes's Key names the **Actaeon complex**: knowing as violating-by-sight).

**The slimy (*le visqueux*).** "Slime is the agony of water" (BN 607). Ambiguous liquidity, a trap: the possessed possesses the possessor. "Slime is the revenge of the In-itself" (BN 609). Sartre calls it an **Antivalue** — the ideal reverse of in-itself-for-itself, in-itself's priority over the for-itself (BN 611). Holes and filling are **pre-sexual** ontological structures that *shape* sexuality, not the reverse (BN 611–614).

The Transcendence chapter had already refused to treat quality as a subjective mode: "The yellow of the lemon is not a subjective mode of apprehending the lemon; it is the lemon" (BN 186). Part Four adds that *choosing* a quality is choosing a relation to being — and that some qualities (the slimy) figure the failure of that relation.

## Key Claims / Positions

- Quality reveals being; it is not a noema or a projection.
- Possession is a magical internal bond aiming to found an in-itself that remains other.
- The slimy is Antivalue: the in-itself's revenge on the for-itself.
- Sexuality is shaped by pre-sexual ontological structures (holes, filling, viscosity).

### How Sartre Differs From Prior Thinkers in the Wiki

- **vs [[Thinkers/Edmund Husserl]]**: quality is not a hyletic or noematic layer awaiting an object-pole; "the yellow … is the lemon."
- **vs Freud**: holes/filling are not first sexual symbols that then generalize; they are ontological and *then* sexualized.
