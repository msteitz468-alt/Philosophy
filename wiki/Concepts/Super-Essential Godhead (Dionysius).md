# Super-Essential Godhead (Dionysius)

> The hidden Godhead of [[Thinkers/Pseudo-Dionysius]] is *beyond* being, mind, life, unity, and every name — "Universal Cause of existence while Itself existing not" (DN I.1) — yet the Cause of all that is named.

## Summary

"We must not then dare to speak, or indeed to form any conception, of the hidden super-essential Godhead, except those things that are revealed to us from the Holy Scriptures" (DN I.1). Super-essential understanding of It "is proper to Unknowing, which lieth in the Super-Essence Thereof surpassing Discourse, Intuition and Being." It is "an Unity which is the unifying Source of all unity and a Super-Essential Essence, a Mind beyond the reach of mind and a Word beyond utterance… the Universal Cause of existence while Itself existing not, for It is beyond all Being" (DN I.1).

The Super-Essence is not a part of God alongside the names. The names celebrate **Providence / emanation**, not the Super-Essential Nature (DN V.1). When Scripture says God, Life, Being, Light, Word, "we conceive of nothing else than the powers that stream Therefrom to us" (DN II.7). Being is "an Attribute of Him," not He of Being; "all attributes may be affirmed at once of Him, and yet He is No Thing" (DN V.8). Only "That Which is Super-Essential can be the Principle, the Being and the Cause of all Existences and of Very Existence Itself" (DN XI.6).

*The Mystical Theology* takes the last step: the Cause "possesses all the positive attributes of the universe (being the universal Cause), yet in a stricter sense It does not possess them, since It transcends them all" (MT I). Chapter V denies soul, mind, number, rest and motion, life, "one," "unity," "Godhead or Goodness," existence and non-existence: "nor can any affirmation or negation apply to it… It transcends all affirmation by being the perfect and unique Cause of all things, and transcends all negation by the pre-eminence of Its simple and absolute nature."

Rolt glosses Super-Essence as "Supra-Personality" and as "precisely that which modern philosophy describes as the Absolute," and reads Dionysius as teaching Bradley's "God is but an Appearance of the Absolute." Those equations are Rolt's. Dionysius's own claim is the scriptural-Neoplatonic one: the Cause is beyond the categories it causes.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Plotinus]] / [[Concepts/The One (To Hen)]]**: the One is beyond being and intellect; negative theology from *Parmenides* Argument I. Dionysius Christianizes the same transcendence and adds Scripture as the only permitted speech, plus a Trinity of *differentiated* names that do not interchange (DN II.5).
- **[[Thinkers/Proclus]]**: Unparticipated Monad above participated unities ([[Concepts/The Henads (Proclus)]]). Dionysius's "Unparticipated Creator" who "surpasses… all the Very Qualities" (DN XII.4) is the same pattern in biblical titles.
- **[[Thinkers/Thomas Aquinas]]**: God is *ipsum esse subsistens*, not beyond being but Being itself. He comments *Divine Names* as a favourite authority while refusing to let Super-Essence cancel Trinity and Incarnation (Sparrow-Simpson).
- **Erigena** (via Sparrow-Simpson): Super-essential means we can say only *that* God is, not *what*; strictly *plus quam bonitas*; Deity as *Nihil*. Sparrow-Simpson: that Absolute cannot be worshipped.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] If the Godhead is beyond even "Godhead" and "Goodness" (MT V), what remains of Christian naming? Dionysius's answer is the two-method itinerary ([[Concepts/Cataphatic and Apophatic Theology (Dionysius)]]); critics from Baur to Westcott treat the negative as cancelling the names.
- > [!warning] Rolt's Bradley reading vs Sparrow-Simpson's editorial caveat vs Dionysius's own Scripture-only rule.
