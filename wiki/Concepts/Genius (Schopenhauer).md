# Genius (Schopenhauer)
> Talent hits a target others cannot reach; genius hits a target others cannot even see.

## Summary

Chapters XXIX–XXXII of *WWR* Volume 2 supplement Book III. The intellect in its natural use knows only **relations** (to the will, then of things to one another). When those relations drop, what remains is not the individual but the Platonic **Idea**: the permanent *species rerum*, “the adequate objectivity of the will at this stage.” The Idea is *not* the thing-in-itself; that opens only in self-consciousness as will. There are no Ideas of artifacts, only concepts.

An Idea enters consciousness only by a non-voluntary “self-denial”: knowledge turns wholly from one’s own will. What remains is the “eternal world-eye,” the **pure subject of knowing**. Art eases this more than nature, because a picture or poem already lies outside possible reference to the will.

**Genius** is not talent (speed of discursive thought) but surplus *perceptive* intellect beyond the **service of the will** — *monstra per excessum*. Its object is the universal in the particular. Inspiration is the intellect free of the will, “the clear mirror of the world.” Works of genius are useless (their “patent of nobility”); they exist as end, not means.

**Madness** is the same uncoupling gone the other way. Sound mind = uniformly coherent recollection. Madness = the *broken thread* of that memory: the will will not assimilate an adverse fact; gaps are filled by invention (“the Lethe of unbearable sufferings”). Kinship of genius with madness is “that very separation of the intellect from the will, essential to genius yet contrary to nature” — not a weaker will (genius needs a vehement will) but intellect in excess of any will’s need.

Ch. XXXI also claims: sensibility over irritability; male body; brain from the mother and passionate circulation from the father; “Women can have remarkable talent, but not genius, for they always remain subjective.” Record the claim; do not sanitize; do not promote it to vault fact.

Architecture (XXXV) as fine art has one theme: **support and load**. Its Ideas are the lowest grades of the will’s objectification — gravity, rigidity, cohesion.

## Key Claims

- Idea = adequate objectivity of the will at a grade, still not the in-itself.
- Aesthetic knowing is will withdrawn until we “no longer know that we belong to” the world.
- Genius = surplus perceptive intellect; talent is a different faculty.
- Madness = will breaking the memory-thread; same uncoupling, opposite use.
- Fine art answers “What is life?” in the language of perception; philosophy says the same *explicite* in the concept.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Arthur Schopenhauer]]** — Book III of Volume 1 stated the will-less subject and the hierarchy of arts; these chapters are the psychology of that subject.
- **[[Concepts/Primacy of the Will in Self-Consciousness (Schopenhauer)]]** — intellect is originally only a tool; genius is the rare surplus that can look instead of serve.
- **[[Thinkers/Nietzsche]]** — *Birth of Tragedy* uses the will-less / Idea / music apparatus and already resists the lyric theory. Do not write Apollinian/Dionysian onto 1844. See [[Concepts/Tragedy from the Spirit of Music (Nietzsche)]].

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] The sexed/somatic claims are his. File them as such. *Parerga* II “On Women” is a later rant, not this chapter’s job.
- > [!warning] Do not weld genius to Kant’s *Critique of Judgment* genius (nature giving the rule to art). Schopenhauer’s genius is surplus intellect, not a faculty of aesthetic ideas in Kant’s sense.
