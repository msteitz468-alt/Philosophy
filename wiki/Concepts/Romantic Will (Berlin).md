# Romantic Will (Berlin)

> Ends cease to be buried treasure and become artistic **making**. Fichte is “the true father of Romanticism”: “I believe it because I will.” One trunk, two branches — integrity to the inner light, and the political artist who treats men as “human material.”

## Summary

In “The Apotheosis of the Romantic Will” and “European Unity and Its Vicissitudes” (1959), [[Thinkers/Isaiah Berlin]] treats Romanticism as the revolt against the **myth of an ideal world**: the three-legged monism (one true answer; knowable; answers cannot clash) that underwrites all utopias from Plato to Fourier. See [[Concepts/Utopianism and the Pursuit of the Ideal (Berlin)]].

**Reason unites, will divides.** Until the later eighteenth century, European “unity” meant one intelligible world and public reason. After the Romantic break, unity is replaced by self-determining “personalities” — individual, tribe, culture, nation, Church. There is no universal tribunal; nationalist gods cannot be adjudicated.

The substitution: the sage yields to the Romantic hero (Beethoven). Integrity to the inner light outranks success, harmony, even artistic achievement. Heresy and “false” martyrdom become sublime. “The only principle which must be sacredly observed is that each man shall be true to his own goals, even at the cost of destruction, havoc, death. That is the most fanatical form of the Romantic ideal.”

This is **not** *The Roots of Romanticism* (a different book, not ingested).

## One Trunk, Two Branches

Berlin refuses to flatten Romanticism into fascism.

- **Kept (Romantic humanism).** Man alone makes values, so nothing higher authorizes slaughter. Institutions are for man. Procrustean patterns are the worst sin. Dignity is *choosing* among incompatible goods. After the holocausts, shock reveals “constitutive” human values — empiricist natural law, not an external code. Variety is expected; uniformity is philistinism or slavery.
- **Discarded (aesthetic politics).** If life must resemble a work of art, men become “human material” for Napoleon, the race, or the State. “From this to extreme nationalism and to Fascism is but a short step.”

Romanticism “has permanently shaken the faith in universal, objective truth in matters of conduct, in the possibility of a perfect and harmonious society” — an ideal “for which more human beings have, in our time, sacrificed themselves and others than, perhaps, for any other cause.” Its service is the fatal blow to Condorcet’s chain of “truth, happiness and virtue.” Its “monstrous fallacy” is the aesthetic-politics model. The modest remainder is an unstable equilibrium — not a panacea.

## Cast (as Berlin uses them)

- **[[Thinkers/Kant]]:** ally — autonomy, man as end, free will vs nature-as-turnspit. Unwitting opener of Pandora’s box; he slams the lid with universalizable reason. The *stürmerisch* mood keeps the will and drops the reason.
- **Fichte:** target-origin. The self is a demiurge imposing form on dead nature. “We do not act because we know, but we know because we are called upon to act.” Ends are *made*, not found. The self slides into Group/nation/Church — political anthropomorphism.
- **[[Thinkers/Johann Gottfried Herder]]:** garden of incommensurable cultures; still “cultural, literary, idealistic and humane” in the eighteenth century; later misusable.
- **[[Thinkers/Jean-Jacques Rousseau]]:** independence vs masters/slaves; still identifies nature and reason; eternal truths on the heart.
- **[[Thinkers/Hegel]] / [[Thinkers/Karl Marx]]:** try to re-objectify history but stay infected. Progress by absorbing the defeated (historic nations; the proletariat). Corpses on the road to Paradise.
- **Hamann, Blake:** against Newton; tree of knowledge vs fruit of life.
- **[[Thinkers/Arthur Schopenhauer]]:** world tossed by blind cosmic will — terminus of the line.
- **[[Thinkers/Nietzsche]] / Carlyle:** dramatize the Fichtean demiurge; hero/genius; *Kraftmenschen*.

German revolt, not “feeling vs reason”: wounded pride after French cultural hegemony (especially East Prussia) produces *Sturm und Drang*. That wound is the same pattern as [[Concepts/Bent Twig Nationalism (Berlin)]].

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Arthur O. Lovejoy]]:** the Chain *temporalizes* plenitude (Romantic striving, Schelling’s God coming to be). Berlin’s Romanticism *pluralizes* ends and then *will-izes* them. Light touch: Platonic “One is good, Many is bad” vs modern praise of variety; Herder’s “Greece is not an antechamber to Rome.” Berlin does not name Lovejoy.
- **[[Concepts/Counter-Enlightenment (Berlin)]]:** Romanticism is adjacent. Vico/Herder are the healthy fruit; Fichtean will is the slide; Maistre detested the Romantic spirit.
- **[[Thinkers/Joseph de Maistre]]:** not a Romantic. Classicism, monarchy, Church.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] Do not flatten Romanticism into fascism. Berlin’s structure is one trunk, two branches.
- > [!warning] Romantic “values are created” sits next to Berlin’s own “world of objective values.” He uses the former historically and keeps a human horizon. See [[Concepts/Pluralism versus Relativism (Berlin)]].
- *The Roots of Romanticism* remains uningested.
