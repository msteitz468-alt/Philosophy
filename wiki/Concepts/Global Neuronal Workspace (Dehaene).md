# Global Neuronal Workspace (Dehaene)

> Consciousness is brain-wide information sharing. A coalition of long-distance pyramidal neurons broadcasts one winning representation so it can be held, rerouted, and reported. Dennett's "fame in the brain," with an address in prefrontal–parietal cortex.

## Summary

Ch. 5 states the theory the signatures were waiting for. "According to this theory, consciousness is just brain-wide information sharing." Local specialized processors do most of the work unconsciously. A flexible long-distance router — the workspace — lets selected information "be shared freely across the modular processors." "This global availability of information is precisely what we subjectively experience as a conscious state."

### Architecture

- **Ancestry**: Baars's psychological global workspace (1988/89) minus the homunculus. "The conscious workspace is the clipboard of the mind." Dehaene: "Like the psychologist Bernard Baars, I believe that consciousness reduces to what the workspace does." Changeux, Naccache, and Dehaene supply the neuronal version. Theater, bottleneck, and Posner–Shallice supervision are synthesized; the "audience" is other unconscious processors. Dennett's hygiene is accepted: "One discharges fancy homunculi from the system by organizing armies of idiots to replace them."
- **Cells**: giant layer II–III pyramids with long axons, especially in prefrontal, parietal, cingulate, and temporal hubs, plus reciprocal cortico-thalamic loops. These sites *coincide* with the Ch. 4 ignition map.
- **Code**: a conscious state is stable activation (tenths of a second) of a distributed subset of workspace neurons that converge, via reentry and synchrony, on one differentiated-and-integrated coalition (Hebb cell assembly; Crick & Koch coalition; Edelman reentry; Damasio convergence zones). Silent neurons also encode content. The P3 is the large positivity of the *inhibited* crown; focal negativities carry the content.

### Dynamics

Changeux–Dehaene integrate-and-fire simulations: brief input → dying feed-forward wave (subliminal); longer or stronger → **global ignition**, a physicist's phase transition. A second transition is **vigilance** (brain-stem neuromodulation). The brain is not Descartes's organ, waiting for input. "The nervous system primarily acts as an autonomous device that generates its own thought patterns." Spontaneous ignitions start in workspace areas and propagate downward; they compete with external ignition (inattentional blindness, blink). "A Darwinian process of variation followed by selection occurs within our global workspace system" — thermal synaptic noise as generator of diversity, synapses as fitness.

### Taxonomy of the unconscious (Ch. 5)

| Kind | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Preconscious** | Strong, unattended, recoverable |
| **Subliminal** | Too weak or short to ignite |
| **Disconnected** | Intact processor, no workspace link (brainstem breathing; split-brain) |
| **Diluted** | Code not explicit enough for the workspace to read |
| **Latent synaptic** | Dormant connections (grammar vs arithmetic knowledge unused) |

### Bridging laws

Mind/brain on the model of kinetic theory of gases: nested levels, "subjective states of matter." NCC-without-theory is "as vain as… pineal gland." Current simulations "far from being conscious," but in principle a computer could capture a conscious state. That is the Ch. 7 machine program.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Daniel Dennett]]**: fame in the brain, no homunculus, Joycean serial machine. **Unmarked tension**: Dehaene affirms a "central bottleneck" and a completion event — "Conscious perception is complete when they converge." [[Concepts/Multiple Drafts Model (Dennett)]] denies a finish line. Dennett is used as hygiene, not as rival architecture.
- **[[Thinkers/David Chalmers]]**: files Baars/GNW among reductive programs that explain *awareness* ([[Concepts/Coherence Between Consciousness and Awareness (Chalmers)]]; surveyed in the [[Arguments/Zombie Argument (Chalmers)|zombie]] chapter as failing the hard problem). Dehaene does not name Chalmers in Ch. 5; Ch. 7 answers him.
- **[[Thinkers/Jakob Hohwy]]**: takes the ignition evidence and reinterprets it as the perceptual→active-inference switch ([[Concepts/Perceptual Unity and Ignition (Hohwy)]]). Unity becomes one-body, one-policy. Dehaene's own unity is winner-take-all broadcasting.
- **Lamme**: local recurrent loops necessary, not sufficient. Only long-distance parietal–prefrontal loops count.
- **Tononi**: cited for the differentiation constraint, not as integrated-information theory.
- **Posner & Shallice**: supervision metaphor "cannot be quite right" — subliminal stimuli can already trigger partial executive inhibition.
