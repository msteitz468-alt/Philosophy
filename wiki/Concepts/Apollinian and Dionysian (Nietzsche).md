# Apollinian and Dionysian (Nietzsche)
> Art evolves by a duality, as the species does by the sexes. Their marriage begets Attic tragedy.

## Summary

The opening of *The Birth of Tragedy* (1872; Kaufmann) states the book’s engine: “art owes its continuous evolution to the *Apollinian*- *Dionysian* duality.” Kaufmann’s spelling is **Apollinian**, not the more common Apollonian. The pair is not a mood-palette. It is two art-sponsoring deities, two physiologies, and two relations to the **principium individuationis**.

**Apollo** is dream, plastic form, measure, soothsaying, “fair illusion.” He is “the marvelous divine image of the **principium individuationis**,” the rowboat Schopenhauer described in *The World as Will and Representation* I.1.3: the individual sits tranquilly on a raging sea, trusting that frail craft. His norms are Hellenic moderation: “Know thyself,” “Nothing too much.” Homeric “naïveté” is not Rousseau’s paradise; it is a *victory* of this illusion over Silenus’s wisdom (best never to have been born). The Olympians are an *artificial* Olympus so a suffering people can live. “The gods justified human life by living it themselves—the only satisfactory theodicy ever invented.”

**Dionysus** is intoxication, music, self-forgetting. Essence: the awe plus transport that arises when the principle of individuation *shatters*. The veil of Maya is torn; “the individual forgets himself completely”; the reveler is “no longer the artist, he has himself become a work of art.” Barbarian Dionysia are a “witches’ cauldron” of lust and cruelty. Greece first *rejects* them (Doric Apollo), then *pacifies* them: abrogation of individuation becomes an **aesthetic event**. Apollinian music is a Doric architecture of sound (cithara, plastic rhythm). Dionysian music is tone, melody, harmony — “the heart shaking power of tone.” Apollinian consciousness is “a thin veil hiding from him the whole Dionysian realm.”

These states arise from nature *without* the human artist. The artist is an “imitator”: dream-artist, ecstatic artist, or both (tragedy). The lyric poet (Archilochus beside Homer) is not the “subjective” artist — that figure is “simply the bad artist.” He is first Dionysian, then sees that state in an Apollinian *dream similitude*. The “I” that speaks is the I “in the ground of being.” Folk song is the *perpetuum vestigium* of the union; melody precedes text. Music *appears as* will (Schopenhauer’s word) but does not *represent* the will’s essence.

§4: “Apollo found it impossible to live without Dionysus!” Doric art and the Doric state are a “perpetual military encampment” of Apollinian forces. Four Greek epochs of this battle aim at one child: the dramatic dithyramb and **Attic tragedy**. §§7–10 give the mechanism: tragedy is “a Dionysian chorus which again and again discharges itself in Apollinian images.” §§16–25 add the metaphysical ranking: the Dionysian is “the eternal and original artistic power that first calls the whole world of phenomena into existence”; Apollo then veils that world so individuation can live. Close of the book: sacrifice “in the temple of both deities.”

Do not import 1888 *décadence*, will to power, or *Gay Science* §370’s “Dionysian pessimism” into these 1872 claims. The later books keep the *name* Dionysus and change the ontology.

## Key Claims

- Duality, not a hierarchy of “form good / frenzy bad.” Each drive forces the other to more energetic production.
- Dream / intoxication are the physiological analogues; *principium individuationis* held vs shattered is the metaphysical analogue.
- Olympian culture is built *over* Silenus, not in ignorance of him.
- Genuine art is a triumph over the waking “I”; the “subjective artist” is the bad artist.
- Tragedy is the marriage, not a third independent style.
- The two drives must keep a strict proportion (§25). Where Dionysus now rises, Apollo “must already have descended among us, wrapped in a cloud.”

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Nietzsche]] (1872)** — first book’s master distinction. Term-layer: Kaufmann *Apollinian*.
- **[[Thinkers/Arthur Schopenhauer]]** — scaffolding, not identity. Adopted: Maya, individuation, music as nearer the Will than plastic art (*WWR* I.1.3; music in §16). Rejected already in 1872: lyric as a hybrid of willing + contemplation (*WWR* I.3.51); subject/object as an aesthetic measure.
- **Later Nietzsche (reception, not this book).** *Gay Science* §370 keeps “Dionysian” as *over-fullness* vs romantic impoverishment (Schopenhauer and Wagner are the romantics). *Twilight* Skirmishes: Apollinian vs Dionysian as kinds of *frenzy*; Ancients: “I, the last disciple of the philosopher Dionysus.” Same names; the 1872 Oneness/Maya metaphysics is what those books drop. See [[Concepts/The Great Health (Nietzsche)]], [[Sources/Twilight of the Idols - Nietzsche (Kaufmann)]].
- **[[Thinkers/Martin Heidegger]]** — *Art and Truth* is a different apparatus (work, earth/world). Do not file Heidegger as a reader of this duality unless a later source says so.
- **[[Thinkers/Isaiah Berlin]]** — Romantic will line. The 1872 book *is* a Romantic-Schopenhauerian metaphysics of art. Berlin is not discussing this text.

## The 1888 Self-Review (*Ecce Homo*) — the author on his own first book

*Ecce Homo*'s *Birth of Tragedy* chapter is the vault's best statement of what the late Nietzsche thought survived from 1872, and it is unusually severe on the form while defending the finds.

**On the style.** "It smells offensively of Hegel; only in one or two formulas is it infected with the bitter cadaverous odour peculiar to Schopenhauer." The complaint is that the Apollinian/Dionysian antithesis was "translated into metaphysics" — i.e. the very Schopenhauerian scaffolding this page describes is what he now regards as the book's damage. A better title, he says, would have been "**Hellenism and Pessimism**," and the book's success was a misunderstanding: it was read as a Wagner manifesto and its real value missed.

**On what stands.** Two innovations, both claimed as firsts. (1) The Dionysian itself — "with this I was the first to understand the wonderful Dionysian phenomenon" — as "the single root of all Hellenic art," and tragedy as proof that the Greeks were *not* pessimists, i.e. that Schopenhauer erred about them. (2) **Socrates** "presented for the first time as the instrument of Hellenic dissolution, as a typical decadent" — reason against instinct. See [[Concepts/Socratism and the Theoretical Man (Nietzsche)]], [[Concepts/Decadence (Nietzsche)]].

**On what was silent.** He now says the book is anti-Christian throughout without saying so: Christianity is "neither Apollonian nor Dionysian," negates aesthetic values, and is "profoundly nihilistic." Whether the 1872 text supports that reading is a separate question; the vault should record it as 1888's account of 1872.

**The 1888 definition of the Dionysian**, quoted in that chapter from *Twilight*, is the one that has left the 1872 metaphysics behind: the eternal joy of Becoming itself — affirmation of impermanence, opposition, and war, and the rejection of Being. He signs off as "the first tragic philosopher," and in the *Zarathustra* chapter, "become hard!" is called "the really distinctive sign of a Dionysian nature."

> [!warning] This self-review is evidence *for* the page's existing "Two Dionysuses" warning, not against it. Nietzsche is not saying the 1872 doctrine was right; he is saying the *name* found something and the metaphysics around it was contemporary vocabulary he had not yet shed. Keep the layers. See [[Sources/Ecce Homo - Nietzsche (Collection 2013)]], [[Concepts/Inspiration as Revelation (Nietzsche)]].

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] Two Dionysuses. 1872: mystical un-selving, torn Maya, original Oneness redeemed by illusion. Late: over-fullness, eternal return of *life*, last disciple. Do not weld them.
- > [!warning] §5 demands “objectivity and disinterested contemplation.” *Twilight*: *l’art pour l’art* still moralizes; art is the great stimulus to life. Flag, do not silently update 1872.
- > [!warning] Early Wagnerism (the pair reborn in German music) is argued in §§16–25 of this book. *The Case of Wagner* recants the application. Keep both layers.
