# Productive Laziness and Cognitive Niches (Clark)
> A good prediction machine does as little inner work as possible. It offloads onto body, world, and culture — then rebuilds those environments so the next guess is cheaper.

## Summary

Part III of *Surfing Uncertainty* answers the charge that hierarchical predictive processing is "model-heavy" Cartesianism. Chapter 8: the predictive brain is a **lazy** brain, "vigilant for any opportunity to achieve more by doing less" (8.12). Chapter 9: distinctly human minds arise only when that lazy engine sits inside **designer environments** and a rolling **cognitive niche**.

**Productive laziness** is Aaron Sloman's phrase; Clark traces it to Herbert Simon's satisficing and bounded rationality (8.2). Cheap heuristics and costly deliberation are not rival systems. They are poles of one [[Concepts/Precision-Weighting (Clark)|precision]]-modulated continuum. Model-based and model-free, "system 1 / 2," sit on the same scale. A second strand is Pfeifer and Bongard's **ecological balance**: load-sharing among morphology, materials, control, and environment. Optical Acceleration Cancellation (the outfielder) is sensing-for-coupling, not reconstructive inner trajectory calculation. "Cheap, fast, world-exploiting action, rather than the pursuit of truth, optimality, or deductive inference, is now the key organizing principle" (8.4).

**Extended predictive minds** (8.9): PP "does not materially alter" the 1998/2008 arguments for or against [[Concepts/Extended Mind (Clark and Chalmers)|extended cognition]]. What it supplies is "a specific, and highly 'extension-friendly', proposal concerning the shape of the specifically neural contribution." Inner coalitions and bio-external tools are recruited by the same prediction-error + precision machinery. The 1998 recruitment puzzle — why coupling is effortless — is already a 2016 claim, not only a 2023 slogan.

**Darkened Room Puzzle** (Friston, Thornton, & Clark 2012): if the job is minimizing prediction error, why not sit in a dark room? Clark's 2016 answer is *not* Hohwy's "we are not dark-room phenotypes" as a slogan, though it is cousin to it. Online PEM answers to evolved, structurally implicit creature-defining "expectations" (scare quotes: not lifetime-acquired beliefs). "Relative to the full set of 'expectations' that in this way define an evolved agent, a darkened room usually holds no lure at all" (8.10). A second layer: itinerant dynamics that destroy attractors, Goldilocks complexity, and culturally installed novelty-seeking — "humanity's escape from the darkened room" (8.11).

Chapter 9 scales the same laziness up to culture. After sensory error is quashed, **efficiency** still drives change (complexity reduction, synaptic pruning). Feldman-style "Lord's Prior" (match the world's objective priors) is rejected: agents need action recipes, not exhaustive world-fits. **Designer environments** and "patterned sociocultural practices" (Roepstorff; Sterelny's downstream epistemic engineering) induce a rolling cognitive niche "able to induce the acquisition of generative models whose reach and depth far exceeds their apparent base in simple forms of sensory contact" (9.5). White lines on a cliff road both cue and constitute a low-cost strategy. Words are cheap **artificial contexts** that manipulate precision across the hierarchy (9.8). Humans help **enact** the worlds they model (Varela/Merleau-Ponty cashed in PP, 9.10).

## Key Claims / Positions

- **One continuum, not two systems.** Precision selects how much model, how much world, how much heuristic.
- **The brain is an organ for situated control of action** (8.4), soft-assembling ensembles across brain, body, and world.
- **PP neither proves nor retracts 1998 extension.** It makes inner and outer recruitment the same process.
- **Dark room:** creature-defining expectations + cultural novelty policies, not quietism.
- **Words as precision tools.** Language is not only communication; it is temporary, artificial control of uncertainty at chosen hierarchical levels.
- **Representations kept.** "Why not simply ditch the talk of inner models…?" Because without the generative-model story, only unintelligible looping dynamics remain (9.11). They "aim to engage the world, rather than to depict it in some action-neutral fashion."

## How This Relates to Prior Thinkers

- **[[Thinkers/Hubert Dreyfus]]**: not named in Chs 8–9. Structural ally: "use the world as its own best model," ecological balance, skilled coupling. Clark keeps representations Dreyfus would still distrust.
- **[[Thinkers/Jakob Hohwy]]**: not discussed in these chapters. Hohwy 2013 answers Dark Room with phenotype priors and treats niches as *channel* optimization, not mind-extension. Clark's niches *train generative models* and can enter the PEM loop (8.9).
- **[[Thinkers/Anil Seth]]**: later biologizes the same "why not a dark room?" question as a living body's essential variables. Clark 2016's first answer is evolved form; his second is culture.
- **Varela / Merleau-Ponty**: "PP has the resources to cash all these 'enactivist' cheques" (9.10) — mutual specification of organism and organism-salient world — *without* ditching inner models.
- **Herbert Simon / Aaron Sloman**: named sources of productive laziness.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] Hohwy will grant niches and still keep the mind secluded. Clark's 8.9 line — PP does not settle the extended-mind debate — is more cautious than *The Experience Machine*'s "perfect internal organs." The 2016 claim is *extension-friendly neural machinery*, not a proof. See [[Contradictions/Hohwy vs Clark - Secluded Inference vs Extended Mind]].
