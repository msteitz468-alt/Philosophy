# Pairing and Analogical Appresentation (Husserl)
> How the Other is constituted *in* my monad: a second epoché to the sphere of ownness, then a passive pairing in which that body over there appresents an ego "such as I should be if I were there" — apperception, not an inference from analogy.

## Summary

The Fifth Meditation of *Cartesian Meditations* is the vault's first primary answer to the charge that phenomenology is **transcendental solipsism**. After epoché, "do I not become *solus ipse*?" Others, *by sense*, are not mere unities of verification in me (§42). Husserl refuses a "metaphysical" escape. The Other must be evinced and verified by intentionalities *inside* my transcendental sphere.

The mechanism occupies §§44–54. A **second epoché** screens off every constitutional effect relating to other subjectivity and isolates the **sphere of ownness** (a founding stratum: Nature-in-my-ownness, plus *my* animate organism as the one body I immediately "rule and govern"). Within that sphere the Other cannot be given *originaliter* — else "he himself and I myself would be the same" (§50). What is available is a **mediate intentionality**: **appresentation** motivated by **similarity**, accomplished by **pairing**, verified by **harmonious behavior**.

Smith had paraphrased this as "analogical apperception" and reported that Husserl was never fully satisfied. In this stretch Husserl calls the difficulties "not inconsiderable" and the write-up incomplete for full Objective-world constitution; he does **not** recant. The argument is written as succeeding at the level of *experiencing someone else*. Dissatisfaction as a settled authorial verdict is Smith's, not a sentence of §§42–54.

## The Second Epoché and the Sphere of Ownness

Inside the already-transcendental sphere, abstract from all constitutional effects relating immediately or mediately to other subjectivity (§44). This is not ordinary abstraction: a plague leaving only me would still leave the sense "experienceable by everyone." What remains is:

- a **founding stratum** of "Nature" *in my ownness* — not the scientist's Objective Nature;
- **my animate organism**, uniquely the body I govern immediately;
- the Ego-pole of pure processes.

Paradox: I am a member of this reduced "world" *and* constitute it. Constituted unities that are inseparable from my constitutive life belong to monadic concretion as **immanent transcendency** / **primordial world** (§§46–47). Objective transcendency is constitutionally *secondary* (§48).

Order of constitution (§49): first the **other Ego** (the first non-Ego); then a "universal super-addition of sense" that makes the primordial world the appearance *of* one Objective world for everyone; then an Ego-community of monads. "Harmony of the monads" here is *not* a metaphysical hypothesis.

## Appresentation, Not Inference

The other is "there in person," yet "neither the other Ego himself, nor his subjective processes… becomes given in our experience originally" (§50). Need a kind of making "co-present" — **appresentation**. Thing-appresentation (the unseen back of a die) *can* become presentation by walking around; the other's original sphere **cannot**, a priori.

Motivation: **similarity** of that body over there with mine → **analogizing apprehension** as another animate organism. "It by no means follows that there would be an inference from analogy. Apperception is not inference, not a thinking act" (§50).

## Pairing

**Pairing** is the primal form of the passive associative synthesis already named in [[Concepts/Genetic Phenomenology - Active and Passive Genesis (Husserl)]]. "Ego and alter ego are always and necessarily given in an original 'pairing'" (§51). Prominent similar data overlay; there is **mutual transfer of sense**.

Why is the transferred animate sense not a second organism of *mine*? Because it **can never be actualized originarily** in my primordial sphere. Combined: original presentation of *his* body + appresentation of the inaccessible psychic. Verification only by **new harmonious appresentations** motivated by changing presentations of **behavior**. Discordant behavior → "pseudo-organism" (§52). "Another monad becomes constituted appresentatively in mine."

## Here / There

My organism is **Here**; his body is **There**. I apperceive him as having the appearances **I should have if I were there**, not as a duplicate of my Here-appearances (§§53–54). The appresented ego is "such as I should be if I were there." Higher "empathizing" of anger, cheer, and the rest is motivated by organismal conduct and reciprocally enriches the pairing.

## From Pairing to One Nature

Appresentation works only in "functional community" with presentation (§55). The body over there is not a *sign* of an analogue; it appresents the Other's governing in *that* body and, mediately, in **identically my primordial Nature** ("as if I were standing over there"). One does not first have two Natures and then ask how they are appearances of one Objective Nature; the identity is *produced* by the presentive–appresentive unity.

The **intrinsically first Object** is the Other's animate organism; the other man is constitutionally the first Objective man (§55). Experience of someone else primally institutes a **common time-form**. Really inherently, monads are **absolutely separate**; there is nonetheless an actual **intentional communion** that "makes transcendentally possible the being of a world" (§56).

See [[Concepts/Intersubjectivity and the Life-World (Husserl)]] for the higher levels (social acts, cultural *Umwelt*, uniqueness of the monad-community).

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Descartes]]**: the problem of other minds is not Descartes's problem; he infers bodies and then other souls from God's veracity. Husserl refuses the inference-form twice: no *veracitas* (§40), and the Other is not inferred from analogy (§50).
- **[[Thinkers/Jean-Paul Sartre]]**: Husserl's pairing is a sympathetic, sense-transferring constitution of an analogue-ego. Sartre's [[Concepts/The Look and the Other (Sartre)|Look]] is an objectifying encounter that reveals the Other as subject by *stealing* my world, not by appresenting a "how I should be if I were there." The 1946 lecture's warmer "the other is indispensable to my existence" is closer to *CM* than *Being and Nothingness* is.
- **[[Thinkers/Thomas Nagel]]**: [[Concepts/Objective Phenomenology (Nagel)]] tries to describe a point of view without occupying it. Husserl's pairing *does* occupy — analogically, from Here, as if There. Nagel's bat is exactly what pairing cannot deliver originaliter.
- **[[Thinkers/Edith Stein]]**: (no page yet) deepened empathy in the 1916 dissertation; *CM* 1929 is Husserl's later, monadological restatement.
- **Smith**: reports Husserl "never fully satisfied." In §§42–54 the text claims incompleteness of the *write-up* for Objective-world constitution, not failure of experiencing-someone-else. §62 will call the solipsism objection "groundless" while keeping "everything existing for me must derive its existential sense exclusively from me myself."

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] The Other is never given originaliter or apodictically (§62). Objectivity rests on a style of *harmonious appresentation*, not on a second *ego sum*. Whether that is enough to "dissolve" solipsism, or only to rename it, is the live question Smith already flagged — now with the primary mechanism in view.
- > [!warning] Birth, death, generation, abnormality, animality, and cultural empathy are postponed (§§58, 61). Pairing as written is a theory of the *adult* other already there.
