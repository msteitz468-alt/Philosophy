# Invisible-Hand Explanation of the State (Nozick)
> A dominant protective association, and then a state, can arise from Lockean anarchy by a process no one intends — the same “lovely quality” as the origin of money.

## Summary

Chapter 2 of *Anarchy, State, and Utopia* defines a type of explanation and claims the protective-association story has it. Chapters 5–6 complete the explanation of *the state*.

An **invisible-hand** explanation shows a complicated pattern arising by a process in which no one intends the pattern. (A *hidden-hand* explanation would show someone secretly intending it.) Nozick distinguishes **filtering** processes (some types of unit are eliminated) from **equilibrium** processes (the system settles). He lists examples (money, from Locke §§46–47, 50; Smith’s market; others) and treats the “lovely quality” of such explanations as a reason they are intellectually satisfying.

**The story.** In Locke’s state of nature people may defend themselves and others, recover compensation, and punish for reparation and restraint. The inconveniences — self-judging, overpunishment, feuds with no firm end, weakness versus stronger violators — must be tested against *all voluntary arrangements within rights* before the state is credited as remedy. Mutual-protection associations form; then hired specialists and market agencies. Functions of detection, judgment, punishment, and compensation tend to converge. Inter-agency conflict yields one of three outcomes, each leaving almost everyone in a territory under a common judging-and-enforcing system “very much resembling a minimal state.” The market monopolizes because protection’s worth is relative and maximal services cannot stably coexist. The story assumes agencies act in good faith within Locke’s law; an “outlaw” agency lacks the legitimacy-induced voluntary cooperation of a state.

At the end of Ch. 2 the dominant association is **not yet a state**: it announces no monopoly on unauthorized force, and only paying clients are protected. Those appearances, he says, “are deceptive.”

**Completion (Chs. 5–6).** Everyone has the right to stop unreliable enforcement procedures; only the dominant power can exercise that right against all. It is a **de facto monopoly** — “the unique agent acting across the board” — not a *de jure* exclusive right. Prohibiting independents’ self-help disadvantages them; the principle of compensation requires protective services in return. The dominant agency then satisfies two necessary conditions for being a state (effective monopoly; protection of all). “The *de facto* monopoly grows by an invisible-hand process and *by morally permissible means*, without anyone’s rights being violated.” The invisible hand explains sole *wielding* of power, not unique *rights* — “the state has no special rights.” No one aims at “establishing a state.” It is not a Lockean compact (§95).

The explanation is also a **fundamental potential explanation** of the political in nonpolitical terms (Ch. 1): even if no actual state arose this way, seeing how one *could* have arisen illuminates the realm. Fact-defective, law-defective, and process-defective potential explanations still carry punch.

## How This Relates to Prior Thinkers

- **[[Thinkers/Locke]]**: Locke was wrong that a compact is needed for civil society, as he was wrong that consent was needed to invent money. Independents may stay out (§95). The process is not the Second Treatise’s contract.
- **[[Thinkers/Thomas Hobbes]]**: Hobbes’s sovereign is instituted by covenant and absorbs rights. Nozick’s state is filtered, not instituted, and has no special rights.
- **[[Thinkers/James M. Buchanan]]** / [[Thinkers/Gordon Tullock]]: *The Calculus of Consent* is a *design* story (constitutional stage under a veil of uncertainty). Nozick’s is a *filter* story. Both are individualist; they split on whether the state is chosen or grown.
- **Smith** (quoted, not a vault primary): the market’s unintended order is the type-case.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- The Ch. 2 cliffhanger (“appears to fall short… appearances are deceptive”) is only closed after the principle of compensation. Do not treat the dominant association as already a state.
- The transition to the *minimal* state still leans on people doing what morality requires. An anarchist boycott of the dominant agency is individually dominated.
- Invisible-hand ≠ historically true. Ch. 1 is explicit: the story can be fact-defective and still explain.
