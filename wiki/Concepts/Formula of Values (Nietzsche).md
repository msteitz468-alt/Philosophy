# Formula of Values (Nietzsche)
> What is good? Whatever augments the feeling of power, the will to power, power itself, in man.

## Summary

*The Antichrist* §2 is the opening table of Book I of the Revaluation. Mencken:

> What is good?—Whatever augments the feeling of power, the will to power, power itself, in man.
>
> What is evil?—Whatever springs from weakness.
>
> What is happiness?—The feeling that power increases—that resistance is overcome.

Not contentment, but more power; not peace at any price, but war; not virtue, but efficiency — *virtu*, “virtue free of moral acid.” First principle of “charity”: “The weak and the botched shall perish… And one should help them to it.” “What is more harmful than any vice?—Practical sympathy for the botched and the weak—Christianity….”

§1 supplies the climate and the *happiness* formula already in *Twilight*, Maxims §44: “a Yea, a Nay, a straight line, a goal.” Hyperboreans: beyond the North, beyond the ice, beyond death; modernity’s “Yea and Nay,” lazy peace, and forgiving *largeur* are a sirocco.

§3–4 state the political use of the table. The problem is not what shall replace mankind (“man is an end”) but what *type* of man must be *bred*, *willed*. The more valuable type has appeared often enough “as a happy accident, as an exception, never as deliberately willed” — “even a sort of superman.” Do not collapse this accident into [[Concepts/The Superman (Nietzsche)|Zarathustra’s Superman]]. “Progress” is a modern idea; today’s European falls below the Renaissance.

§6 then gives the life-diagnosis the table presupposes: “Life itself appears to me as an instinct for growth, for survival, for the accumulation of forces, for power: whenever the will to power fails there is disaster.” Highest aspirations now are *décadence*-values.

This is **published** doctrine. It is not the unpublished notebooks’ “degree of power” (n.55), not BGE’s morphology of philosophers, and not *Zarathustra*’s “I am that WHICH MUST EVER SURPASS ITSELF.” Same word, three jobs. See [[Concepts/Will to Power (Nietzsche)]].

## Key Claims

- Good / evil / happiness are defined from power, not from utility, duty, or pity.
- Happiness is increase against resistance, not contentment or peace.
- “Charity” inverted: help the botched to perish. Practical sympathy for them *is* Christianity.
- The higher type is to be willed; it has so far been an accident.
- Life is accumulation of forces; failure of will to power is disaster.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Nietzsche]]** — 1888: the table the rest of *The Antichrist* applies. *Twilight* shares the happiness formula (Maxims §44) but does not reprint §2. *Genealogy* historicizes good/bad vs good/evil; this page *defines* good and evil from the other side of that history.
- **[[Concepts/Will to Power (Nietzsche)]]** — GS §13 is feeling of power (1882); *Zarathustra* names Will to Power as Life’s self-surpassing; BGE §13 / §259 define life as discharge / exploitation. A §2 is the *value* sentence those ontologies had not yet published as a table.
- **[[Concepts/Pity as Practice of Nihilism (Nietzsche)]]** — §7 is the practice that inverts §2.
- **[[Concepts/Master Morality and Slave Morality (Nietzsche)]]** — *Genealogy* I’s pathos of distance is historical-psychological. A §2 is not that history; it is the new “shalt.”
- **[[Thinkers/Kant]]** — A §11: impersonal duty / “good for its own sake” is decay; each must invent his “own categorical imperative.” The table is that invention’s content.
- **[[Thinkers/Spinoza]]** — BGE already dropped self-preservation as cardinal instinct. A §6’s “growth / accumulation / power” is the 1888 restatement, not conatus.
- **[[Thinkers/Ryan Holiday]]** — Maxim 8 of *Twilight* is already flagged as not Marcus. Do not feed A §2 to Stoic endurance.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] Mencken’s **transvaluation** and Kaufmann’s **revaluation** are one German (*Umwerthung*). Do not split them.
- > [!warning] “The weak and the botched shall perish” is a value-formula against Christian pity, not a public-health program and not [[Concepts/Biopolitics and Governmentality (Foucault)]].
- > [!warning] A §3’s “sort of superman” is a lucky stroke in the past. *Zarathustra*’s Superman “never yet hath been.” Do not merge.
