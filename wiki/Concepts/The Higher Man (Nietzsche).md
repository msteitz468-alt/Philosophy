# The Higher Man (Nietzsche)
> They are bridges. They are failures. They are not the Superman. Pity for them is Zarathustra's last sin.

## Summary

Part IV of *Zarathustra* (Common 1909) gathers **higher men** (*höhere Menschen*) in the cave. The soothsayer comes to seduce Zarathustra to his **last sin: pity**. The cry of distress *is* "THE HIGHER MAN." The catch: two kings and an ass, the conscientious in spirit (leech), the magician, the last pope, the ugliest man, the voluntary beggar, the shadow.

They are not the figure of the Prologue. "It is not for YOU that I have waited here in these mountains." "Ye may, verily, all of you be higher men, … but for me—ye are neither high enough, nor strong enough." "Ye are only bridges: may higher ones pass over upon you!" Discourse LXXIII, "The Higher Man": "The Superman, I have at heart; THAT is the first and only thing to me—and NOT man…" "Ye higher men here, have ye not all—been failures?" God's death removes *their* greatest danger; petty people remain "the Superman's greatest danger." They must laugh and dance *beyond themselves*.

**The ugliest man** is named "THE MURDERER OF GOD." Motive: revenge on the witness. "The God who beheld everything, AND ALSO MAN: that God had to die! Man cannot ENDURE it that such a witness should live." The last pope: the old God "suffocated of his all-too-great pity." See [[Concepts/Death of God (Nietzsche)]].

**The ass-festival.** The guests kneel and cense the ass (litany of YE-A, never Nay). The pope: better this God-form than none. The shadow: "The old God liveth again." Zarathustra first rebukes the nursery, then **blesses the festival** as a convalescents' omen. They have learned to laugh; they have not learned not to worship.

**The sign.** A yellow lion and a cloud of doves. "MY CHILDREN ARE NIGH." The lion routs the higher men. Last temptation, named and refused: **"FELLOW-SUFFERING WITH THE HIGHER MEN."** He strives after his **work** and the **great noontide**. The down-going that begins is toward children, not toward these guests.

## Key Claims

- Higher men ≠ [[Concepts/The Superman (Nietzsche)]]. They are remnant-of-God types, convalescents, bridges.
- Higher men ≠ [[Concepts/The Last Man (Nietzsche)]]. They still suffer and still fail *upward*.
- Pity / fellow-suffering is the last temptation of the teacher.
- The ass-festival is a relapse into worship (YE-A) that Zarathustra can bless as convalescence, not as arrival.
- The ugliest man kills God as witness, not as GS §125's civilizational event told to unbelievers.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Nietzsche]]** — Part IV as a *test of the teacher*, not a second doctrine of the Superman. Laughter vs wrath; festival vs new idol; work vs pity.
- **[[Concepts/The Superman (Nietzsche)]]** — only four "Superman" hits in Part IV, all in LXXIII, all as what is *still* desired after these guests.
- Reception that treats "Nietzsche's higher man" as the Übermensch is reading the wrong noun.
