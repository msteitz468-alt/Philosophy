# Soul as Social Structure (Nietzsche)
> After soul-atomism: a mortal soul, a soul of subjective multiplicity — the body as a commonwealth of underwills.

## Summary

*Beyond Good and Evil* §§12 and 19 replace Christian/Cartesian soul-atomism without scrapping the soul-hypothesis.

**§12.** Materialistic atomism is “one of the best-refuted theories” (Boscovich after Copernicus). War must also be declared on **soul-atomism**: the soul as “indestructible, eternal, indivisible… a monad, an atomon.” That belief “ought to be expelled from science.” It is “not at all necessary to get rid of ‘the soul’” — only to open “mortal soul,” “soul of subjective multiplicity,” and “soul as social structure of the instincts and passions” as legitimate scientific hypotheses. The new psychologist is “condemned to invent — and, who knows? perhaps to discover.”

**§16–17.** There are no “immediate certainties.” “I think” (and Schopenhauer’s “I will”) already packs unproven claims. “A thought comes when ‘it’ wishes, and not when ‘I’ wish”; the subject “I” is not the condition of the predicate “think.” Even “one thinks” is “an interpretation of the process.” Grammar (“every activity requires an agency”) is the same habit as atomism’s “earth-residuum.” Descartes is not named; the target is the *cogito*’s immediate certainty.

**§19.** Will is “a unity only in name.” Willing = a plurality of sensations + a ruling thought + the **emotion of the command**. “Freedom of the will” is “the emotion of supremacy in respect to him who must obey.” The same person is commander and obedient; the synthetic “I” hides the duality. “Our body is but a social structure composed of many souls.” Willing belongs in morals as “the doctrine of the relations of supremacy under which the phenomenon of ‘life’ manifests itself.”

**§21.** Metaphysical free will is the wish to be *causa sui*. The contrary, “non-free will,” is “mythology.” “In real life it is only a question of **strong and weak wills**.”

This is the anti-atomist psychology of the “I.” Will to power explains the *economy* (discharge, supremacy); this page holds the *scene* of command and obey. Do not fold it into WzM or into *Genealogy*’s later “subject behind the doing.”

## Key Claims

- Soul-atomism is the Christian afterlife of material atomism; both should go.
- Successor hypotheses: mortal soul, multiplicity, social structure of instincts.
- The “I” of “I think” / “I will” is grammatical fiction, not immediate certainty.
- Freedom of will is the feeling of a commander who has identified with the obedient parts.
- Strong vs weak wills, not free vs unfree.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Nietzsche]]** — BGE I’s psychology; later *Genealogy* “no neutral subject behind the doing” is a moral-historical use of the same suspicion.
- **[[Thinkers/Descartes]]** — §§16–17 attack the *cogito* as immediate certainty and the ego as cause of thought, without naming him.
- **[[Thinkers/Arthur Schopenhauer]]** — §16 “I will” as superstition; §19 will treated as absolutely known, actually a popular prejudice exaggerated.
- **[[Thinkers/Spinoza]]** — §13 (on the Will to Power page): self-preservation as “Spinoza’s inconsistency,” not this page’s target.
- **[[Concepts/Will to Power (Nietzsche)]]** — §13 discharge; §19 command-structure; keep the ontology of the “I” here.
