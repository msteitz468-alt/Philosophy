# Coexistence and the Other (Merleau-Ponty)
> Others are not inferred by analogy. "The other's gaze does not transform me into an object" unless both gazes withdraw into thinking nature. The social world is a permanent field of existence.

## Summary

Part Two, Chapter IV ("Others and the Human World") is the 1945 account of how there are others. **The word *intercorporeity* / *intercorporeité* does not occur in this book.** Merleau-Ponty's terms here are **intersubjectivity**, **coexistence**, **experience-shared-by-many**, **coupling**, and **body-schema correspondence**. (The later name belongs to *The Visible and the Invisible* and should not be back-read.)

"The very first cultural object… is the other's body as the bearer of a behavior." Cultural objects already emit "an atmosphere of humanity." The "easy response" — standing before vestiges of a lost civilization, I imagine *through analogy* the type of man who lived there — is refused: "it would first be necessary to know how I could have the experience of my own cultural world." Instead, "his body schema assures the immediate correspondence of what he sees done and what he does." "Vision [is] the gaze gearing into the visible world, and this is why another's gaze can exist for me." "A vortex forms around the perceived body into which my world is drawn." The child "lives in a world that he believes is immediately accessible to everyone."

This is not Husserl's **pairing** (*Paarung*) of an *alter ego* after a second epoché to the sphere of ownness. "Pairs off" in this book is first the sensing–sensible couple ("My gaze pairs off with the color"). Appresentation is used for the thing's unseen sides. Others are reached by pre-personal perceptual coexistence, not by analogical transfer of my ego to that body over there. See [[Concepts/Pairing and Analogical Appresentation (Husserl)]].

It is also a refusal of Sartre's **Look**. "The other's gaze does not transform me into an object, and my gaze does not transform him into an object, unless both gazes draw us back into the background of our thinking nature." If they are behaviors, "the other's positing of me does not reduce me to the status of an object." See [[Concepts/The Look and the Other (Sartre)]].

Solipsism is not dissolved. Consciousnesses present "the absurdity of a solipsism-shared-by-many." Solitude and communication are two moments. "Transcendental subjectivity is a revealed subjectivity… and as such transcendental subjectivity is an intersubjectivity." The social world is to be rediscovered "not as an object or a sum of objects, but as the permanent field or dimension of existence."

The assigned problem is **transcendence**: "how I can be open to phenomena that transcend me and that, nevertheless, only exist to the extent that I take them up and live them" — how *Urpräsenz* to myself is simultaneously *Entgegenwärtigung*. Sensing already prepared this: every perception "takes place within an atmosphere of generality and is presented to us as anonymous." "*One* perceives in me, and not that I perceive."

## Key Claims / Positions

- Others via body-schema correspondence and shared behavior, not analogy.
- The Look objectifies only when both withdraw from behavior into thinking nature.
- Transcendental subjectivity *is* intersubjectivity (Husserl kept, pairing dropped).
- Solipsism has a permanent truth; it is not overcome "in God."

### How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Maurice Merleau-Ponty]]**: the social is a dimension of being-in-the-world, continuous with sensing and space.
- **[[Thinkers/Edmund Husserl]]**: the Fifth Meditation's question is kept; the mechanism is not. MP attended the 1929 lectures.
- **[[Thinkers/Jean-Paul Sartre]]**: the park's "drain hole" vs MP's "vortex" that *draws my world in* without freezing me as in-itself.
- **[[Thinkers/Martin Heidegger]]**: *Mitsein* is lived as field, not as explicit *Fürsorge*. Freedom "is not distinct from my insertion in the world."
- **[[Thinkers/Thomas Nagel]]**: the other's point of view is not a missing fact to be inferred; it is already a behavior in a shared field. That does not give me the bat's sonar.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- Whether "body-schema correspondence" actually *reaches* the other as for-himself, or only as a privileged cultural object — Husserl's worry, restated.
- The late term intercorporeity should be introduced only when *The Visible and the Invisible* is ingested.
