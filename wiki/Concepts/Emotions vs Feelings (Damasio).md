# Emotions vs Feelings (Damasio)

> Emotion is what the body is set to *do*. Feeling is the mind's view of that doing, laid next to the image that started it. They are not two names for one thing.

## Summary

Ch. 7 of *Descartes' Error* is definitional. "Nature appears to have built the apparatus of rationality not just on top of the apparatus of biological regulation, but also *from* it and *with* it." Emotion and feeling are the bridge.

**Emotion** = "the combination of a *mental evaluative process,* simple or complex, with *dispositional responses to that process,* mostly *toward the body proper,* resulting in an emotional body state, but also *toward the brain itself*" (neurotransmitter nuclei). The essence is "the collection of changes in body state… under the control of a dedicated brain system." Perception of those changes is *left out* of the definition.

**Feeling** is reserved "for the experience of those changes." "If an emotion is a collection of changes in body state connected to particular mental images… *the essence of feeling an emotion is the experience of such changes in juxtaposition to the mental images that initiated the cycle.*" Combination, not blending — plus a change in "style and efficiency" of thought (fast and idea-rich on the pleasant band; slow and repetitive on the painful). "All emotions generate feelings if you are awake and alert, but not all feelings originate in emotions."

**Feelings are cognitive.** "Feelings are just as cognitive as any other perceptual image." They "let us *mind the body*." "Because the brain is the body's captive audience, feelings are winners among equals." This is the Introduction's landscape metaphor: feeling is the view through a window onto a continuously updated image of body structure and state.

## Primary and Secondary

- **Primary** ("early"): innate, preorganized, **Jamesian**. Limbic — amygdala and anterior cingulate. Triggered by features (size, motion, growling, pain) without recognizing the object as such. "Particular stimuli… excite, by means of an innately set and inflexible mechanism, a specific pattern of body reaction."
- **Secondary** ("adult"): after feelings form "systematic connections between categories of objects and situations… and primary emotions." Need prefrontal + somatosensory cortices; "utilize the machinery of Primary Emotions" (ventromedial "piggy-backed" on amygdala). Prefrontal patients lose secondary, keep primary.

## Varieties of Feeling

1. Feelings of the basic universal emotions — happiness, sadness, anger, fear, disgust — "profiles of body state response which are largely preorganized in the James sense."
2. Subtler variations (euphoria, melancholy, panic, *Schadenfreude*).
3. **Background feelings**: the body landscape *between* emotions. "The feeling of life itself, the sense of being." Mood is related but not identical. Anosognosia destroys the *current* body-state update and thus background feeling and self-integrity.

## Body as Theater; As-If Loop

Default loop: mind/brain → body → mind/brain. James is right that "we always use the body as theater for the emotions" — *often*, not always.

**As-if body loop**: the brain "concocts the fainter image of an 'emotional' body state" without reenacting it. Brain-stem neurotransmitter nuclei also bypass the body. As-if feelings "feel different." Body states "are not algorithmically predictable"; the brain "waits for the body to report."

**Fooling the brain** (Ekman): composing a face can produce the matching feeling from a fragment. Make-believe smiles still differ electrically from real ones. Not all of the brain is fooled.

## James–Lange: Endorse the Core, Revise the Rest

"William James seized upon the mechanism essential to the understanding of emotion and feeling." Feeling is a readout of body change.

What James underweighted: mental *evaluation* of the situation (Othello, Hamlet, Lady Macbeth); any supplementary route to the body-sense (the as-if loop); the role of emotion in cognition and behavior. "The body is *always* interposed" in James; Damasio adds a bypass. He rejects the alternative that feelings *always* come from an as-if signal run in parallel with (not caused by) body change.

## Fingerprints?

**Yes for basic/universal emotions as preorganized profiles; no for exact, stereotyped play-out.**

- For: happiness/sadness/anger/fear/disgust "correspond to *profiles of body state response which are largely preorganized*." When the body *conforms to the profiles* we *feel* the corresponding emotion. Secondary feelings are "a *variation on a preorganized body-state profile*."
- Against rigidity: homeostasis is "a continuous succession of profile changes"; "What is played out in the body is constructed anew, moment by moment, and is not an exact replica of anything that happened before." "The body landscape is always new and hardly ever stereotyped."

This is the precise joint with [[Concepts/Interoception and Constructed Emotion (Clark)]]: Clark/Barrett deny "neat, recurrent 'bodily fingerprints'"; Damasio keeps Jamesian *characteristic profiles* for five universals while denying that each episode is a fixed replica.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/William James]]**: the ancestral theory. Vault James is *Pragmatism* only; this page is the psychologist James entering through Damasio.
- **[[Thinkers/Spinoza]]**: not cited. 3def3 is the philosophical twin — affect = affection of the body + idea of that affection. Damasio's emotion/feeling split is that definition pulled apart for neurology.
- **[[Thinkers/Andy Clark]]**: constructed emotion, allostasis, no fingerprints. Same body-first commitment; opposite on whether the five universals have characteristic profiles.
- **[[Thinkers/Jakob Hohwy]]**: James–Lange as interoceptive PEM; arousal is ambiguous prediction error; the bridge study is an emotional illusion. Hohwy's "not a fingerprint" is closer to Clark than to Damasio's profiles.
- **[[Thinkers/Daniel Dennett]]**: feelings as cognitive percepts, not intrinsic given data — a naturalist cousin of qualia-deflation, but Damasio keeps a real body-landscape as content.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] Profiles vs construction: Damasio's five Jamesian profiles vs Clark/Barrett's "no neat bodily fingerprints." Both deny stereotyped replicas; they disagree on whether a characteristic *profile* remains. See [[Concepts/Interoception and Constructed Emotion (Clark)]].
- The emotion/feeling split is terminological discipline, not a metaphysical dualism. Feeling is still a body-perception.
