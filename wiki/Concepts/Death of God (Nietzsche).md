# Death of God (Nietzsche)
> Not an atheist slogan shouted at believers, but a historical event whose shadows still inhabit caves — announced first to *unbelievers*, too early, and still on its way.

## Summary

*The Gay Science* is the vault's primary for the phrase. It does not begin as a marketplace taunt. **§108** ("New struggles") places it after Buddha's cave-shadow: "God is dead; but given the way of men, there may still be caves for thousands of years in which his shadow will be shown. —And we—we still have to vanquish his shadow, too." The task is de-deification, not announcement.

**§109** names the shadows: the world as organism or machine; laws in nature (there are only necessities); purposes, hence accidents; living as more than "a type of what is dead." "The total character of the world… is in all eternity chaos — in the sense not of a lack of necessity but of a lack of order." "When will all these shadows of God cease to darken our minds? When will we complete our de-deification of nature?"

**§124** burns the land: "We have left the land and have embarked… destroyed the land behind us." Homesickness for the old shore mistakes captivity for freedom: "there is no longer any 'land.'"

Only then **§125**, the madman. He lights a lantern in the morning, seeks God in the marketplace, and is *laughed at by those who do not believe*. He tells *them*: "We have killed him — you and I." "God is dead. God remains dead. And we have killed him." The horizon is wiped away; the earth is unchained from its sun; there is no up or down; "Are we not straying as through an infinite nothing?" "Do we not need to light lanterns in the morning?" The deed is "too great for us"; "Must we ourselves not become gods simply to appear worthy of it?" Then: "I have come too early… This tremendous event is still on its way, still wandering; it has not yet reached the ears of men." Churches are "the tombs and sepulchers of God."

The 1887 Book V restates the event for "we fearless ones." **§343**: "the belief in the Christian god has become unbelievable." European morality, grown into that faith, must collapse. The multitude has not received the tidings. For philosophers and free spirits the *first* consequence is not gloom but dawn: "the sea, our sea, lies open again." **§357** supplies the mechanism: what triumphed over the Christian God is *Christian morality / truthfulness* itself. **§344** then refuses simple atheism: "even we… godless anti-metaphysicians still take our fire… from the flame lit by a faith that is thousands of years old… that God is the truth, that truth is divine." The "will to truth" may be "a concealed will to death." See [[Concepts/Ascetic Ideal (Nietzsche)]], [[Concepts/Intellectual Conscience (Nietzsche)]].

## In *Zarathustra*: the saint, pity, the witness, the ass

Do **not** collapse these scenes into GS §125.

- **Prologue §2.** After the forest saint (who still loves *his* God, not men), Zarathustra to his heart: "Could it be possible! This old saint in the forest hath not yet heard of it, that GOD IS DEAD!" The saint is not the madman's marketplace unbelievers. He has not heard. §3 then: once blasphemy against God was the worst; "God died, and therewith also those blasphemers." Remain true to the earth.
- **Part I close.** "DEAD ARE ALL THE GODS: NOW DO WE DESIRE THE SUPERMAN TO LIVE." Backworldsmen: "that God whom I created was human work and human madness."
- **Part II, "The Pitiful."** "God is dead: of his pity for man hath God died." Great love is *above* pity.
- **Part IV.** Last pope: the old God "suffocated of his all-too-great pity." Ugliest man: **"THOU ART THE MURDERER OF GOD!"** Motive: revenge on the *witness* — "The God who beheld everything, AND ALSO MAN: that God had to die! Man cannot ENDURE it that such a witness should live." Ass-festival: the higher men reawaken a YE-A God-form; the pope, "Better to adore God so, in this form, than in no form at all!"; the shadow, "The old God liveth again"; "with Gods DEATH is always just a prejudice." See [[Concepts/The Higher Man (Nietzsche)]].

GS remains the *sequence* (shadows → madman → still pious toward truth). *Zarathustra* supplies the saint who has not heard, the pity-death, the murder of the witness, and the relapse into worship.

## Key Claims

- Sequence is load-bearing: shadows (§108) → de-deified nature (§109) → knowledge as late, weak, and possibly un-incorporable (§§110–121) → no land (§124) → the parable (§125). Kaufmann: isolate §125 and the slogan is born.
- The madman addresses *unbelievers*, not priests. Marketplace atheism is the problem, not the solution.
- The event is done and not yet heard. Earliness, not triumph.
- 1887 aftermath: cheerfulness *and* the collapse of European morality; piety migrates into the unconditional will to truth.
- Eternal recurrence ([[Concepts/Eternal Recurrence (Nietzsche)]], §341) is the later *weight* against this weightlessness; it is not stated in §125.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Nietzsche]]** — first published in GS (1882), dramatized in *Zarathustra* (saint / pity / witness / ass), restated after both in GS Book V and *BGE* (1887). The *Genealogy* (same year as Book V) then asks what the ascetic ideal *means* once this event is underway. *Twilight* adds two late formulas, not a new parable: "I am afraid we are not rid of God because we still have faith in grammar" (Reason §5); "We deny God, we deny the responsibility that originates from God: and thereby we redeem the world" (Four Errors §8). The six-stage True World fable is the *table* of the shadows' withering. See [[Sources/Twilight of the Idols - Nietzsche (Kaufmann)]], [[Concepts/How the True World Became a Fable (Nietzsche)]]. *The Antichrist* is **not** the madman. §§16–19: degeneration of the god-type (national will-to-power gods vs the “good” god; Christian god as nothingness deified). §47: “We deny that God is God”; *deus, qualem Paulus creavit, dei negatio.* A crime against life, not an announcement that the marketplace already believes. See [[Sources/The Antichrist - Nietzsche (Mencken, Collection 2013)]], [[Concepts/Paul as Falsifier of the Evangel (Nietzsche)]].
- **BGE Ch. III (1886) is not the madman.** §53: “the religious instinct is in vigorous growth,—it rejects the theistic satisfaction.” §54: modern philosophy is “anti-Christian… by no means anti-religious.” §55: last rung of religious cruelty is “to sacrifice God himself” and worship nothingness. Do not flatten into GS §125.
- **Johnston *Genealogy* III.24–27** — cites *The Gay Science* V, "The Extent to Which We Also Are Still Devout" (this page's §344), not by number: "even we knowledgeable people of today, we godless and anti-metaphysical people—we still take our fire from that blaze… that God is the truth, that the truth is divine." **III.27:** what triumphed over the Christian God is Christian *truthfulness* become scientific conscience. Unconditional atheism is a last *stage* of the ascetic ideal, not its opposite. The question that then opens: "What is the meaning of all will to truth?" See [[Concepts/Ascetic Ideal (Nietzsche)]], [[Sources/On the Genealogy of Morals - Nietzsche (Johnston)]].
- **Notebooks, Book I** — the same mechanism as a *history of European nihilism*, not as the madman. **n.1.2:** Christianity ends by its own morality; rebound from "God is truth" to "All is false." **n.2:** "the highest values devaluate themselves." **n.18:** "Those who have abandoned God cling that much more firmly to the faith in morality." No marketplace lantern. See [[Concepts/European Nihilism (Nietzsche)]], [[Sources/The Will to Power - Nietzsche (Kaufmann, Collection 2013)]].
- **[[Thinkers/Martin Heidegger]]** — reads the proclamation as the end of the metaphysics of presence and Nietzsche as last metaphysician (being = will to power). The primary text is a parable about shadows, earliness, and lanterns, not a treatise on Being. See [[Thinkers/Nietzsche]] (Heidegger's Critique).
- **[[Concepts/Groundlessness and the Middle Way (Varela)]]** — objectivism's collapse and the nihilist rebound share a grasping root; Nishitani is cited there on the will / eternal return still clinging. GS §124–125 is the Western scene of that collapse.
- **[[Thinkers/William James]]** — not named. Nearest kinship is §347 (how much faith one needs is a measure of weakness) against James's will to believe; do not flatten.
- **[[Thinkers/Arthur Schopenhauer]]** — §151: the "metaphysical need" is not the origin of religion but a late afterimage; §357: first honest German atheist, still answering with Christian-ascetic perspectives.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] "God is dead" is not "there is no God." The madman's audience already thinks that. The claim is that a civilizational *trust* has become unbelievable, that its shadows (purpose, law, organism, truth-as-divine) remain, and that the deed is still arriving.
- > [!warning] Book V's cheerfulness (§343) and the "logic of terror" (collapse of European morality) are both first consequences. Do not pick one.
