# Pathos of Distance and Order of Rank (Nietzsche)
> The social looking-down that first makes possible an inner looking-down — and the innate rank of souls that reverence tests.

## Summary

Chapter IX of *Beyond Good and Evil* (“What is Noble?”) makes two terms technical as a pair.

**Pathos of distance (§257).** Elevation of the type “man” is always the work of an aristocratic society that believes in “gradations of rank” and “requiring slavery.” The social pathos of distance — “such as grows out of the incarnated difference of classes” — makes possible a second, inner pathos: “the longing for an ever new widening of distance within the soul itself… the continued ‘self-surmounting of man.’” Origins are hard: the noble caste begins as barbarian “men of prey,” “more complete men” / “more complete beasts.” A healthy aristocracy is not a function of king or commonwealth but their “significance and highest justification”; society exists as “scaffolding” for a select class (§258).

**Order of rank.** “Unalterable, innate,” tested by the instinct for reverence (§263). Not works but *belief* decides rank; “the noble soul has reverence for itself” (§287). Depth of suffering also ranks souls (§270). Lofty spirituality is “the spiritualizing of justice” authorized to keep gradations of rank (§221). Esoteric/exoteric in Ch. II already used the same geometry: the esoteric class “views things from above downwards” (§30).

This pair is the condition under which master morality can create values. It is **not** Berlin’s bent-twig nationalism and **not** yet *Genealogy*’s history of the slave revolt. *Genealogy* later uses “pathos of distance” as the aristocratic homestead of “good”; BGE states the *structure* first.

## Key Claims

- Social distance is the condition of inner self-surmounting, not a regrettable leftover.
- Rank is innate and tested by reverence, not by achievement-lists.
- “Beyond good and evil” in §260 is how the noble type may act *toward inferiors* — not a third table beyond noble good/bad.
- Exploitation as “primary organic function” (§259) is the physiological counterpart: an anti-exploitation ethics is “Will to the denial of life.”

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Nietzsche]]** — Ch. IX close of BGE; *Genealogy* First Essay uses pathos of distance as etymological/historical engine of “good.”
- **[[Concepts/Master Morality and Slave Morality (Nietzsche)]]** — §260 is the typological pair; this page holds the *condition* (§257) and the *test* (§263, §287).
- **[[Concepts/Will to Power (Nietzsche)]]** — §259: a living body is “incarnated Will to Power”; exploitation is not a primitive vice.
- **[[Thinkers/Isaiah Berlin]]** — *Crooked Timber* groups Nietzsche with Romantic *Kraftmenschen*. This page is not that grouping. Ch. VIII is anti-fatherland “good Europeans,” not bent-twig nationalism.
- **[[Thinkers/Aristotle]]** — natural slavery and gradations of soul are a distant foil, not a source. Do not collapse.
