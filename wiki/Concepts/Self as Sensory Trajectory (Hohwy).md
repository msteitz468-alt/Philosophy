# Self as Sensory Trajectory (Hohwy)
> The self is not a thing found in experience and not a homunculus behind it. It is the agent-bound sensory trajectory a hierarchical model must posit in order to act.

## Summary

Chapter 12 of *The Predictive Mind* extends PEM "deep into matters of the mind" — emotion, introspection, privacy, and self — more speculatively than Parts I–II. Hohwy quotes Dehaene calling the framework "much closer to a grand theory" and treats that scope as license to anticipate.

**Emotion is James–Lange interoceptive inference.** Feeling states arise as responses to bodily arousal (James: we are "afraid because we tremble"). Interoceptive signals are "internally generated and highly ambiguous prediction error"; emotion is explaining them away by priors and context (pp. 242–44). The Dutton and Aron bridge study is an emotional illusion: arousal from a dangerous bridge, disambiguated as attraction. Heightened arousal is PE because we expect a relatively stable heart rate. The percept/concept distinction "washes out" in the hierarchy, so non-linguistic creatures can have emotions. This is Hohwy 2011 / Seth, Suzuki et al. 2012, not yet Clark's later Barrett-style constructionism — see [[Concepts/Interoception and Constructed Emotion (Clark)]].

**Introspection is inference on mental causes.** Experiential surprise licenses a generative model of experience (fault in the "mirror," not the world). "Introspective dissonance" — certain vs fleeting — is high- vs low-hierarchy inference. Introspective attention hits a precision floor (pp. 245–49).

**Privacy is for social Bayes.** Consciousness is private so reports stay **conditionally independent**; we share parameters and posterior precision, not the experience itself. Motto: "consciousness is private so that it can play its social role" (pp. 249–54). Frith and Nietzsche are in the notes.

**The self is an agent-bound sensory trajectory.** A hierarchical self-model of one's causal commerce with the world, needed for [[Concepts/Active Inference and Action (Clark)|active inference]] (policies, expected proprioceptive precision). Hume sees only more experiences because that is what the self-model predicts (pp. 254–56). "Emotion is just predictive inference on interoceptive states, introspection is linked to what happens when experience expectations are violated, the privacy of mind is needed to secure social Bayesian inference, and the self is an agent-bound sensory trajectory" (p. 256).

## Key Claims / Positions

- Emotion = disambiguation of interoceptive PE, not a fingerprint and not a read-out of a prior feeling.
- Introspection is not privileged look-inside; it is more inference, with a precision floor.
- Privacy is an epistemic design feature for social inference, not a metaphysical leftover.
- The self is a model of a trajectory, posited because action needs an agent-bound cause.

## How This Relates to Prior Thinkers

- **William James / Lange, Schachter–Singer, Damasio, Prinz**: the tradition Hohwy recodes as PEM. *Descartes' Error* is now ingested ([[Sources/Descartes' Error - Antonio Damasio (1994)]]): emotion ≠ feeling; primary emotions are Jamesian profiles; secondary emotions are the prefrontal overlay; somatic markers bind those feelings to predicted outcomes. Hohwy's "not a fingerprint" is closer to Clark/Barrett than to Damasio's five universals.
- **[[Thinkers/Hume]]**: bundle theory as what a self-model *predicts*, not as a refutation of the self.
- **[[Thinkers/Andy Clark]]**: later develops interoception via Barrett, allostasis, body-budgeting, and a Dark Room answer. Hohwy 2013 already has the James–Lange PEM core and the Dark Room as phenotype priors (ch. 4). Clark is richer on mood, depression, and aesthetics; Hohwy is earlier on privacy and the trajectory-self.
- **[[Thinkers/Daniel Dennett]]**: Hohwy's self-as-model is in the neighbourhood of Dennett's narrative gravity / Joycean machine, but Hohwy does not make the deflationary move about qualia.
- **[[Concepts/Center of Narrative Gravity (Dennett)]]**: a cousin — both treat the self as a construct the system uses, not a thing it finds.
- **[[Thinkers/Anil Seth]]**: collaborator (Palmer–Hohwy–Seth 2015). Hohwy's self is a trajectory the model must posit *in order to act*. Seth's [[Concepts/Self as Controlled Hallucination (Seth)|self as controlled hallucination]] is a six-layer perception whose function is *control of essential variables*, not self-knowledge. Same PEM family; Hohwy's emphasis is epistemic/action, Seth's is allostatic/ground-state.
