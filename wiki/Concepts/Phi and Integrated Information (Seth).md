# Phi and Integrated Information (Seth)
> Every conscious experience is simultaneously *informative* (this scene, not the unused alternatives) and *integrated* (one unified scene). Treat that pair as properties to explain — do not identify consciousness with Φ.

## Summary

Chapters 2–3 of *Being You* are Seth's treatment of conscious **level** (how conscious, as opposed to of what). Measurement is not a side issue: it is how the [[Concepts/Real Problem of Consciousness (Seth)|real problem]] becomes quantitative. A "consciousness meter" is valuable even if consciousness turns out more like *life* than like *temperature*.

Level is not wakefulness (dreaming is asleep-but-conscious; the vegetative/"unresponsive wakefulness" state cycles sleep–wake with no behavioural awareness). It is not neuron count (the cerebellum has ~4× the neurons of the rest of the brain and "seems barely involved"). What matters is how parts of the **thalamocortical system** interact.

The phenomenological constraint comes from Tononi and Edelman (1998): *all* experiences are both **informative** (information as reduction of uncertainty) and **integrated** (experienced "all of a piece"). Mechanisms that *account for* experience should exhibit both. Fit-for-purpose measures must jointly score the pair: neural complexity (Tononi/Edelman/Sporns), Seth's **causal density**, later Φ variants. Algorithmic complexity (LZW) approximates randomness and so is a poor stand-in for complexity as the middle ground between order and disorder; PCI ("zap and zip": TMS + LZW of the EEG echo) only "suggests" integration. These principled measures have "not yet worked particularly well" on real imaging; Seth's bet is better maths and data, not that the insight is misguided.

**Integrated Information Theory (IIT)** is the identity claim: consciousness *is* integrated information. Φ = how much a whole generates more information than its parts. High Φ = lots of consciousness; 0 Φ = none. "Ultimate expression of a temperature-based view." The identity requires **intrinsic** (not Shannon observer-relative) information — all a system *could* do, not what it does — which for brains is "nearly or actually impossible." Hence "IIT's extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence, yet it is precisely the ambition of IIT – to solve the hard problem – that renders its most distinctive claims untestable in practice."

## Key Claims / Positions

- Keep I+I as conserved properties of experience. Drop the identity with Kelvin/temperature.
- PCI and spontaneous LZW track level independently of behaviour (lower in dreamless sleep, anaesthesia, vegetative state; higher in wake, REM, MCS, locked-in). Useful meters; incomplete theories.
- Covert consciousness is real (Owen 2006 tennis/house imagery; perhaps 10–20% of VS patients). PCI needs no language or imagery.
- Level is not a unidimensional adult-human scale. Transitions can be "sharpish" from none to some, then graded along multiple dimensions. Psychedelic LZW *increases* vs waking rest: level and content are not fully separable.
- If IIT's identity were true: unused "could-fire" neurons would change all experience; information would have ontological status (Wheeler "it from bit"); **restrained panpsychism** wherever Φ is found, "not everywhere."
- Seth's working Φs (with Barrett, Mediano) are observer-relative and usable. Measure, not theory of what consciousness is.

## How This Relates to Prior Thinkers

- **Giulio Tononi**: source and foil. Seth takes the 1998 I+I insight, criticises early IIT (Seth et al. 2006), stays engaged, collaborates on an optogenetic test of inactive vs inactivated neurons. Qualify, do not accept or reject wholesale.
- **Gerald Edelman**: Seth's mentor; co-author of the 1998 *Science* paper; neural complexity with Sporns.
- **[[Thinkers/David Chalmers]]**: 1990s "consciousness meter" (hairdryer) joke; IIT is the neuroscientific theory that "tackles the hard problem head on." Seth: that ambition is exactly what makes the identity untestable.
- **[[Thinkers/Claude Shannon]]**: observer-relative information is what IIT must reject to make Φ intrinsic — and what Seth's usable measures keep.
- **[[Thinkers/Jakob Hohwy]]**: already borrowed Dehaene's **ignition** and recast unity as one active-inference policy ([[Concepts/Perceptual Unity and Ignition (Hohwy)]]). Seth's I+I constraint is the phenomenological twin of that unity demand, without Hohwy's decline of the hard problem.
- **Dehaene / GNW**: **not discussed** in these chapters. Do not read Seth's level-measures as a GNW page. (Dehaene's *Consciousness and the Brain* is an in-progress ingest.)

## Contradictions / Open Questions
- > [!warning] If IIT's identity claim were true, Φ > 0 would license machine (and even photodiode-array) consciousness. Seth keeps the measure and refuses the identity — the same fork as his later refusal of liberal functionalist machine criteria ([[Concepts/Machine Minds (Seth)]]).
