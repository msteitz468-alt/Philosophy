# Morality as Anti-Nature (Nietzsche)
> Healthy morality serves an instinct of life. Almost every morality so far taught turns against that instinct: the church fights passion with excision. The saint is the ideal eunuch.

## Summary

A chapter of *Twilight of the Idols* (1888), completed by the next, "The ‘Improvers’ of Mankind."

Passions have a stupid early phase and a later phase when they "wed the spirit" and "spiritualize" themselves. The old moral monsters agreed: *il faut tuer les passions*. The New Testament formula is "If thy eye offend thee, pluck it out." "We no longer admire dentists who ‘pluck out’ teeth so that they will not hurt any more."

The church never asks "How can one spiritualize, beautify, deify a craving?" Its practice is **castratism** — extirpation of sensuality, pride, the lust to rule, avarice, vengefulness. "An attack on the roots of passion means an attack on the roots of life: the practice of the church is hostile to life."

Radical extirpation is the expedient of those "too weak-willed, too degenerate, to be able to impose moderation on themselves" — "the inability not to respond to a stimulus." The most poisonous things against the senses were said not by the impotent or by actual ascetics but by "the impossible ascetics."

Two "triumphs over Christianity": spiritualization of sensuality = **love**; spiritualization of hostility = valuing having enemies. "One has renounced the great life when one renounces war." "Peace of soul," the Christian desideratum, is alien — and often a misnomer for digestion, weariness, repletion, senility, laziness, or (one of the list) "calm breathing, attained ‘freedom of the will.’" "Twilight of the Idols — who knows? perhaps also only a kind of ‘peace of soul.’"

**Formula.** "Every naturalism in morality — that is, every healthy morality — is dominated by an instinct of life." "Anti-natural morality — that is, almost every morality which has so far been taught, revered, and preached — turns, conversely, against the instincts of life." "God looks at the heart" says No to both lowest and highest desires; "posits God as the enemy of life." "The saint in whom God delights is the ideal eunuch. Life has come to an end where the ‘kingdom of God’ begins."

A condemnation of life by the living is only a **symptom** of a kind of life. There is no standpoint outside life from which to judge life's value. "Life itself forces us to posit values; life itself values through us when we posit values." Even anti-natural morality is a value judgment *of* life — of "declining, weakened, weary, condemned life." Schopenhauer's "negation of the will to life" is "the very instinct of decadence, which makes an imperative of itself."

The single human being is "a piece of *fatum* from the front and from the rear." "Change yourself!" demands that everything be changed, even retroactively. Immoralists "do not easily negate; we make it a point of honor to be affirmers."

## The ‘Improvers’ of Mankind

"There are no moral facts." Moral judgments are misinterpretations, but "semiotically… invaluable": "a language of signs, a group of symptoms."

"Improvement" has meant two things: **taming** the beast called man, and **breeding** a particular kind of man. Taming (the church as kennel) weakens: "They are weakened, they are made less harmful… they become sickly beasts." The "improved" Teuton in the monastery: "a ‘sinner’… sick, miserable, hateful to himself." "The church… sickened and weakened man — and by so doing ‘improved’ him."

Breeding's example is the law of Manu (four types). It is "healthier" than the Christian dungeon and still terrible against the chandala. Christianity is then diagnosed as "the counter-movement to any morality of breeding, of race, privilege: it is the anti-Aryan religion par excellence" — "the revaluation of all Aryan values, the victory of chandala values… the undying chandala hatred… disguised as a religion of love." This is the 1888 restatement of *Genealogy* I's slave revolt / Rome vs Judaea. Report it as that diagnosis; do not expand it into a race program the vault endorses.

Both methods are "entirely worthy of each other" in means. "To make men moral one must have the unconditional resolve to act immorally." The *pia fraus* is "the heirloom of all philosophers and priests who ‘improved’ mankind." "Manu nor Plato nor Confucius nor the Jewish and Christian teachers have ever doubted their right to lie." Formula: "all the means by which one has so far attempted to make mankind moral were through and through immoral."

## Key Claims

- Spiritualize the passion; do not cut it out. Castration is a symptom of weak will.
- Healthy morality = instinct of life given a "shalt / shalt not." Anti-natural morality condemns that instinct and calls the result God.
- Value-judgments of life are symptoms. Life values through us.
- No moral facts; morality is a sign-language.
- Improvement = taming or breeding; both immoral in means; Christianity is the anti-breeding religion in this schema.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Nietzsche]]** — late formula for what *Genealogy* III called the ascetic ideal and *Case of Wagner* called decadence. "To have to fight the instincts — that is the definition of decadence" (Problem of Socrates 11) is the same claim in another chapter. *The Antichrist* §§55–57 adds the *ends* of the holy lie: Manu (preserve a noble order) vs Bible/Christianity (poison it). Three physiological types; “Wrong never lies in unequal rights; it lies in the assertion of ‘equal’ rights.” The anarchist and the Christian have the same ancestry. Do not re-ingest TI’s anti-Aryan thesis; this is preserve-vs-destroy. See [[Sources/The Antichrist - Nietzsche (Mencken, Collection 2013)]].
- **[[Concepts/Ascetic Ideal (Nietzsche)]]** — church castratism is the practice; "horror vacui" was the meaning. This chapter names the *method* (excision) and the *hostility to life*.
- **[[Concepts/Master Morality and Slave Morality (Nietzsche)]]** — Improvers §4 is the 1888 chandala/anti-Aryan restatement of the slave revolt. Keep it on that page as genealogy, not as a new racial ontology.
- **[[Thinkers/Arthur Schopenhauer]]** — "negation of the will to life" = decadence making itself imperative.
- **[[Concepts/Amor Fati (Love of Fate)]]** — **not named.** Closest: the human being as *fatum*; immoralists as affirmers. Do not weld §276 to this chapter.
