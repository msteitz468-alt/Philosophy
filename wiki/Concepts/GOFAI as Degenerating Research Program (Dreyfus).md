# GOFAI as Degenerating Research Program (Dreyfus)
> After fifty years, facts-plus-rules AI has not produced general intelligence. That is not a proof of impossibility. It is what [[Thinkers/Imre Lakatos]] called a degenerating research program.

## Summary

John Haugeland's label **GOFAI** (Good Old-Fashioned AI) names the program [[Thinkers/Alan Turing]] opened in 1950: a high-speed digital computer, programmed with rules and facts, exhibiting intelligent behavior. [[Thinkers/Hubert Dreyfus]]'s 1992 introduction treats that program as historically over. "This book now offers not a controversial position in an ongoing debate but a view of a bygone period of history."

A **degenerating research program**, as Dreyfus takes the term from Lakatos, starts with impressive results in a limited domain, expands to similar problems, then meets unexpected phenomena that resist the techniques. Researchers stay until a progressive alternative appears. GOFAI's pattern:

1. **Promise (late 1950s).** Newell and Simon show that bit-strings can stand for world features and programs for rules relating them — the computer as a **physical symbol system**. The information-processing model of mind is born.
2. **Expansion (1960s).** Micro-worlds (Winograd's SHRDLU) make extension look routine. [[Thinkers/Marvin Minsky]] announces that "within a generation the problem of creating 'artificial intelligence' will be substantially solved." Simon (1957) had already predicted a computer chess champion within ten years and, in 1965, that "machines will be capable, within twenty years, of doing any work that a man can do."
3. **Stall.** Children's-story programs lack a four-year-old's common sense. Machine translation is written off by the NAS/NRC. GPS is abandoned. Chess does not produce a champion on the predicted schedule. Bar-Hillel names the "fallacy of the successful first step."
4. **Ad hoc rescue (1967–77).** Micro-worlds are elevated to a method; then frames, scripts, and KRL try to represent everyday understanding. None generalizes. Minsky and Papert themselves call the micro-world "a fairyland in which things are so simplified that almost every statement about them would be literally false if asserted about the real world."
5. **First defection.** Expert systems (DENDRAL, MYCIN) and Japan's Fifth Generation retreat to domains where relevance is pre-fixed. Dreyfus and Stuart predicted defeat; both the Japanese project and American hematology-diagnosis efforts conceded.
6. **Collapse.** Connectionism's arrival "completed the degeneration of the GOFAI research program." Defense Department funding shifted to neural nets after the autonomous-truck goals failed.

Lenat's **CYC** is the last GOFAI defense: formalize "consensus knowledge" by ontological engineering. Dreyfus's verdict: body, deixis, *savoir faire*, innovation, and analogy make the project hopeless. More facts make machines slower and humans faster.

The 1972 conclusion already had the right grain: associationistic and simple-formal tasks are digitally simulable; complex-formal tasks are formalizable in principle and intractable in practice; **nonformal** intelligence (*esprit de finesse*) is not a further increment on the same continuum. Alchemy distilled mercury from dirt and never transmuted lead. Early AI success licenses the same patience-as-adventure.

This is **not** a negative proof. "No one has been able to come up with such a negative proof." The claim is that the facts-and-rules program has reached a dead end, and there is no reason to think it could ever succeed.

## Key Claims / Positions

### How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Hubert Dreyfus]]**: GOFAI is Western metaphysics implemented. Failure is evidence against the [[Concepts/Four Assumptions of Artificial Reason (Dreyfus)|four assumptions]], not a funding drought.
- **[[Thinkers/Marvin Minsky]]**: the same history is a sequence of first steps. Frames and the large data base are the next increment. Dreyfus reads this as the continuum error.
- **[[Thinkers/Melanie Mitchell]]**: later documents the same cycle as [[Concepts/AI Hype Cycles (Mitchell)|hype cycles]] and [[Concepts/Brittleness of Deep Learning (Mitchell)|brittleness]], now for deep learning rather than GPS. She shares the empirical moral and not the Heideggerian diagnosis.
- **[[Thinkers/Thomas Kuhn]]**: Dreyfus's 1972 methodology chapter already uses anomaly-without-paradigm against Newell–Simon. The 1992 verdict is Lakatosian (degenerating program) rather than a full paradigm-shift narrative — connectionism is the alternative that let researchers leave, not a new incommensurable world.
- **[[Thinkers/Alan Turing]]**: origin of the dream, not its defender in this book. Dreyfus quotes the discrete-state / universal-machine / imitation-game apparatus as the point at which the metaphysical demand acquired a machine.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] Whether later deep learning is a new program or GOFAI's connectionist successor still failing the same [[Concepts/Commonsense Knowledge Problem (Dreyfus)|commonsense]] and generalization tests — see [[Contradictions/Dreyfus vs Computationalism - Skilled Coping vs Formal Rules]].
- Dreyfus's own 1957–67 cutoff was attacked as dated in 1972; the 1979 and 1992 introductions exist to absorb that objection. A 2026 reader can run the same test again.
