# Paul as Falsifier of the Evangel (Nietzsche)
> There was only one Christian, and he died on the cross. Paul is the genius for hatred who nailed him to *his* cross.

## Summary

*The Antichrist* §§36–47 is the vault’s first primary for Nietzsche’s Paul. It is a *falsifier-type*, not a thinker page.

§36: the church is built from *denial* of the Gospels — world-historical irony. §37: from the death on the cross, Christian history is a “progressively clumsier misunderstanding of an original symbolism,” ending in the church as hostility to honesty, loftiness, discipline of spirit, and “spontaneous and kindly humanity.” §38: “the other world,” “the last judgment,” “the immortality of the soul” are instruments of torture by which the priest remains master.

**§39** is the hinge: “The very word ‘Christianity’ is a misunderstanding—at bottom there was only one Christian, and he died on the cross. The ‘Gospels’ died on the cross.” What was then called Gospels was the reverse of what he had lived: “bad tidings,” a **Dysangelium**. Not faith in salvation but the *way of life* is Christian. “Faith” is a cloak for instincts and an instinctive hatred of reality.

Decay begins “the day after.” The disciples did not take the example of dying free of *ressentiment*. Revenge demanded recompense and judgment; the already-fulfilled kingdom was recast as a future last act; Jesus was turned into a Pharisee/theologian. “The One God and the Only Son of God: both were products of *ressentiment*” (§40). “God gave his son as a sacrifice” ends the gospels (pagan sacrifice of the innocent). Jesus had done away with guilt and *lived* God–man unity. Judgment, second coming, sacrificial death, and resurrection juggle blessedness into a state *after* death (§41).

**§42:** the cross ended an effort to found a “Buddhistic peace movement” and happiness on earth. Paul is the dysangelist, “genius for hatred,” who “nailed him to his own cross,” struck out Christian “yesterday,” and shifted the centre of gravity to the lie of the “risen” Jesus. He needed the *death*, not the life; he willed power. Immortality is the doctrine of “judgment,” the device of priestly tyranny (what Mohammed later borrowed).

**§43:** personal immortality removes life’s centre of gravity, flatters vanity, and lures “the botched.” “Equal rights for all” is forged from *ressentiment* of the masses against the pathos of distance. “The gospel of the ‘lowly’ lowers.”

**§44–46:** the Gospels already show decay in the primitive community; Paul, “with the cynical logic of a rabbi,” only concludes it. “The Christian is simply a Jew of the ‘reformed’ confession.” Words put in the Master’s mouth (Mark, Matthew, Luke) and 1 Corinthians are unevangelical, pay-demanding, Chandala-morality. “Paul was the greatest of all apostles of revenge.” The NT is psychologically filthy. Only Pilate is honourable: “What is truth?”

**§47:** “We deny that God is God.” Formula: *deus, qualem Paulus creavit, dei negatio.* Faith, as an imperative, vetoes science. The church borrows this from Paul.

Do **not** create a Paul thinker page from this polemic.

## Key Claims

- Only one Christian; the Gospels died with him. What followed is a *Dysangelium*.
- Paul needed the death and the risen Jesus, not the life. Centre of gravity shifted *behind* existence.
- One God / Only Son, judgment, sacrificial death, personal immortality are products of *ressentiment* and priestly will to power.
- Equal rights / gospel of the lowly is the political fruit of that shift.
- Paul’s God is not an error about a real God. It is the negation of God: *dei negatio*.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] Do not identify Paul with *Genealogy*’s “Jewish priest” without remainder. Decay begins in the little community the day after the cross; Paul *concludes* it, as a distinct falsifier who needed the *risen* Jesus.
- > [!warning] “The Christian is simply a Jew of the ‘reformed’ confession” is this book’s polemic, not a history of Second Temple Judaism. Report it; do not expand it into a race thesis the vault endorses.
