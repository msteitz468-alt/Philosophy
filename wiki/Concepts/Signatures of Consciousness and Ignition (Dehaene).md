# Signatures of Consciousness and Ignition (Dehaene)

> Reproducible markers that appear if and only if a stimulus is consciously accessed: a late parietal–prefrontal avalanche, the P3 wave, a late gamma burst, and a long-distance "brain web." Ignition is the name of the phase transition.

## Summary

Fifteen years of fMRI, EEG/MEG, and intracranial electrodes, Dehaene says, yielded "the same signatures" across vision, audition, touch, and cognition. A **signature** is stronger than a correlate: pre-stimulus fluctuations and linear early visual ramps *predict* ignition but are not the conscious state. The signatures are content-specific, late, and (where tested) causally intervening.

### The four laboratory signatures (Ch. 4)

1. **Spatial ignition.** Subliminal perception is "a surf wave that looms large on the horizon but merely licks your feet." Conscious perception is "a tsunami — or perhaps an avalanche." Crossing threshold amplifies sensory activity into parietal–prefrontal circuits (plus ACC). In one intracranial series, ~70% of sites feel a seen word vs ~25% unseen; visual word-form area can jump ~12×.
2. **P3 / P300.** Third large positive peak, onset ~270 ms, peak 350–500 ms. "Our consciousness lags behind the external world" by at least a third of a second (often half a second if the stimulus is faint or the mind is occupied). All-or-none: early P1/N1 can be identical on seen and unseen trials (Del Cul). One P3 can exclude another (blink, refractory). Conscious errors carry a Pe "virtually indistinguishable" from P3. Salti & Lamy: same stimulus + same correct response → only full frontal P3 indexes awareness.
3. **Late gamma burst.** Sustained high-frequency power from ~300 ms on seen trials only. Early gamma is *not* sufficient — the reply to Crick & Koch's 40 Hz. "Too strong": unconscious stimuli also induce gamma.
4. **Brain web.** Late long-distance synchronization and bidirectional Granger "causal explosion" (forward + top-down) on seen trials only. "Consciousness lives in the loops: reverberating neuronal activity, circulating in the web of our cortical connections, causes our conscious experiences." Lamme's local A↔B recurrence is necessary, not sufficient.

Ignition is a **phase transition / bifurcation**, not a ramp — Hebb's cell assembly as applause that suddenly fills the hall. Content cells (Quiroga/Fried: Clinton, WTC) fire iff the picture is seen, imagined, or recalled. TMS and intracranial stimulation can *cause* phosphenes, memories, out-of-body feelings, but "local activity is insufficient… the induced activity must first be dispatched to distant brain sites." Dual TMS / parietal–frontal disruption can destroy a percept or split subjective from objective performance (Lau).

Descartes's *Meditation* II epigraph — "a thing which thinks" — is reduced: "It is at this point, where a coherent brain web suddenly ignites, that conscious awareness seems to be established."

### Clinic (Ch. 6)

The same markers become a "consciousness-o-meter." Bedside exam misses ~40% of MCS and often misses locked-in (mean 2.5 months to correct diagnosis). Tests are **asymmetric**: positive ≈ conscious; negative proves nothing.

- Owen 2006 tennis/navigation fMRI: sustained prefrontal–parietal activity ~30 s is workspace, not automatic priming. Ropper's "I have brain activation, therefore I am" is rejected as a joke that gets the inference wrong — **cortico ergo sum** when the activation is *willful and sustained*.
- Local–global oddball (Bekinschtein, Naccache, Sigman, Dehaene): unconscious **MMN** vs conscious **P3b** to a global rule violation. P3b ⇒ conscious (and predicts recovery); many false negatives.
- Massimini TMS–EEG "ping": complex, long-lasting, distributed echo indexes consciousness even without a task.
- King/Sitt/Naccache EEG classifier (weighted symbolic mutual information, β/γ): long-distance sharing can outrun the bedside exam.

Interventions: Schiff/Giacino central-thalamic DBS; levodopa, amantadine; paradoxical Ambien. Ethics: half of ICU deaths are withdrawal of support; treating *all* VS patients as conscious on the strength of rare Owen-type cases would be "utterly irrational."

### Development and species (Ch. 7)

Two-month-olds already show a slow frontal novelty response and working memory; five-month-olds have a masking threshold (~100 ms) and the same two-stage linear-then-ignition architecture, three to four times slower. Macaques show rivalry, masking, blindsight, delayed response, and local–global prefrontal responses. Mice may pass the oddball. The workspace is old; the human extra is a compositional language of thought, not access itself.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Jakob Hohwy]]**: this is the primary he already cited. He accepts the evidence and *reinterprets* ignition as the switch from perceptual to active inference ([[Concepts/Perceptual Unity and Ignition (Hohwy)]]).
- **[[Thinkers/David Chalmers]]**: NCC research, including these signatures, only ever ties a process to *awareness*. "We can only have empirical evidence for a link between a process N and consciousness if we already have evidence for a link between N and awareness."
- **Crick & Koch**: opening allies (illusions; search for a signature); 40 Hz rejected as stated.
- **Libet**: not used in Ch. 4. Dennett already read Libet as confusing time of representing with time represented; Dehaene's lag is a *processing* delay before ignition, not backwards referral.
