# Metaphysics of Sexual Love (Schopenhauer)
> All amorousness is individualized sexual impulse. What is decided is the composition of the next generation. After possession, the individual is the dupe of the species.

## Summary

Chapter XL of *WWR* Volume 2 says the unused space of Book IV is given to **sexual love** as the inner nature of **affirmation of the will-to-live**, “entirely neglected by all philosophers before me.” Chapters XLII–XLIV are the doctrine.

The sexual impulse is “the most complete manifestation of the will-to-live.” The genitals are “the focus of the will.” Individual life is the force of the species “checked by damming up.” What love decides is nothing less than “the *composition of the next generation*” (*existentia* by the impulse generally; *essentia* by selection).

The **genius of the species** (Cupid) uses **instinct/delusion**: the species-good appears as private bliss. After consummation “everyone who is in love finds himself duped; for the delusion by means of which the individual was the dupe of the species has disappeared.” Love-marriages serve the species at the present’s expense.

**Heredity (XLIII), folded here, not a separate page:** will/character from the father; intellect from the mother. Character is unalterable. The same will, given a new maternal intellect each generation, gets a new view of life and must again **affirm or deny**.

Chapter XLV then: the life of a man, “with its endless care, want, and suffering, is to be regarded as the explanation and paraphrase of the act of procreation.” Shame at the act proves the body as objectification of will. Optimism would require that act to look otherwise.

*Parerga* II restates generation as the “world-knot.” This volume is the systematic primary. See [[Concepts/Affirmation and Denial of the Will-to-Live (Schopenhauer)]].

## Key Claims

- Romantic love is species-interest wearing the mask of personal happiness.
- Sexual impulse is the will-to-live’s most complete manifestation.
- Father gives will; mother gives intellect.
- After possession the delusion vanishes.
- Generation is affirmation; voluntary suppression of sex is the will’s turning.
