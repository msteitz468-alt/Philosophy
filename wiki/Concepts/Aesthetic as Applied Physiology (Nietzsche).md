# Aesthetic as Applied Physiology (Nietzsche)
> Why clothe a fact of breath and foot in aesthetic formulae? Aesthetic is applied physiology.

## Summary

*Nietzsche Contra Wagner*, “Wherein I Raise Objections,” refuses to start with taste-language. “My objections to Wagner’s music are physiological objections. Why should I therefore begin by clothing them in æsthetic formulæ? Æsthetic is indeed nothing more than applied physiology.” The *petit fait vrai*: he can no longer breathe easily; the foot rebels (it needs time, dance, march — even the Kaiser could not march to the Imperial March); stomach, heart, circulation, hoarseness. There is “no such thing as a soul.” The body needs relief: light, bold, unfettered rhythms; weight taken off brazen life by smooth melody. “Wagner makes one ill.”

“Wagner as a Danger” gives the musical law behind the symptom. Old music made one *dance*; measure forced “continual sobriety of thought.” Wagner wanted another movement: “we must swim, we must hover.” “Unending melody” breaks symmetry of time and strength. The danger is “the complete degeneration of the feeling for rhythm, *chaos* in the place of rhythm,” worse when music cleaves to pantomime. “Expressiveness at all costs and music a servant, a slave to attitudes—this is the end.”

*The Case of Wagner* promised a fuller “Physiology of Art” and gave the decadent-style formula (life no longer animates the whole). This compilation states the method: judge the work by what it does to the animal functions, not by an independent “beauty.” See [[Concepts/Decadence (Nietzsche)]], [[Concepts/Histrionics and Theatrocracy (Nietzsche)]].

## Key Claims

- Aesthetic formulae are clothing; the fact is physiological.
- No soul: music is for the body’s relief or it is disease.
- Unending melody overthrows the first principle of older music (dance, measure).
- Effect at all costs selects the mob, the blasé, the Wagnerite.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Nietzsche]]** — NCW method. *CW* §7 is the style-formula of the same physiology. *GS* 1887 Preface: philosophies as misunderstandings of the body; the philosophical physician. Do not treat NCW as the first time he thought this; treat it as the Wagner *application* he selected in 1888.
- **[[Concepts/The Great Health (Nietzsche)]]** — convalescent need: another art, light feet, south. The foot that wants to march is that weather made local.
- **[[Thinkers/Kant]]** — not named here. The implied foil is disinterested aesthetic judgment. “Disinterestedness” is branded decadence in “We Antipodes.” Do not write a Kant debate this chapter does not have.
- **[[Thinkers/Arthur Schopenhauer]]** — music as immediate Will is the metaphysics this method will not use. NCW’s Wagner is a body-event, not a copy of the Will.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] This is not a hygiene programme and not Foucault’s bio-power. It is a criterion for works: do they accelerate the animal functions or make one ill?
