# Will to Power as Knowledge (Nietzsche)
> Knowledge works as a tool of power. There are no facts, only interpretations. To impose upon becoming the character of being — that is the supreme will to power.

## Summary

Book III, Part I of the Kaufmann compilation [[Sources/The Will to Power - Nietzsche (Kaufmann, Collection 2013)]] (notes 466–617). Not a published treatise. The 2013 collection has **only this part** of Book III; Nature, Society, and Art are missing. Do not import them.

The published epistemology is already in the vault: *Gay Science* (useful errors, no organ for truth, infinite interpretations) and *Beyond Good and Evil* (frog perspectives, interpretation not text). These notes are the **workshop**: thicker anti-positivism, thicker anti-Cartesian subject, thicker physiology of logic. They are not a replacement for *BGE* §22 or *GS* §354.

## Method and the inner world (466–480)

**n.466–469:** what marks the century is **method** over science. Religious self-control schooled scientific conscientiousness.

**n.473:** the intellect cannot criticize itself; that would presuppose a "true reality" / in-itself. **n.477–479:** there are no given "facts of consciousness." The inner world is as phenomenal as the outer. "Thinking," spirit, and a subject-substratum are fictions. Consciousness-contents are **terminal phenomena** and **cause nothing**. Inner experience **inverts** cause and effect. See [[Concepts/The Four Great Errors (Nietzsche)]] — this is the notebook of Twilight's "false causality," not a second doctrine.

**n.480:** "Knowledge works as a tool of power." The concept of knowledge is "anthropocentric and biological." Organs of knowledge develop so that observations suffice for preservation. "The measure of the desire for knowledge depends upon the measure to which the will to power grows in a species."

## No facts, only interpretations (n.481)

Against positivism's "There are only facts":

> "No, facts is precisely what there is not, only interpretations. We cannot establish any fact 'in itself'… The 'subject' is not something given, it is something added and invented and projected behind what there is.—Finally, is it necessary to posit an interpreter behind the interpretation? Even this is invention, hypothesis."

> "In so far as the word 'knowledge' has any meaning, the world is knowable; but it is interpretable otherwise, it has no meaning behind it, but countless meanings.—'Perspectivism.'"

> "It is our needs that interpret the world; our drives and their For and Against. Every drive is a kind of lust to rule."

This is where the vault's word **perspectivism** is **named** as a heading for the claim. *GS* §354 named "phenomenalism and perspectivism" for herd-consciousness. Do not flatten n.481 into that aphorism, or into *BGE* §2. See [[Concepts/Perspectivism (Nietzsche)]].

**n.483:** a belief can be a condition of life and nonetheless be false. **n.488:** "there is no such thing as will" — meaning the mental-causal faculty. **Do not flatten will-to-power into Twilight Error 2's "will as cause."** The notes deny that faculty and still talk power as species, drive, and command.

## Logic, truth, consciousness (493–529)

**n.493:** "Truth is the kind of error without which a certain species of life could not live." **n.507:** "what is needed is that something must be held to be true—not that something is true."

**n.512:** logic does not spring from will to truth; identical cases must be *treated as* fulfilled — fundamental falsification. **n.515:** "Not 'to know' but to schematize—to impose upon chaos as much regularity and form as our practical needs require." **n.516:** the law of contradiction is an **imperative** for what should count as true, not a criterion of being. **n.521:** "The world seems logical to us because we have made it logical."

**n.524:** consciousness is "not the directing agent, but an organ of the directing agent" — a means of communication.

## Judgment, causalism, thing-in-itself (531–586)

**n.531:** "If I say 'lightning flashes,' I have posited the flash once as an activity and a second time as a subject." **n.534:** "The criterion of truth resides in the enhancement of the feeling of power." **n.540:** "There are many kinds of eyes. … and consequently there are many kinds of 'truths,' and consequently there is no truth."

**n.551:** "There are neither causes nor effects. Linguistically we do not know how to rid ourselves of them." **n.552:** drop the effective subject and the thing-in-itself falls with it. "'Truth' is therefore not something there, that might be found or discovered—but something that must be created… It is a word for the 'will to power.'"

**n.553–568:** Kant has no right to appearance vs thing-in-itself. No facts-in-themselves; essence is "what is that for me?" Interpretation *is* will to power (do not ask "who interprets?"). **n.567:** "The antithesis of the apparent world and the true world reduced to the antithesis 'world' and 'nothing.'" Notebook of [[Concepts/How the True World Became a Fable (Nietzsche)]].

Metaphysical need (**n.570–586**) = deriving the conditioned from the unconditioned (laziness, timidity, *ressentiment*). Moral values infest epistemology (Kant, Plato, Spinoza, Descartes). Will to truth is a form of will to power, not a moral force.

## Science and the last note (594–617)

Science schematizes chaos and masters nature; it is a **means**. Need a **will to ignorance**. **n.604:** "There are no facts, everything is in flux, incomprehensible, elusive; what is relatively most enduring is—our opinions." **n.616:** "the world with which we are concerned is false, i.e., is not a fact but a fable… for—there is no 'truth.'"

**n.617** is the last note in this file. It is **not** note 1067.

> "To impose upon becoming the character of being—that is the supreme will to power."

> "That everything recurs is the closest approximation of a world of becoming to a world of being:—high point of the meditation."

Knowledge-in-itself in a world of becoming is impossible; knowledge is "error concerning oneself, as will to power, as will to deception." Life redefined as will to power. "The entire idealism of mankind hitherto is on the point of changing suddenly into nihilism." Zarathustra "adopts a parodistic attitude toward all former values as a consequence of his abundance."

This recurrence is a **stamp of being onto becoming**. It is not *GS* §341's demon and not **n.55**'s "meaningless, eternally." See [[Concepts/Eternal Recurrence (Nietzsche)]], [[Concepts/European Nihilism (Nietzsche)]].

## Key Claims

- Knowledge is a tool of power, not a mirror of a fact-in-itself.
- No facts, only interpretations; no need for an interpreter-behind (**n.481**).
- The subject, will-as-cause, and thing-in-itself are fictions that life requires.
- Logic and causality are commanded falsifications (making-equal; doer/deed).
- Truth is created; the word is a name for will to power (**n.552**).
- Supreme will to power = imposing being on becoming (**n.617**). The cosmological closer (n.1067) is **not in this file**.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Nietzsche]]** — published perspectivism is *GS* / *BGE*. This is the notebook that makes the same move against positivism and the ego. Quote n.481 as notebooks, not as *BGE*.
- **[[Thinkers/Kant]]** — thing-in-itself has no right (**n.473, 553**); a tool cannot criticize itself (**n.486**); categories as species idiosyncrasy (**n.515**).
- **[[Thinkers/Spinoza]]** — named in the metaphysical-need stretch (**n.576–578**): fear of change; value of the transient against *sub specie aeternitatis*.
- **[[Thinkers/David Hume]]** — nearest published ally on cause as custom. These notes go further: no causes at all, linguistically inescapable.
- **[[Concepts/Correspondence Theory of Truth (Russell)]]** — the foil. Truth here is not intellect matching reality. Russell's survey already ranked Nietzsche low; this notebook is the epistemology that ranking never read.
- **[[Thinkers/Martin Heidegger]]** — being as will to power / value-positing. **n.617** is the sentence that reading needs. Do not let Heidegger's "last metaphysician" erase the anti-subject move in n.481.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] **n.480/552/617** talk will to power; **n.478/488/529** deny will as cause. Both are in this Part. Do not pick one.
- > [!warning] "No facts, only interpretations" is **n.481**, not a published slogan and not a license to skip the published books.
- > [!warning] n.617 ≠ n.1067. This file ends at 617.
