# Indestructibility of Our True Nature (Schopenhauer)
> After death you will be what you were before your birth. Cerebral consciousness perishes. The will does not. Indestructibility without continuance.

## Summary

The systematic primary is *WWR* Volume 2, Chapter XLI — the longest chapter in the supplements. *Parerga* II Ch. X is, by its own admission, “a short selection of isolated observations” on a doctrine “dealt with… consistently and fully in my chief work.”

**XLI.** Fear of death is *a priori* the reverse of the **will-to-live**, not a judgement of life’s value. “Death is the real inspiring genius or Musagetes of philosophy… without death there would hardly have been any philosophizing.” Non-existence after death is the same as non-existence before birth; Epicurus is right *for knowledge*. *Fuga mortis* belongs to the **blind will**.

What is indestructible is **not** the person, consciousness, or intellect (brain-function, phenomenon). “All philosophers have made the mistake of placing that which is metaphysical, indestructible, and eternal in man in the *intellect.* It lies exclusively in the *will.*” Birth and death bound the phenomenon; the will is not in time.

The **Idea/species** is the will’s most immediate objectification in time; the individual is the Idea under the **principium individuationis**. “Death is for the species what sleep is for the individual.” **Palingenesis, not metempsychosis:** the will sheds the mortal intellect (Lethe) and takes another. Each renewal is a new chance to **deny** the will. Death is the reprimand of egoism.

*Parerga* II then: “After your death you will be what you were before your birth.” After death one is “everything and nothing”: “indestructibility without continuance.” Duration and annihilation are schemas transferred from the phenomenon to the thing-in-itself. See [[Concepts/The Will (Schopenhauer)]], [[Concepts/Affirmation and Denial of the Will-to-Live (Schopenhauer)]].

Do **not** weld to [[Concepts/Being-toward-Death and Authenticity (Heidegger)]]. Here death annihilates the knowing individual; consolation is metaphysical identity of will/species, or denial — not authentic resoluteness.

## Key Claims

- Immortality of personal consciousness is the wrong question.
- Intellect dies with the brain. Will does not.
- Palingenesis ≠ metempsychosis.
- “Everything and nothing”: indestructible without continuance in time.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Arthur Schopenhauer]]** — *WWR* II Ch. XLI is the system; *Parerga* II Ch. X is additional remarks. Volume 1’s spirit-seeing essay already said intellect is a bodily function and perishes ([[Concepts/Spirit-Seeing and Idealistic Explanation (Schopenhauer)]]).
- **[[Thinkers/Kant]]** — the paralogism of a thinking substance is already demolished. This chapter uses that demolition.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] “Not absolutely without consciousness” after the subject/object contrast vanishes is left as a hint, not a positive doctrine of a surviving mind.
