# Self as Controlled Hallucination (Seth)
> The self is not the perceiver behind the eyes. It is another perception — a tightly woven bundle of neurally encoded predictions geared toward keeping the body alive.

## Summary

Part III of *Being You* turns [[Concepts/Controlled Hallucination (Clark)|controlled hallucination]] inward. Hospital delirium (Seth's mother) is the clinical setup: "Is [the self] the sort of thing that can be departed from and returned to?" Against the Cartesian "indivisible, immutable, transcendental" soul — and against the intuition that unified selfhood "signif[ies] the existence of an 'actual self'" — Seth treats the self as a perception of a very special kind. Its elements are "Bayesian best guesses, designed by evolution to keep you alive."

The self is **layered and dissociable**, bound only in ordinary cases:

1. **Bedrock / ground-state** — formless "feeling of being alive" / "just being" an organism, prior even to ownership and perspective. The start of [[Concepts/Beast Machine Theory (Seth)|beast-machine]] selfhood.
2. **Embodied selfhood** — ownership of *this* body; emotions, moods, arousal. Rubber-hand, full-body, and body-swap illusions; autoscopic vs heautoscopic (doppelgänger) hallucinations.
3. **Perspectival self** — first-person origin, usually "between the eyes."
4. **Volitional self** — intention and agency (what people usually mean by free will). Developed in [[Concepts/Free Will as Controlled Hallucination (Seth)]].
5. **Narrative self** — name, autobiographical thread, past/future; enables regret. Clive Wearing / Sacks as the thread cut.
6. **Social self** — "how I perceive others perceiving me"; theory of mind as nested generative models.

**Subjective stability** of the self is exaggerated (self-change-blindness). The function is not self-knowledge. "We do not perceive ourselves in order to know ourselves, we perceive ourselves in order to control ourselves." Interoceptive inference is the mechanism of layers 1–2: one Bayesian process, not appraisal theory's two-step (bodily readout + cognitive evaluation).

## Key Claims / Positions

- The self is not an immutable entity lurking behind the windows of the eyes.
- Unified experience of being me does not imply an actual self (Kant: no simple substance; Hume: bundle; Metzinger: *Being No One*; Buddhists; split-brain; craniopagus twins).
- Rubber-hand and OBE phenomena are inferences, not evidence of a detachable soul ("skyhooks").
- Suggestibility may confound some embodiment illusions (Lush and Dienes).

## How This Relates to Prior Thinkers

- **[[Thinkers/Daniel Dennett]]**: Seth's *narrative self* is the nearest neighbour to [[Concepts/Center of Narrative Gravity (Dennett)]]. Dennett is **not named** in these chapters; the teletransportation setup comes via Parfit / Lem.
- **[[Thinkers/Jakob Hohwy]]**: [[Concepts/Self as Sensory Trajectory (Hohwy)|self as agent-bound sensory trajectory]] is what a hierarchical model must posit in order to act. Seth's emphasis is control-first *stability* of essential variables, not trajectory-over-time. Palmer–Hohwy–Seth 2015 is cited for social objecthood.
- **Thomas Metzinger**: *Being No One* — there is no one home; there is a transparent self-model.
- **[[Thinkers/Hume]]** / **[[Thinkers/Kant]]**: bundle vs no simple substance, used as historical dissolutions of the actual-self inference.
- **[[Thinkers/Andy Clark]]**: constructed emotion and interoceptive PP; Clark does not give this six-layer taxonomy.

## Contradictions / Open Questions
- > [!warning] If the self is a perception for control, the "I" that has experiences is itself a construct. That is friendly to Dennett and Metzinger and hostile to any view (Cartesian, or some readings of Nagel) on which a subject must already be in place for there to be something it is like.
