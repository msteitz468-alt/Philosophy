# Li — Ritual Propriety (Confucius)

> In Annping Chin's *Analects*, *li* is **rites** / **ritual propriety** — the measured forms of serving, burying, sacrificing, receiving a guest, employing the people, and holding oneself. Harmony is "what is most prized" in their practice; harmony aimed at without the rules "will not work" (1.12). Without humaneness, a man can do nothing with the rites (3.3). Returning to them is the way to be humane (12.1).

## Load-bearing claims in the text

- **Harmony under rules (1.12).** Youzi: "Harmony is what is most prized in the practice of the rites [*li*]. … Yet it does not always work: if you aim only at achieving harmony … and do not let the rules of the rites guide your action, it will not work." Liu Baonan against Xing Bing: "Harmony here refers only to the rites, not to music."
- **Virtue-rule needs them (2.3).** Guide with *de* and keep in line with *li*: the people "will have a sense of shame and will know to reform themselves." Ordinances plus punishment: stay out of trouble, no shame.
- **Filiality is rites, not chores (2.5).** "When your parents are alive, serve them according to the rites. When they die, bury them according to the rites, and then offer sacrifice to them according to the rites."
- **Zhou as the model (3.14).** "Zhou took stock of the two previous dynasties. Splendid is her culture! I follow the Zhou."
- **Spirit without which virtues warp (8.2).** Without "the spirit of the rites," respect becomes tiring, caution timidity, bravery unruly, forthrightness derisive.
- **Curriculum (8.8, 16.13).** "The *Odes* are to stimulate [our mind and spirit]. The rites are to steady us. Music is the final lesson." To Boyu: "Unless you learn the *Odes*, you won't be able to speak. … Unless you learn the rites, you won't be able to find your balance."
- **Change allowed, presumption not (9.3).** Silk caps instead of linen: frugal, accepted. Bowing at the top of the stairs: presumptuous, refused.
- **Embodied (Book Ten).** Chin: "essentially a record of what other people observed about Confucius" — home "too clumsy to speak"; temple and court fluent, cautious, not more than necessary. Stables fire: "Was anyone hurt?" He did not ask about the horses (10.17). Not a complete code; 10.5 (jade-tablet embassy) "could not have taken place."
- **Return (12.1–12.2).** *Keji fuli*: restrain the self and return to the rites. In office: receive as an honored guest; employ the people as if in charge of a grand sacrifice.
- **Names before rites (13.3).** If names are not rectified, nothing gets accomplished, "rites and music will not flourish," punishments take their place and fail to be just.
- **Not the bronze (17.11).** Chin's cluster: jade and silk are not themselves the rites; the spirit is.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **Vs. *Daodejing* ch. 38**: "Ritual propriety is the thinning out of loyalty and trust, the beginning of disorder." Confucius: rites are how humaneness takes form, and how a people acquire shame instead of merely dodging statutes (2.3). Same graph *li*; opposite place in the descent/ascent. See [[Contradictions/Confucius vs Laozi - Humaneness and Rites vs Course Lost]].
- **Vs. [[Concepts/Unhewn Background and the A-B Reversal (Laozi)]]**: carving and naming shatter the unhewn into "vessels and tools." Confucius wants the gentleman *not* to be a vessel (2.12) but he wants the Zhou forms — the carving is the education.
- **Vs. [[Concepts/Spiritual Exercises (Hadot)]]**: Odes / rites / music are formative practices that change speech, balance, and spirit (8.8, 16.13). They are not Hadot's Greco-Roman *askēsis* (live, dialogue, die, read) and they are aimed at office, not at cosmic consciousness.
- **Vs. [[Concepts/Theurgy (Iamblichus)]]**: both are ritual. Iamblichus's theurgy is "divine work" for union with the gods. Confucian *li* is human relationships and Zhou culture; gods and spirits are to be respected and kept at a distance (6.22); he "did not speak about … spirits" (7.21).
- **Vs. Plato's music and gymnastic** ([[Thinkers/Plato]]): both form character before argument. Confucius does not exile the *Odes*; they *are* the education in speaking (16.13, 13.5).

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] 3.8 color/white: Han/Qing (Chin) = white *after* colors, nature rich, *li* distinguishes it. Zhu Xi = colors on a white ground, nature plain. Chin: the Song reading is their agenda, not Confucius.
- Book Ten as diary vs teaching-demo vs later compilation. Chin treats it as observed portrait, not a rule dump.
- 15.5 Shun's *wuwei* (face south) can be read as ritual stillness or as Laozi-adjacent nondoing. Confucian commentators insist on the difference; Chin says "some differences do exist" *and* both emphasize the ruler's cultivation.
