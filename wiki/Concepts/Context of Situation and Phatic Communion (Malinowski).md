# Context of Situation and Phatic Communion (Malinowski)

> Two coinages from [[Thinkers/Bronislaw Malinowski]]'s supplement to *The Meaning of Meaning*: meaning lives in the **situation** speech is bound up with, and a large class of talk (**phatic communion**) creates ties by the exchange of words whose meanings barely matter.

## Summary

Malinowski writes as a Trobriand field-worker who cannot translate canoe-boasts, fishing commands, or even tense/aspect (*boge laymayse* ≈ "already moving hither," not "have arrived") without an account of culture, custom, and tribal psychology. Primitive living speech is not a self-contained inscription. Meaning is not "contained in" the utterance; utterance and situation are "bound up inextricably."

**Context of situation** — "if I may be allowed to coin an expression" (p. 306). A widening of verbal context into the conditions under which speech occurs. He *equates* it with Ogden–Richards's [[Concepts/Contextual Theory of Reference (Ogden-Richards)|sign-situation]], then immediately marks the "context" as "compatible, but not identical" with theirs. His is ethnographic and behavioural; theirs is psychological/mnemic.

**Language as a mode of action.** In its primitive function language is "not an instrument of reflection." Fishing commands are links in concerted work. To treat language as the embodiment of thought is "to take a one-sided view of one of its most derivate… functions."

**Four speech-types**:
1. **Speech-in-action** — technical, embedded in the pursuit
2. **Narrative** — social/emotive first; referential function "subordinate" (he cites Ogden–Richards Ch. X)
3. **Ritual / magic** — words as handles with power over things (genetic [[Concepts/Word Magic (Ogden-Richards)|word-magic]])
4. **Phatic communion** — "a type of speech in which ties of union are created by a mere exchange of words" (p. 315). Gossip, greetings. "Are words in Phatic Communion used primarily to convey meaning…? Certainly not!"

**The triangle, genetically.** For developed/scientific/literary speech he keeps the [[Concepts/Triangle of Reference (Ogden-Richards)|triangle]]. In early stages "the triangle is reduced to its base, which stands for a *real* connection… SOUND-REACTION and SITUATION." The dotted base Ogden–Richards call a fallacy is, genetically, a surviving primitive bond.

**Grammar.** Neither logic nor autonomous. "Real categories" (crude substance / *protousia*, action, speaker–hearer, possession, space) from the pragmatic *Weltanschauung* mould noun, verb, pronoun, cases. Root-shift into the noun-box yields hypostatized "Goodness."

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/C. K. Ogden]] / [[Thinkers/I. A. Richards]]**: they print this as a friendly supplement. It "substantiates" Ch. II and extends Ch. X. It does *not* replace the triangle for the speech the book cares most about (science, argument, definition).
- **[[Thinkers/Ludwig Wittgenstein]] / [[Concepts/Language-Games (Wittgenstein)]] / [[Concepts/Forms of Life (Wittgenstein)]]**: neighboring later ground — meaning as use in activity, greetings as a game, no essence of language. Malinowski is genetic/ethnographic and still keeps a developed-speech triangle; later Wittgenstein drops the triangle. Do not identify them.
- **[[Thinkers/A.J. Ayer]]**: phatic and ritual speech are not Ayer's concern; the symbolic/emotive cut already covers "Hurrah." Malinowski adds the *social-tie* function Ayer never names.
- **[[Thinkers/John Searle]]**: speech-act taxonomy (later, uningested here) will sort greetings and commands without Malinowski's "primitive" framing.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] He says context of situation *is* the sign-situation, then says his "context" is not identical. Folding this page into [[Concepts/Contextual Theory of Reference (Ogden-Richards)]] would bury the two coinages and the action/phatic split.
- > [!warning] Period language ("savage," "primitive") is load-bearing in his genetic story. Quote it; do not upgrade it into a completed theory of all non-literate speech.
