# Functions of Conscious Broadcasting (Dehaene)

> Unconscious processors are fast parallel statisticians. Consciousness samples one brief, holds it, and routes it — a biological Turing machine, and in humans a social clipboard.

## Summary

Ch. 2 maps what consciousness is *not* needed for, so Ch. 3 can say what it is for. The "zombie theory" that consciousness is a backseat driver (Velmans, Nørretranders's "user illusion") is the target. William James is enlisted: consciousness is "efficacious."

### Via negativa (Ch. 2)

"Delineating the contours of the unconscious will progressively print a negative photograph of the conscious mind." Invisible stimuli travel far: case-invariant letter identity, letter-order binding, expert chess chunks, McGurk fusion, cocktail-party name, N400 semantics (even *not/very happy/sad*), amygdala to threat words, number magnitude, coin value, Iowa-gambling pre-hunch, approximate means, sleep statistical learning, subliminal stop-signals, unaware saccade-error ACC. "Virtually all the brain's regions can participate in both conscious and unconscious thought."

Freud is denied priority (Gauchet: the claim that pre-psychoanalysis identified mind with consciousness is "rigorously false"). Helmholtz/Alhazen unconscious inference is the real lineage: "our brain acts as a clever sleuth that ponders all the separate pieces of sensory information we receive, weighs them according to their reliability, and binds them into a coherent whole" — PEM-adjacent without the label.

Limits: de novo sentence binding under anesthesia; context-selection of one meaning of *bank*; chained arithmetic; Poincaré-level proof without conscious initiation and verification. "The unconscious mind proposes while the conscious mind selects."

### Positive functions (Ch. 3)

1. **Sample, don't average.** Unconscious processes keep the probability distribution; consciousness delivers one "sample" — a simplified, disambiguated brief. "Unconscious processes act as fast and massively parallel statisticians, while consciousness is a slow sampler." Rivalry stops when attention is withdrawn.
2. **Lasting thoughts.** Subliminal traces decay (~1 s). Naccache: "the unconscious is not structured as a language but as a decaying exponential." Only access supports working memory, Edelman's "remembered present," and trace (vs coincidence) conditioning.
3. **Router / biological Turing machine.** Collect → synthesize → broadcast a conscious symbol to arbitrarily chosen routines → repeat. Single routines (name, *n*+2, *n*>5) can run subliminally; **chained** steps cannot (Sackur). "With its flexible routing capacity, the conscious brain operates as a biological Turing machine." Not: the whole brain *is* a classical computer. The serial TM models only the operations *accessible to a conscious mind*.
4. **Social sharing.** Nietzsche's epigraph: consciousness as a connecting network between man and man. Language lets the synthesis "jump mind to mind." Confidence tags travel with the brief (Bahrami: two heads plus confidence ≈ near-optimal fusion). Self-knowledge uses the same database — "oneself as another" (Ricoeur). Cost: the virtual-reality simulator that makes lying and literature possible.

These are the functional replies to Descartes's [[Concepts/The Two Tests and the Animal-Machine (Descartes)|two tests]], which Ch. 3 does not restate: report/share answers the language test; flexible non-reflexive chaining answers the universal-reason test.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Alan Turing]]**: Dehaene's "human Turing machine" is serial *conscious* thought, not the whole discrete-state machine. No Imitation Game in this chapter; Ch. 7's conscious-machine blueprint is the later step.
- **[[Thinkers/Daniel Dennett]]**: lasting thoughts, fame, Joycean serial virtual machine on parallel hardware — strong convergence. Dehaene adds a specific anatomical router.
- **[[Thinkers/Andy Clark]] / [[Thinkers/Jakob Hohwy]]**: the sleuth/reliability weighting is their PEM; they disagree with each other on seclusion and the hard problem, not with this functional split.
- **Epiphenomenalists** (Velmans; philosophical [[Concepts/Epiphenomenal Qualia (Jackson)|qualia]] in a different register): rejected. Consciousness compresses, holds, routes, and shares — those are jobs.
