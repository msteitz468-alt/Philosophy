# Natural Drift (Varela)
> Evolution as natural drift is the biological counterpart of cognition as embodied action: not optimal fit to a pregiven environment, but a viable history of structural coupling that lays down a path in walking.

## Summary

Chapter 9 of [[Sources/The Embodied Mind - Varela Thompson and Rosch (2016)|*The Embodied Mind*]]. Neo-Darwinism is to evolutionary theory "what cognitivism is to cognitive science." Adaptation is the centerpiece as representation is for cognition. "Representationism in cognitive science is the precise homologue of adaptationism in evolutionary theory."

The critique is empirical, not a logical refutation. Adaptationism is "as plausible as any other scientific enterprise." Limits they list: linkage and pleiotropy (genome as "highly interwoven network"); development (Drosophila blastoderm as a finite "epigenetic code"; homeotic mutants; intrinsic self-organization); random genetic drift in the population-genetic sense (hitchhiking, sampling error, ~40% "junk" DNA — **not** their "natural drift"); stasis; multiple units of selection. Lewontin: diversions from "the ascent of Mount Fitness." Gould & Lewontin "spandrels of San Marco" / Panglossian paradigm are in the notes.

The shift: **prescriptive** selection (pick the optimal design) → **proscriptive** selection ("discards what is not compatible with survival and reproduction"); optimizing → satisficing / bricolage / viability. "Much of what an organism looks like and is 'about' is completely underdetermined by the constraints of survival and reproduction." Survival and reproduction constraints are "far too weak" for a global optimal-fitness scheme.

**Natural drift** in four points:

1. The unit is a network capable of a rich repertoire of self-organizing configurations.
2. Structural coupling generates selection as an ongoing satisficing that "triggers (but does not specify)" change in the form of viable trajectories.
3. The trajectory is the interwoven, nonoptimal result of multiple levels.
4. Organism and medium mutually specify each other. Lewontin: "Just as there is no organism without an environment, so there is no environment without an organism."

Origin note: they "expand and modify" Maturana & Varela, *The Tree of Knowledge*.

Color spaces and bee–flower UV coevolution are incommensurable enacted histories of drift, not optimal adaptations. Gibson's affordances ("always there"; "Invariance comes from reality") are refused as independent invariants.

The slogan: "paths that exist only as they are laid down in walking." "We are always constrained by the path we have laid down, but there is no ultimate ground to prescribe the steps that we take."

**Brooks** is the AI exhibit. "Intelligence without Representation"; complete robots in the real world; "It turns out to be better to use the world as its own model." "Representation is the wrong unit of abstraction in building the bulkiest parts of intelligent systems." Decomposition by activity/layers; no central system; no world-model at a single place. Contrasted with Smolensky/harmony (optimal encoding of a pregiven environment). Dreyfus and Chapman are **not** named in this chapter; Heidegger appears only in a note via Winograd & Flores.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Francisco J. Varela]] et al.**: completes [[Concepts/Enaction (Varela)|enaction]]. Formal answers: cognition = that history; via multilevel sensorimotor subnetworks; adequate when it enters or shapes a world. Intelligence = "enter into a shared world of significance."
- **Lewontin / Gould / Oyama**: named biological allies. Not ingested.
- **[[Thinkers/Hubert Dreyfus]]**: 1992 already quotes Chapman, "the world is its own best representation," and names Heideggerian AI (Winograd, Agre–Chapman). This chapter supplies Brooks as the enactive robot. Same slogan, different bibliography.
- **[[Thinkers/Marvin Minsky]]**: "societies of agents" analogized once — weak constraints, many viable designs.
- **[[Thinkers/Andy Clark]]**: not named. Later productive laziness / "use the world as its own best model" is a neighbor. Do not write Clark into this page's 1991 claims.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- Whether proscriptive viability plus self-organization still needs a notion of function or information is left to later enactivism (Di Paolo, Thompson 2016).
- No claim here about the evolution of consciousness.
