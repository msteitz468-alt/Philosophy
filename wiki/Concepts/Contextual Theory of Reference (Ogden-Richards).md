# Contextual Theory of Reference (Ogden-Richards)

> Thinking is not a unique mind–object relation. A thought is "of" a flame when it is similar, in the relevant respects, to thoughts that have been caused by flame — once "cause" is unpacked as the **recurrence of a context**.

## Summary

Chs. III–IV of *The Meaning of Meaning* give the positive theory that Chs. I–II only promised. Meaning depends on a theory of signs. The rival view — Keynes's acquaintance with propositions, Husserl's *Urteil*, Meinong, van Ginneken's "adhesion" — treats thinking as a unique tie to a timeless entity. That "rules out" a scientific account.

**Sign.** "A sign is always a stimulus similar to some part of an original stimulus and sufficient to call up the engram." Interpretation is typical: when a context has affected us, recurrence of *part* of the context causes us to react as before. Match-scrape → flame-expectation; Lloyd Morgan's chicken; the dinner-gong dog. Mnemic causation is taken from Semon (*Die Mneme*); the account is deliberately neutral on mind–body options.

**Context** (technical, not literary surround): a set of entities sharing constitutive characters and a uniting relation, occurring "*nearly* uniformly." External contexts vs psychological contexts (recurrent mental events, often widely separated in time). A context may be determinative in respect of one or more characters.

**Reference** = being adapted to. "Thinking of A" = being caused by A, with cause expanded as contextual recurrence.

**Truth.** If an event completes the external context the psychological context points to, the reference is true and that event is its referent; if not, false. "When the problem of reference is settled that of truth is found to be solved as well." Raising "correspondence of belief with fact" solves the problem twice. Negative facts and Russell's two "modes of reference" (true/false objective reference) are rejected. Appendix E: a fact is "a referent which belongs to the order to which it is allocated"; negation reallocates order; rival locutions are "rival prose styles."

**Images** are "mental luxuries," not the basis of meaning. They may be absent; they are accepted or rejected by a prior reference, not by pictorial matching. This is the main split from Russell's mnemonic account.

**Propositions** are "relational characters of acts of referring," not objects of acquaintance. The proposition-as-entity is "a linguistic fiction foisted upon us by the utraquistic subterfuge."

**False and general reference.** A false belief has a referent only by courtesy: analyse into true simple references; the compound's "referent" is the set of the scattered referents. Inclusive vs non-inclusive ("all"/"some") is learned from sets vs turn-taking. Universals are "phantom" denizens.

**Perception** (Ch. IV) is interpretation of neural signs. Helmholtz: "A sign need have no kind of similarity whatever with what it signifies." Direct apprehension is a modification of a sense organ plus further neural modification — not a unique knowing-relation. "By no manner of make-believe can we discover the *what* of referents. We can only discover the *how*." Seeing is a hierarchy of orders of signs (retinal colour → surfaces → chair → … → electrons). Bent-stick and florin-ellipse paradoxes are bad symbolization.

**Probability** is shorthand for those features of a context on which its degree of uniformity depends.

**Ch. IX's accepted sense.** Definition XIII: "the meaning of A is that to which the mental process interpreting A is adapted. This is the most important sense in which words have meaning." For *true* interpretations, natural-sign meaning (XII) and XIII(b) coincide — which "removes the necessity for any Correspondence Theory of Truth."

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Bertrand Russell]] / [[Concepts/Correspondence Theory of Truth (Russell)]]**: Russell's 1912 theory makes truth the correspondence of a belief to a *fact*. O&R relocate: the referent of an adequate reference *is* the fact, not something that corresponds to it. They prefer his later mnemic psychology to *Principles* denoting, and still deny acquaintance with propositions and images-as-meaning.
- **[[Thinkers/Edmund Husserl]]**: *Urteil* / ideal *Bedeutung* as spaceless object of logic is the same unique-relation error. Appendix D exhibits the Word / Meaning / Object triad as phenomenological hypostatization.
- **[[Thinkers/William James]] / [[Concepts/Pragmatic Method (James)]]**: listed as definition IX (practical consequences), not as the theory of reference. Contextual adaptation is not "what works."
- **[[Thinkers/Bronislaw Malinowski]] / [[Concepts/Context of Situation and Phatic Communion (Malinowski)]]**: he *equates* context of situation with this sign-situation, then marks non-identity. His "context" is ethnographic and behavioural; this one is psychological/mnemic.
- **Helmholtz**: the named ancestor for perception-as-signs. (The vault's later Helmholtz, via Clark/Seth/Hohwy, is predictive; this one is not.)

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] Conflicts with [[Concepts/Correspondence Theory of Truth (Russell)]] on whether truth is a further relation of belief to fact. O&R: that question "solves the problem twice."
- > [!warning] Conflicts with [[Concepts/Knowledge by Acquaintance and Knowledge by Description (Russell)]] on whether there is a unique, inference-free knowing-relation. Perception here is already interpretation.
- > [!warning] Pre-Quinean: each reference still has *a* completing context. [[Concepts/Confirmation Holism (Duhem-Quine Thesis)|Holism]] is not yet in view.
