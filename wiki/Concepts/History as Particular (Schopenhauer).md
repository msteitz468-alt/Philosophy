# History as Particular (Schopenhauer)
> History is not a science. Sciences speak of what always is; history of what is once and no more. Motto: *Eadem, sed aliter.*

## Summary

Chapter XXXVIII of *WWR* Volume 2 is the vault’s primary. History “lacks the fundamental characteristic of science, the subordination of what is known; instead of this it boasts of the mere co-ordination of what is known.” “A science of individual things… implies a contradiction.”

Philosophy sees that “at all times exactly the same was, is, and will be.” History teaches that at each time something different has been. The motto of history should run: *Eadem, sed aliter* — the same, but otherwise.

Hegelian “organic” construction of world-history is “crude and shallow *realism*” (phenomenon taken as being-in-itself), secretly mythological, optimistic/eudaemonist, and un-Christian (it treats earthly happiness as if it were the point). Do not import “cunning of reason”; he does not use that phrase.

Residual dignity: “*What the faculty of reason is to the individual, history is to the human race*” — the race’s reflected self-consciousness. A nation without its history is like an animal confined to the present.

This is not Popper’s critique of historicism (fallibilism, open society). Shared hostility to laws of historical development; different ground (Platonic Ideas; time ideal; will as thing-in-itself). See [[Concepts/Critique of Historicism and Dialectic (Popper)]].

## Key Claims

- Science = subordination under universals. History = co-ordination of particulars. Hence not a science.
- The same will plays the same play under new costumes.
- Hegelian world-history-as-plan is realism about the phenomenon.
- History still has a use: it is the race’s reason.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Arthur Schopenhauer]]** — Book III’s claim that poetry knows mankind better than history, now argued as a theory of science.
- **[[Thinkers/Hegel]]** — named “Hegelian pseudo-philosophy.” Reception, not a reading of the *Philosophy of History*. See [[Sources/Philosophy of History - Hegel (1837)]].
- **[[Thinkers/Karl Popper]]** — later kinship only. Do not weld.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] Do not file this as a denial that anything happened. The claim is about *what kind of knowledge* history is.
