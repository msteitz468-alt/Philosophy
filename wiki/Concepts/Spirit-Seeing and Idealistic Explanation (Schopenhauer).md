# Spirit-Seeing and Idealistic Explanation (Schopenhauer)
> Animal magnetism and clairvoyance are treated as facts. There are no spirits. The explanation is *idealistic*, not spiritualistic: the will beyond space and time.

## Summary

The “Essay on spirit-seeing and related issues” applies the system of *WWR* sideways to apparitions, magnetic sleep, and second sight. Empirical proofs against “spirits” only show that no *body* acted on retina or eardrum. A spirit-apparition is an intellect-image indistinguishable from bodily appearance, not a body. Spiritualistic explanations fall under Kant’s *Dreams of a Spirit-seer*. Schopenhauer attempts an *idealistic* one.

Animal magnetism and the clairvoyance tied to it are treated as **facts**: “Whoever nowadays doubts [them] … should not be called incredulous but ignorant.” External intuition is not merely sensuous but *intellectual/cerebral*. The brain can process an *internal* stimulus with the same a priori causality, space, and time, so the product is indistinguishable from sense-based intuition.

The **dream** is the primary phenomenon: a faculty of space-filling, sense-like intuition without outer excitation. He names this the **dream-organ** (Scottish “second sight” would name the whole genus). **Truth-dreaming** / sleep-waking: one can dream the actual surroundings with eyes shut. Somnambulism and magnetic sleep are the same organ, not physical vision. The magnetizer’s *will* acts; the body is “nothing but the image of the will generated in the brain.”

Magnetic sleep = intensified natural sleep; clairvoyance = controlled, continuous truth-dreaming; magnetic healing = intensified healing power of sleep. Clairvoyance exists for self-prescription; other uses are “misuse.”

Clairvoyance and magic (action at a distance) violate space, time, and causality as *brain-forms*, not as determinations of the thing in itself. That is why they look “impossible” physically and are intelligible only metaphysically. “Clairvoyance is a confirmation of the Kantian doctrine of the ideality of space, time, and causality; moreover, magic also confirms my doctrine of the sole reality of the *will* as the core of all things.” It is “the path that does not pass through time and space led by the string of causality. It is the path through the thing in itself.” See [[Concepts/The Will (Schopenhauer)]], [[Concepts/Transcendental Idealism (Kant)]].

Magnetism, sympathetic cures, magic, second sight, truth-dreaming, spirit-seeing are “branches of one stem”: a deeper nexus in which individuation (space/time) does not isolate wills. Immediate cause of waking dream-organ images is always *inside* the organism; if the remoter cause is also only internal → **hallucination**; if external (non-spatial, via the thing in itself) → vision / “magical” communication.

The fundamental error of both believers and deniers: assuming a locomotive immaterial *soul* present like a body (**spiritualism**). Apparitions are dream-organ products. Living (especially dying) wills can induce another’s brain-image; whether the *deceased* can is not denied a priori (will is not annihilated) but is heavily blocked: intellect is a bodily function and perishes. “A spirit apparition is, at first and immediately, nothing but a vision in the brain of the spirit-seer.”

## Key Claims

- Facts of magnetism/clairvoyance are granted. Spirits as locomotive immaterial substances are denied.
- The dream-organ explains sense-like intuition without outer excitation.
- Will is outside the principle of individuation; boundaries of space/time do not isolate it.
- Hallucination vs vision: remoter cause internal vs via the thing in itself.
- Not a new mind–body dualism. The body is the brain-image of the same will as in *WWR*.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Arthur Schopenhauer]]** — application of idealism to a mid-century craze. The essay is speculative in the same register as transcendent fatalism, but here the *facts* are insisted on.
- **[[Concepts/Dreams as Residual Sense-Movements (Aristotle)]]** — **flag only**. Aristotle is cited (*On Sleep and Waking* 2; *On Dreams* III, residual/hypnopompic figures). Do not rewrite that page from this essay.
- **[[Concepts/Transcendent Fatalism (Schopenhauer)]]** — same will beyond representing consciousness; dream as the analogy for fate there, as the organ of vision here.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] Do not file this as occultism or as a new soul-doctrine. He **denies spirits**. Post-death agency is left open only as a strained, exceptional possibility, not asserted.
- > [!warning] “Whoever doubts… is ignorant” is a period claim about animal magnetism, not a vault fact. Record it as *his* stance.
