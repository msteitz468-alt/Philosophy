# Word Magic (Ogden-Richards)

> The conservative force of language: treating the name as a power, a soul, or a direct stand-in for the thing — filling in the base of the [[Concepts/Triangle of Reference (Ogden-Richards)|triangle]].

## Summary

Ch. II of *The Meaning of Meaning* is a history of the habit the triangle was drawn to break. "The power of words is the most conservative force in our life." Language carries a "common inherited scheme of conception" (they quote Cornford). In the days before psychological analysis, the evidence for *nomina* as *numina* — names as occult powers — must have appeared overwhelming.

**Archaic forms**: the name as part of the soul; secret names; taboos on naming; onomancy; Egyptian Name-soul; spells as verbal medicine. The East is "the true home of verbal superstition," but Greece is where it became philosophy.

**Greek crystallization**:
- To classify is to name; the name is the soul; Logos as Meaning/essence.
- [[Thinkers/Plato]]'s Ideas are **name-souls** deposited in a World of Being (*Symposium* 211, *Phaedo* 80). The *Cratylus* — the one dialogue that takes the name-thing problem straight — is neglected.
- [[Thinkers/Aristotle]] "consummates" the verbal method: the Categories track Greek grammar (Trendelenburg, Gomperz, Mauthner: a different mother-tongue, a different logic). Yet *De Interpretatione* says significant speech is significant "by convention only" — a tension they flag and do not resolve.
- Aenesidemus and the Sceptics (Appendix C) are the ancient exception: a scientific approach to signs.

**Modern ravages**: journalism and cliché; Hegelian verbal machinery; theological "dictionary adjectives"; Rignano's "verbal carapace" (the word-husk that outlives content and props emotion); Coué; logicians as mystics. Ingraham's "gostak distims the wousin" shows how far verbal scepticism can go; the exit is not nihilism but a [[Concepts/Contextual Theory of Reference (Ogden-Richards)|contextual]] account of how symbols gain power by occurring *with* things.

**Russell as neo-Platonist.** The *Principles of Mathematics* "term" is immutable and includes man, chimera, and relation. That reconstructs the World of Being on a "purely linguistic basis." [[Concepts/Canons of Symbolism (Ogden-Richards)|Canon III]] (Expansion) is the solvent: universals and classes are symbolic conveniences; "The World of Pure Being will then be definitively denuded of its quondam denizens."

**Medical word-magic** (Crookshank, Supplement II): "a disease" is a general reference symbolized by a name, not a morbid entity *in rebus Natura*. Diagnosis too often means "the formal and unctuous pronunciation of a Name." Official labels (P.U.O., N.Y.D., encephalitis lethargica vs poliomyelitis) create entities and fake statistics.

**Genetic account** ([[Thinkers/Bronislaw Malinowski]]): infantile meaning is pragmatic efficiency — the name *summons* the mother/object. Ritual speech treats words as handles. The dotted base of the triangle is a surviving primitive *real* bond.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Plato]]**: O&R's most aggressive historical claim. Ideas = name-souls. Sits beside, and does not replace, [[Concepts/Critique of Platonic Forms (Aristotle)]].
- **[[Thinkers/Bertrand Russell]]**: the 1903/1912 world of universals is filed as word-magic. His later psychological account (*Analysis of Mind*) is treated more kindly and still not adopted.
- **[[Thinkers/A.J. Ayer]] / [[Concepts/Metaphysics as Meaningless (Ayer)]]**: Ayer condemns metaphysics as unverifiable. O&R condemn it as *filled-in base* — a different diagnosis of the same patients (Being, substance, the Absolute).
- **[[Thinkers/Ludwig Wittgenstein]] / [[Concepts/Philosophy as Therapy (Wittgenstein)]]**: neighboring aim (deflate verbal bewitchment) by a different method. O&R offer a *science* of symbolism and canons; later Wittgenstein offers case-by-case therapy and no criterion-weapon.
- **Locke on "nomads"**: Ch. VI quotes Locke on words without "determined ideas" as one of four dangerous-word classes (Irritants, Degenerates, Mendicants, Nomads).

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] "Platonism as primitive word-magic" is a genetic reduction. It does not engage the arguments of the *Republic* or *Phaedo* on their own terms. Flag, do not treat as a refutation of Forms.
- > [!warning] The Aristotle tension is internal: convention-theory in *De Interpretatione* vs Categories-as-grammar. They leave it standing.
