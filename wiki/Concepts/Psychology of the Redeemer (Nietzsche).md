# Psychology of the Redeemer (Nietzsche)
> Not a hero, not a genius, not a founder of a church. A physiological type: resist not evil; the kingdom is a state of the heart.

## Summary

*The Antichrist* §§28–35 is the vault’s first primary for Nietzsche’s Jesus. It is a *type*, not a biography and not a thinker page.

Against Renan: the Saviour is neither “genius” nor “hero.” “If there is anything essentially unevangelical, it is surely the concept of the hero.” The key is “resist not evil!” Two physiologies: an instinctive hatred of reality, and the exclusion of all hostility, both from extreme susceptibility to pain. Result: “a religion of love,” a “sublime super-development of hedonism.” He “speaks only of inner things.” The type reaches us distorted by milieu, disciples, and propaganda. Against Renan’s *impérieux* fanatic: “recrudescent childishness of the spirit”; tentatively a “free spirit” and “anti-realist.”

Mencken does **not** title him “the idiot.” He writes of the Gospel-world’s “‘childish’ idiocy” (§31) and wishes for the missing psychologist: “It is greatly to be regretted that no Dostoyevsky lived in the neighbourhood of this most interesting décadent.” Kaufmann’s later “idiot” (in Dostoevsky’s sense) is reception relative to this English. Quote Mencken; do not back-correct.

§§33–35: the Gospels, read as this type, lack guilt, punishment, and reward; “sin” is abolished. Not a new *faith* but a “way of life.” He died not to “save mankind” but “to show mankind how to live”: non-resistance, even love of “the Evil One.” “The ‘kingdom of heaven’ is a state of the heart—not something to come ‘beyond the world’ or ‘after death.’” “The Kingdom of God is within you.”

§27 already prepares the split: the Gospel Jesus is a “saintly anarchist” / “political criminal” who “died for his *own* sins,” not others’. §20–23 give the Buddhist contrast that makes the type legible: both religions are nihilistic *décadence* religions, but Buddhism is “a hundred times as realistic,” a struggle with *suffering* not *sin*; “egoism is a duty”; prayer and asceticism are not included. Europe is “not yet ripe” for Buddhism. The type is a Buddhist-like inwardness that Christianity will not remain.

Do **not** create a Jesus thinker page from this polemic.

## Key Claims

- The Redeemer is a physiological *décadent*, not a hero, genius, or fanatic.
- The evangel is a way of life (non-resistance, inner kingdom), not a faith, a sacrifice, or a future judgment.
- Dostoevsky is named as the missing psychologist of the type.
- Buddhism is the honest *décadence* religion; Christianity moralizes the same fatigue as sin.
- The church’s God-as-person, coming kingdom, Trinity, second coming, last judgment are “un-Christian” overlays on this type.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Nietzsche]]** — 1888: the middle of Book I, so that Paul can be a *falsifier* rather than a successor. *Genealogy* I’s “Redeemer as crown of Jewish hate” is the *bait* of revenge, not this physiology. Keep both.
- **[[Concepts/Paul as Falsifier of the Evangel (Nietzsche)]]** — the next movement: only one Christian, and he died on the cross; Paul nails him to *his* cross.
- **[[Concepts/Decadence (Nietzsche)]]** — *CW* gave the style-formula and the Wagner case. Here both Buddhism and the Jesus-type are *décadence* religions / types; Jews, by contrast, *use* décadence and are “the very opposite of *décadents*” (§24).
- **[[Concepts/Ascetic Ideal (Nietzsche)]]** — Buddhism: “Prayer is not included, and neither is asceticism” (§20). Christian self-cruelty and body-hatred are the *other* décadence.
- **[[Thinkers/Søren Kierkegaard]]** — Abraham / knight of faith is not this type. Do not file the Redeemer as a teleological suspension.
- **[[Thinkers/Pierre Hadot]]** — Christianity as a philosophy / way of life is a different claim (discourse *about* vs lived *askēsis*). Nietzsche’s “way of life” here is non-resistance of a pain-type, not Hadot’s spiritual exercises.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] *Genealogy* I’s Jewish/Christian slave revolt and this book’s “Jews are the very opposite of *décadents*” are not the same sentence. Here the priest *stages* décadence as a means. Flag, do not silently reconcile.
- > [!warning] Early Wagnerism and the uningested *Birth of Tragedy* are not this Jesus. Do not read Dionysus-vs-the-Crucified (*Ecce Homo*, still outstanding as a whole) back into §§28–35.
