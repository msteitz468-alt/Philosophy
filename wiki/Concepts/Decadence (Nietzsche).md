# Decadence (Nietzsche)
> Good and evil are only a playful subdivision of decline. Nietzsche includes himself.

## Summary

In the Preface to *The Case of Wagner* (1888) Nietzsche names *decadence* as his “greatest preoccupation hitherto.” The word is not a slur attached to late Wagner after the fact. It is the master problem of which the revaluation’s moral vocabulary is a part: “‘Good and evil’ form only a playful subdivision of this problem. If one has trained one’s eye to detect the symptoms of decline, one also understands morality, — one understands what lies concealed beneath its holiest names and tables of values: *e.g.*, *impoverished* life, the will to nonentity, great exhaustion. Morality *denies* life.”

He does not exempt himself. “I am just as much a child of my age as Wagner — *i.e.*, I am a decadent. The only difference is that I recognised the fact, that I struggled against it. The philosopher in me struggled against it.” The mission required siding against all that was morbid in himself, “including Wagner, including Schopenhauer, including the whole of modern *humanity*.” Recovery, not a theoretical refutation, is the event: “The greatest event of my life took the form of a *recovery*. Wagner belongs only to my diseases.”

§7 gives the **formula of every decadent style**: “life no longer animates the whole.” Words leap out of the sentence, sentences out of the page, the page at the cost of the whole. “There is always anarchy among the atoms, disaggregation of the will, — in moral terms: ‘freedom of the individual,’ — extended into a political theory ‘*equal* rights for all.’” Life is driven back into the smallest structure; the remainder is almost lifeless. Paralysis, distress, numbness, or hostility and chaos, increasing as one ascends the forms of organisation. “The whole no longer lives at all: it is composed, reckoned up, artificial, a fictitious thing.”

Wagner is the complete case, not a defective genius: “every sign of ‘free will’ was lacking,” every feature necessary. He sets up a principle wherever capacity fails him (the inverse of Kant, who invents a faculty when a principle fails). He is a master of small things — the musical *miniaturist* — and disguises the inability to create organic form as a “dramatic style.” Second Postscript: decay is universal; Wagner did not cause the ruin of music any more than Bernini caused the ruin of sculpture; he accelerated the fall and had the *ingenuousness* of decadence — he believed in it and did not halt before any logical consequence. Half-measures cannot stand beside completeness. Brahms is the melancholy of impotence, not an antagonist.

Epilogue: an age has the virtues of *ascending* life or of degeneration. Aesthetic is “inextricably bound up with these biological principles”: decadent aesthetic and *classical* aesthetic. “‘Beauty in itself’ is just as much a chimera as any other kind of idealism.” Christian morality grows on morbid soil; master-morality is the speech of well-constitutedness. One cannot refute a diseased eyesight. Modern man is a *contradiction of values* — yea and nay in one breath. That innocence in contradiction *is* modernity.

## Key Claims

- Decadence is the genus; “good and evil” is a species.
- Recognition + struggle, not exemption, distinguishes the philosopher from the typical *décadent*.
- Style, nerves, and politics share one formula: the whole no longer lives.
- Completeness in corruption is more instructive than half-measures (Wagner vs “the others”).
- Recovery is an event in a life, not an argument.

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Nietzsche]]** — 1888: the problem that organises the late work. Already approached as romantic *impoverishment* vs Dionysian over-fullness in *Gay Science* §370, and as the ascetic ideal’s “will to nothingness” in *Genealogy* III. Here the word is technical. *Twilight*, Problem of Socrates 11, gives the *instinct* formula: "To have to fight the instincts — that is the definition of decadence: as long as life is ascending, happiness equals instinct." Same year, different case: Socrates as physician who was only sick, not Wagner as complete *décadent*. See [[Sources/Twilight of the Idols - Nietzsche (Kaufmann)]]. Notebooks **n.43.4**: "Nihilism is no cause but merely the logical result of decadence." Do not reverse the arrow. See [[Concepts/European Nihilism (Nietzsche)]]. *The Antichrist* is the **Christian case**: both Buddhism and Christianity are *décadence* religions (§20); “rottenness” is décadence, not a moral accusation (§6); preponderance of pains over pleasures is “the formula for *décadence*” (§15). Jews are “the very opposite of *décadents*” and *use* décadence as a means (§24). See [[Sources/The Antichrist - Nietzsche (Mencken, Collection 2013)]], [[Concepts/Psychology of the Redeemer (Nietzsche)]].
- ***Ecce Homo* (1888) — the word turned on the philosopher's own trade.** Three uses worth keeping. (1) "Why I am So Clever" §8 extends the diagnosis to intellectual work itself: the instinct of self-preservation shows as an instinct of *defence*, habitual saying-of-no is ruinous expenditure, and a purely reacting medium is therefore diseased — "**The scholar is a decadent.**" (2) The *Birth of Tragedy* self-review supplies the 1888 verdict on the 1872 book's real find: Socrates "presented for the first time as the instrument of Hellenic dissolution, as a **typical decadent**," with the *Untimely Essays* review adding that "the 'historical sense' of which this century is so proud is for the first time recognized as sickness, as a typical symptom of decay." (3) "Why I am a Destiny" §7 gives the compressed definition the late work runs on: "Morality is the idiosyncrasy of decadents having the hidden desire to revenge themselves upon life – and being successful." See [[Sources/Ecce Homo - Nietzsche (Collection 2013)]], [[Concepts/The Small Things - Nutrition Place Climate Recreation (Nietzsche)]], [[Concepts/The Immoralist (Nietzsche)]].
- ***Nietzsche Contra Wagner* (1888/89 compilation).** “We Antipodes” is §370 selected as a Wagner chapter: two sufferers (overflow vs reduced vitality); Wagner and Schopenhauer deny life; “Disinterestedness”—principle of decadence, the will to nonentity. He admits he bestowed *himself* on them. Not a new theory — a dossier. See [[Sources/Nietzsche Contra Wagner - Nietzsche (Ludovici, Collection 2013)]], [[Concepts/Aesthetic as Applied Physiology (Nietzsche)]].
- **[[Thinkers/Arthur Schopenhauer]]** — named “the *philosopher of decadence*” who allowed “the *artist of decadence*” to find himself (*CW* §4). The *Ring*’s wreck on the fourth book of *The World as Will and Idea* is the conversion. See [[Concepts/The Will (Schopenhauer)]].
- **[[Concepts/Ascetic Ideal (Nietzsche)]]** — *Genealogy*: man will sooner will nothingness than not will. *CW*: that will is now read as a symptom of decline; *Parsifal* is its most seductive artwork, not a riddle about whether Wagner is joking.
- **[[Concepts/The Great Health (Nietzsche)]]** — the 1887 Preface’s philosophical physician and §382’s health one acquires by giving it up. *CW* is the case-history: Wagner belongs only to the diseases; *Carmen* and the south are the convalescent climate (*il faut méditerraniser la musique*).
- **[[Concepts/Master Morality and Slave Morality (Nietzsche)]]** — *Genealogy* I is historical-psychological; the *CW* Epilogue restates the pair as *optics* that arguments cannot refute.
- **[[Thinkers/William James]]** — *Varieties* already answers Nietzsche on saintliness without this word. Do not file James as a theorist of *décadence*.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] Early Wagnerism is now primary (*The Birth of Tragedy*, 1872). That book does **not** use *décadence*. It hopes German music (Bach–Beethoven–Wagner) will rebirth tragedy. This 1888 page is the later diagnosis of the same artist. Do not close the tension by rewriting 1872 as already-decadent. See [[Sources/The Birth of Tragedy - Nietzsche (Kaufmann, Collection 2013)]], [[Concepts/Tragedy from the Spirit of Music (Nietzsche)]].
- > [!warning] *Décadence* here is not a period style-label (Huysmans, the 1880s “Decadents”) and not a bio-political hygiene norm. It is a criterion for works, virtues, and ages: do they come from plenitude or from exhausted life? Do not feed it to [[Concepts/Biopolitics and Governmentality (Foucault)]].
