# On Women (Schopenhauer)
> Chapter XXVII is a primary rant, not a metaphysics. Record it. Do not sanitize it. Do not present it as a vault fact about women.

## Summary

“On Women” (*Parerga* II, Ch. XXVII) is the essay Janaway flagged from Volume 1’s introduction. It is here. It is metaphilosophically thin: no new will/representation machinery. The systematic hook he points to is *WWR* II ch. 44 (species-sexuality). The **sexual-honour / *esprit de corps*** argument — female front, sex only in exchange for provision, marriage as male “surrender” — is **not** this chapter. It is Aphorisms Ch. IV: [[Concepts/What One Is, Has, and Represents (Schopenhauer)]]. File this page as **reception / historiography**, not as natural history. Do not let 2026 videos weld the two essays into one “prophecy.”

Schopenhauer claims: woman is “not destined for great work, either intellectual or physical”; she pays “the debt” by childbirth, child-care, and “submissiveness to her husband.” She is a lifelong “grown-up child,” an “intermediate stage between the child and the man.” Nature’s “stage-effect” is a few years of beauty to trap a man into lifelong provision. Women “see only what is nearest”; they are “intellectual myope[s].” They have compassion (the “first” of virtue) but lack justice — “injustice is the fundamental failing of the female character”; “dissimulation is… inborn.” “Women exist solely for the propagation of the race.” They are “*sexus sequior*, the sex that takes second place in every respect.” The European “lady” is a *fausse position* — “Christian-Germanic stupidity.” Monogamy “halve[s] one’s rights and double[s] one’s duties”; surplus women become “human sacrifices on the altar of monogamy.”

**This is his rant.** It is not a vault claim. Attribute every sentence. Do not omit the chapter: it is how the popular *Parerga* taught a century to read him, and how later readers (including Nietzsche’s “sick thinkers” typology) had a text to react to.

The neighboring short essays must not be swamped:

- **Education** (XXVIII): concepts should arise by abstraction from intuitive perceptions. “Artificial education” crams concepts first. Purpose: acquaintance with the world. See [[Concepts/What One Is, Has, and Represents (Schopenhauer)]].
- **Physiognomy** (XXIX): the face is a “hieroglyphic”; it “does not lie; it is we who read from it what is not there.” Only the first glance is objective.
- **Din and noise** (XXX): sensitivity to noise tracks sensitivity to ideas. The whip-crack is “the real murderer of ideas.” Character-sketch, not a new metaphysics.
- **Porcupine parable** (XXXI): society’s need for warmth vs. quills; the “mean distance” is politeness. Those with “internal warmth” withdraw.

## Key Claims (as his)

- Species over individual. Woman as instrument of the race.
- *Sexus sequior*. The “lady” should not exist.
- Compassion without justice. Dissimulation inborn.
- Record, do not endorse.
