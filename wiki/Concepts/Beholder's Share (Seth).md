# Beholder's Share (Seth)
> That part of perceptual experience contributed by the perceiver and not to be found in the artwork — or the world — itself.

## Summary

Chapter 6 of *Being You* takes Alois Riegl's and Ernst Gombrich's art-historical phrase and makes it a lab result. There is no "innocent eye" (Kandel via Gombrich). Impressionism reverse-engineers vision: the canvas under-specifies, and the perceiver's generative model completes it. That completion *is* [[Concepts/Inside-Out Perception (Seth)|inside-out perception]] made visible.

Seth's own and collaborators' experiments treat the share as measurable prior-strength:

- Expected houses and faces break flash-suppression faster (Pinto).
- Word context sharpens early visual letter codes (Heilbron / de Lange).
- Strong priors overwhelm data: LSD, pareidolia, schizophrenia.
- Keisuke Suzuki's "hallucination machine" (DeepDream VR) is excess beholder's share — computational phenomenology of uncontrolled perception.

Hallucination, on this view, is not a different kind of mental event. "What we call 'hallucination' is what happens when perceptual priors are unusually strong, overwhelming the sensory data." Ordinary seeing is the same process with the priors held in check. The [[Concepts/Controlled Hallucination (Clark)|controlled hallucination]] slogan is the general case; the beholder's share is its aesthetic and clinical display.

Objecthood, change, time, and even "reality" are further applications: objecthood as richness of counterfactual/sensorimotor predictions; change ≠ change-of-perception (Roseboom / Sherman); substitutional reality (Mariola) as inferred realness. We perceive *with and through* generative models (Metzinger: transparency). The *seeming-to-be-real* of ordinary contents is what makes the hard problem feel compulsory.

## Key Claims / Positions

- Art is not a metaphor for perception; it is perception with the top-down contribution isolated.
- Individual differences in priors are not noise: they *are* the different worlds people inhabit.
- Clinical hallucination sits on a continuum with seeing, not across a kind-boundary.

## How This Relates to Prior Thinkers

- **Gombrich / Riegl / Kandel**: the phrase and the "no innocent eye" thesis. Seth laboratory-operationalizes them.
- **[[Thinkers/Andy Clark]]**: same prior-driven construction (The Dress, Hollow Mask, Mooney, White Christmas). Seth's distinctive contribution is the art-historical name and the hallucination-machine demo.
- **O'Regan and Noë**: sensorimotor contingency as the structure of objecthood.
- **Thomas Metzinger**: transparency — we look *through* the model, not at it.
- **[[Thinkers/Hume]]**: gilding and staining, now as measurable prior strength.
