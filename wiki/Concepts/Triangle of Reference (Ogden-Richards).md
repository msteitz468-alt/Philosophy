# Triangle of Reference (Ogden-Richards)

> In any statement there are three factors — **Symbol**, **Thought/Reference**, **Referent** — and the base of the triangle is empty: words do not stand for things except *through* an act of interpretation.

## Summary

[[Thinkers/C. K. Ogden]] and [[Thinkers/I. A. Richards]] introduce the diagram in Ch. I of *The Meaning of Meaning* as the corrective to the "primitive conception" that from the presence of a name you may argue to the existence of a thing. Words "mean nothing by themselves"; they are instruments. Two of the triangle's sides are real (causal); the third is only imputed.

- **Thought/Reference ↔ Symbol**: causal. We use the word because of the thought; the word in turn organizes and communicates the reference.
- **Thought/Reference ↔ Referent**: causal, more or less direct, or a long chain of [[Concepts/Contextual Theory of Reference (Ogden-Richards)|sign-situations]] (Napoleon → historians → books → this sentence).
- **Symbol ↔ Referent**: *no relevant direct relation* — only an imputed "stands for," via someone's use. "The fundamental and most prolific fallacy is… that the base of the triangle… is filled in."

**Referent** is their technical term for "whatever we may be thinking of or referring to," chosen to avoid "thing" and "object." **Symbols** include words, arrangements of words, images, gestures, drawings, mimetic sounds.

**Exception**: when the symbol is *like* the referent (onomatopoeia, image, gesture, drawing) a real base is supplied. "Standing for and representing are different relations." That exception is how they will later refuse Wittgenstein's identification of standing-for with picturing.

Everyday "means" is dangerous shorthand for the whole triangle. Using "meaning" for both the mental goings-on and the referent locates Grantchester, influenza, and the universe "inside their heads."

## How Different Thinkers Use This

- **[[Thinkers/Locke]] / [[Concepts/Words as Signs of Ideas and Language (Locke)]]**: Locke already made the word–thing relation indirect (words signify ideas, and only secretly "the reality of things"). O&R keep the mediation and replace "idea" with a causal **reference** that can be checked from others' behaviour. They name Locke as a forerunner of the attack on verbal mystery, not as their theory.
- **F. de Saussure**: rejected in Ch. I. *La langue* is an invented "integral object"; the Saussurean sign (*signifié* + *signifiant*, both psychical) builds interpretation into the sign by definition and "neglects entirely the things for which signs stand." Semiology so defined cannot be verified.
- **[[Thinkers/Bertrand Russell]]**: the *Principles* "term" (man, chimera, relation) fills in the base — a world of Being reached from the word. Acquaintance with propositions does the same on the thought–referent side (a unique, non-causal tie to a timeless entity). See [[Concepts/Word Magic (Ogden-Richards)]].
- **[[Thinkers/Ludwig Wittgenstein]] / [[Concepts/Picture Theory of Language (Wittgenstein)]]**: the *Tractatus* fills in the base as logical isomorphism. Appendix A: that identification of "standing for" with representation is arbitrary; on a causal theory structural correspondence is "unnecessary and highly improbable."
- **[[Thinkers/C. S. Peirce]]**: a rival triangle (sign–object–interpretant), reproduced in Appendix D and not adopted. Peirce's Immediate Interpretant is what O&R would call "meaning" and then refuse to hypostatize.
- **[[Thinkers/Bronislaw Malinowski]]**: endorses the triangle for *developed* speech; genetically he collapses it to the base (sound-reaction ↔ situation) and treats that real base as the surviving primitive bond that word-magic later mystifies.
- **[[Thinkers/A.J. Ayer]]**: does not use the diagram, but the symbolic half of [[Concepts/Symbolic and Emotive Functions of Language (Ogden-Richards)|the twofold cut]] is exactly the referential side of the triangle.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- > [!warning] Conflicts with [[Concepts/Picture Theory of Language (Wittgenstein)]] on whether the symbol–world relation is isomorphism. O&R leave the base empty except in the simulative case.
- > [!warning] Malinowski's genetic diagrams *fill in* the base for infantile/primitive speech. That is a friendly amendment O&R print, not their official doctrine.
