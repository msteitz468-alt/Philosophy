# The Birth of Tragedy — Friedrich Nietzsche (1872; Kaufmann trans.)
> Attic tragedy is the marriage of Apollo and Dionysus. Only as an aesthetic phenomenon is existence justified.

## Bibliographic Note

Friedrich Nietzsche, *Die Geburt der Tragödie aus dem Geiste der Musik* (Leipzig, 1872). Twenty-five numbered sections. Written while Nietzsche was professor of classical philology at Basel, dedicated in the first edition to Richard Wagner (the dedication is **not** in this extract). The 1886 reissue added an “Attempt at a Self-Criticism”; **that preface is not in this collection text**. *Genealogy* III’s pointer to “the preface to *The Birth of Tragedy*” therefore cannot be filled from this ingest.

## Argument Spine

Art evolves by the *Apollinian*–*Dionysian* duality (dream / plastic form / *principium individuationis* versus intoxication / music / shattering of that principle). The Greeks first hid Silenus’s wisdom — best never to have been born — behind the Olympian illusion; then they married the two drives in Attic tragedy, whose chorus is the satyr-wisdom of the Dionysian, given Apollinian dream-images. Tragedy dies when Euripides and Socrates install the theoretical man: “to be beautiful everything must be intelligible.” The Socratic will-to-know is the antagonist of myth and of music. Nietzsche hopes German music (Beethoven to Wagner) and German philosophy (Kant to Schopenhauer) will reopen the Dionysian and rebirth tragedy. The metaphysical thesis, stated more than once: only as an *aesthetic phenomenon* is existence and the world eternally justified. The 1888 books already in the vault recant the Wagner application and the Schopenhauerian “artist’s metaphysics”; they do not recant Dionysus.

## Section-by-Section Summary

**§§1–6. Duality, Silenus, lyric, justification.** Art evolves by the Apollinian–Dionysian duality (sex-duality analogue). Physiologies: dream vs intoxication. Apollo = *principium individuationis* (Schopenhauer’s rowboat, *WWR* I.1.3). Dionysus = shattering of that principle; the reveler becomes a work of art. Nature produces both states without the human artist. Barbarian Dionysia are a witches’ cauldron; Greece first rejects then pacifies them — abrogation of individuation becomes an aesthetic event. Olympian culture is built over Silenus (“not to have been born… second best is to die soon”). “The gods justified human life by living it themselves—the only satisfactory theodicy ever invented.” Homer’s naïveté is a victory of illusion, not Rousseau’s paradise. Original Oneness, “ever suffering and contradictory,” needs illusion to redeem itself. “Apollo found it impossible to live without Dionysus!” Lyric/Archilochus is not the “subjective” artist. Against *WWR* I.3.51: subject/object is worthless in aesthetics. Formula: “Only as *aesthetic phenomenon* can the world be *justified* to all eternity.” `[pages created: 2] [pages updated: 0]`

**§§7–10. Chorus, satyr, tragic myth.** Tragedy arose out of the chorus and was at first nothing but chorus. Religious origins, not political (Aristotle’s phrase rejected). Satyr chorus as living wall (Schiller kept). Metaphysical solace: life is at bottom indestructibly joyful and powerful; art saves the Greek from Buddhistic denial of the will. “Knowledge kills action.” Enchantment: the reveler sees himself as satyr and, as satyr, sees the god. “Greek tragedy as a Dionysian chorus which again and again discharges itself in Apollinian images.” Oedipus = passivity / Dionysian wisdom as crime; Prometheus = action / Janus face. Heroes are masks of Dionysus (Zagreus). Individuation is the source of all suffering. Music endows myth with new depth. Euripides already announced as the killer of myth and music. `[pages created: 1] [pages updated: 0]`

**§§11–15. Death of tragedy; Socratism.** Tragedy died by suicide. Euripides fights the death-struggle; New Attic Comedy is the monument. Spectator transported onto the stage. Euripides is a mask; the daemon is Socrates. Aesthetic Socratism: “Whatever is to be beautiful must also be sensible.” Instinct is the critic, consciousness the creator — *per defectum*; the non-mystic. Plato burns his tragedies; dialogue is poetry’s lifeboat. Three Socratic maxims are “basic formulations of optimism” and “spell the death of tragedy.” Optimistic dialectics drive music out. New type: the *theoretical man* / theoretical optimist. Science’s illusion: thought can know and even *correct* being. Dying Socrates as science’s emblem. Logic bites its own tail → tragic insight → “Practice music, Socrates!” `[pages created: 1] [pages updated: 0]`

**§§16–25. Music, German rebirth, Wagner, dissonance.** Music is “an immediate copy of the will itself” (Schopenhauer endorsed). It gives birth to tragic myth. Tragic joy = joy in the annihilation of the individual; the Will is not affected. Opera = *stilo rappresentativo*, theoretical layman, words master over music. German music “from Bach to Beethoven, from Beethoven to Wagner” plus Kant/Schopenhauer as conceptual Dionysian wisdom. Hoped rebirth of tragedy and of German myth. *Tristan* III: music is the real idea of the world; drama is the reflection. “Dionysus speaks the language of Apollo; and Apollo, finally the language of Dionysus.” §24 restates the justification formula; tragic joy = musical dissonance. §25: man is “dissonance become man”; the two drives in strict proportion; close in “the temple of both deities.” `[pages created: 0] [pages updated: pending synthesis]`

## Whole-Work Assessment

The vault’s first *early* Nietzsche primary, and the book the 1888 cluster has been citing as uningested background. Four things reception had flattened are now sharp.

1. **The duality is a physiology and a metaphysics, not a taste contrast.** Dream / intoxication; *principium individuationis* held vs shattered; original Oneness redeemed by illusion. Kaufmann’s **Apollinian** is the term-layer.

2. **“Only as aesthetic phenomenon” is the thesis, not a purple sentence.** It answers Silenus, not Leibniz. Tragic joy is musical dissonance. Catharsis and moral world-order are refused.

3. **Socrates is first a cultural type.** Aesthetic Socratism (beauty = intelligible); theoretical man; science as the faith that thought can correct being; music-practicing Socrates as the open symbol of a limit. *Twilight*’s physiological Socrates *cites* this book and adds a different diagnosis. Layers.

4. **Wagner is the hoped German rebirth, named.** Bach–Beethoven–Wagner; *Tristan* as the test; opera as the Socratic counterfeit. This is the early Wagnerism *The Case of Wagner* presupposes (“I have loved Wagner”) and recants. Both are now primaries. The tension stays open. No Wagner thinker page.
