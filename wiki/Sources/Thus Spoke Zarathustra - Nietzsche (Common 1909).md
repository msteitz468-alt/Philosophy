# Thus Spoke Zarathustra — Friedrich Nietzsche (1883–85; Thomas Common trans., 1909)

> The missing Nietzsche primary after *The Gay Science*: God is dead is told to a saint who has not heard; the market prefers the last man; the Superman is taught, not as Kaufmann's "overman" and not as Deleuze's superfold. Recurrence is dramatized, not proved.

## Argument Spine

*Also sprach Zarathustra* (1883–85) is the poetic continuation GS §342 hands off: Zarathustra, sick of his wisdom, *goes under*. After ten years on the mountain he descends to teach that man is a bridge, not a goal — the *Übermensch* (Common: **Superman**) — and that God is dead (the forest saint has not heard). The marketplace laughs and asks for the **last man**. He learns to seek companions, not a herd. Part I gives the three metamorphoses (camel–lion–child), the body against backworldsmen, and the bestowing virtue. Part II names will as **self-surpassing** (will to power) and sets the task of **redeeming the "it was"** from revenge. Part III is the thought he almost cannot bear: the vision and the enigma, the convalescent, the seven seals. Part IV gathers **higher men** who are not the Superman; the sign is a laughing lion. This edition is Thomas Common (1909), not Kaufmann and not Hollingdale. Common's "Superman" is a translation choice. The German is *Übermensch*. The phrase *amor fati* is not expected in this book.

## Whole-Work Assessment

The vault's missing Nietzsche primary between *The Gay Science* and *BGE*. Five things reception had flattened are now sharp.

(1) **Superman is not the higher man and not Kaufmann's overman.** Common's English is *Superman* for *Übermensch*. The Prologue teaches a meaning of the earth and a rope over an abyss. Part II: never yet has there been one. Part IV: higher men are bridges and failures; pity for them is the last sin. Deleuze's superfold and Heidegger's last metaphysician are reception.

(2) **The last man is the market's preference**, not a later political slogan. Discovered happiness, the blink, herd without shepherd. They will *take* this and *give* Zarathustra the Superman as a joke.

(3) **Will to Power is first published here.** Part I (a people's table of values); Part II Self-Surpassing (Life must ever surpass itself; not Will to Life). BGE is the later analytic of philosophers. The vault's BGE-first story was a catalog error.

(4) **Redemption of the "It was"** is the hinge that makes return more than cosmology. Revenge = the Will's antipathy to time. Creating Will must will backwards — then silence. Part III's three voices (dwarf's circle, gateway Moment, animals' hurdy-gurdy) and the nausea that the *smallest man* returns keep Deleuze's selective ontology from being an exegesis.

(5) ***Amor fati* is not in this book.** Seven Seals "I love thee, O Eternity" and the child's holy Yea are dramatic cousins. GS §276 remains the name.

The Argument Spine of Step 1 held, with corrections: Will to Power appears already in Part I XV, not only Self-Surpassing; God dies of *pity* and as *witness* in addition to the saint who has not heard; the ass-festival is a relapse the teacher can bless as convalescence. Concurrent collection sessions (HATH, Wagner) claimed the Nietzsche thinker page during this ingest — updates were applied onto their Related block.

## Composition History (added 2026-08-12 from *Ecce Homo*)

The vault now has Nietzsche's own account of how this book was made — [[Sources/Ecce Homo - Nietzsche (Collection 2013)]], the *Zarathustra* chapter. It is self-report from 1888 about work done 1881–85, and nothing in the vault corroborates it, but it is the only account we have.

- **Conception of the fundamental idea** (eternal recurrence, "the highest formula of a life affirmation that can ever be attained"): **August 1881**, beside the Lake of Silvaplana in the Upper Engadine, "not far from Surlei beside a huge pyramidal block of stone." The note he made carried the postscript "Six thousand feet beyond man and time." An "omen" two months earlier at Recoaro, as a sudden change of taste in music.
- **Gestation**: "exactly eighteen months" — the interval belonging to *The Gay Science*, which "already presents Zarathustra's fundamental thought in the last aphorism but one of the fourth book" (§341). Nietzsche's own confirmation of the vault's reading order.
- **Part I**: Rapallo, in a cold and sickly winter — "everything decisive comes to life in defiance of every obstacle." Zarathustra *as a type* arrived on the coastal walks between Zoagli and Portofino: "it was on these walks that he waylaid me."
- **Part II**: conceived in the Engadine. **Part III**: "beneath the halcyon sky of Nice." The composition claim for each: **ten days**, "the whole task having occupied me scarcely a year."
- Wagner died at Venice in February 1883, as Part I was finished.
- The type's "fundamental physiological condition" is the **great health**, and §2 establishes this by quoting GS §382 at length. See [[Concepts/The Great Health (Nietzsche)]].
- The account of **inspiration** in §3 — passivity, involuntariness, rhythm as the measure of force, "the body is inspired" — now has its own page: [[Concepts/Inspiration as Revelation (Nietzsche)]].

Two negative findings from that chapter, verified by search, that bear directly on this page's assessment: **"amor fati" does not occur in it** (confirming point 5 above from the other end — even the 1888 retrospect on this book does not import the phrase), and **"will to power" does not occur in it either**, so the priority claim in point 3 stands on this book's own text, not on Nietzsche's later say-so.
