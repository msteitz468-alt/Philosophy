# Nietzsche Contra Wagner — Friedrich Nietzsche (1888/89; Ludovici)
> Selected from past works, made briefer. Read consecutively they leave no doubt: we are antipodes.

## Bibliographic Note

Friedrich Nietzsche, *Nietzsche contra Wagner. Aktenstücke eines Psychologen* (assembled Turin, Christmas 1888; published 1889). Not a new polemic. Preface: “The following chapters have been selected from past works of mine… Some of them date back as far as 1877… more brief.” The 1888 thesis is the *selection*: “we are antipodes.” An essay “for psychologists and *not* for Germans.”

## Argument Spine

The dossier proves a break that *The Case of Wagner* then writes up as a case. He had misread Wagner and Schopenhauer as Dionysian overflowing; he had bestowed *himself* upon them. There are two sufferers: overflowing vitality (Dionysian, tragic luxury) and reduced vitality (repose, spasm, revenge upon life). Wagner and Schopenhauer answer the second; they deny and slander life; they are his antipodes. Objections to the music are **physiological** — “Æsthetic is indeed nothing more than applied physiology.” He admires Wagner only where Wagner sets *himself* to music (the master of the very small, the Orpheus of secret misery) — not the fresco-Wagner, not the stage. Bayreuth 1876 is the inner farewell (anti-Semitism; the *décadent* on his knees before the Cross). Illness is the answer when one doubts one’s mission. The Epilogue names ***amor fati*** as “the very core of my being,” credits illness with a higher health and even with his philosophy, and asks for a convalescent art — mocking, nimble, only for artists; Greeks “superficial — from *profundity*.”

Much of the wording is already in *The Gay Science* (1887 Preface; §370). The 1888 act is to *bind those pages into a Wagner file* and declare antipodes.

## Section-by-Section Summary

**Preface.** Turin, Christmas 1888. Selected, abridged, some from 1877. Antipodes. For psychologists, not Germans. Readers in Vienna, Petersburg, Copenhagen, Stockholm, Paris, New York — none in “Europe’s Flat-land.” Italians; *Quousque tandem, Crispi*; Triple Alliance as *mésalliance* with the Empire.

**Wherein I Admire Wagner.** Artists miss what they do best. Wagner is unmatched in tones of the suffering and the last short gladness; Orpheus of secret misery; master of the microscopical, of pieces often one bar long. He refuses this and wants vast walls. “I admire Wagner wherever he sets *himself* to music.”

**Wherein I Raise Objections.** Not healthy, least of all where it speaks of Wagner. Objections are physiological, not aesthetic formulae. Cannot breathe; the foot wants dance and march; stomach, heart, circulation, hoarseness (Géraudel’s pastilles). No soul — the body needs relief, light rhythms, weightless melody. Wagner makes one ill. Anti-theatrical: stage success drops a man from sight. Two Wagners: unique musician *and* mimomaniac. Theory said drama is the object, music the means; practice said **the attitude is the end**. Bayreuth: the individual lies; one becomes neighbour, herd, Wagnerite.

**Wagner as a Danger.** “Unending melody” is entering the sea: lose footing, swim. Old music made one dance; sobriety of measure. Wagner overthrew “the physiological first principle of all music.” Danger: degeneration of rhythm into *chaos*; music slave to attitudes. *Haut-relief* at all costs sins against Mozart; it acts on the mob, the blasé, the diseased, Wagnerites.

**A Music Without a Future.** Music is the last plant of a culture, a swan song (Dutch/Gothic; Handel/Luther; Mozart/Louis XIV; Beethoven and Rossini/eighteenth century). Wagner’s soil is already subsiding. Catholic feeling + “national” sagas as first condition. Reaction and wars may give sudden glory, not a future. “The Germans themselves have no future.”

**We Antipodes.** He began with hope: nineteenth-century pessimism as overflowing luxury; Wagner as Dionysian earthquake. Misinterpretation: “what I *bestowed* upon Wagner and Schopenhauer—myself.” Two sufferers (overflow vs reduced vitality). Wagner and Schopenhauer deny life — antipodes. Dionysian plenitude may handle the horrible; the pauper in vitality needs a Saviour-God and “humaneness.” Epicurus and the Christian as hedonism beyond honesty. Artist-test: creation from loathing of life or from plenitude? Goethe overflow; Flaubert hate (`l’homme n’est rien, l’œuvre est tout`). “Disinterestedness”—principle of decadence, will to nonentity.

**Where Wagner Is at Home.** France of taste, not the *Norddeutsche*; Schopenhauer more at home in French; Heine. Paris is Wagner’s soil; *l’âme moderne*. Wagner’s 1871 scoff at Paris was disgraceful. In Germany he is a misapprehension. French romanticism and Wagner: literary, virtuosos, enemies of logic, ill.

**Wagner as the Apostle of Chastity.** Verse against German kneeling to Rome. Sensuality/chastity need not be a tragic contrast (Hafis, Goethe). *Parsifal*: wish it were satyric self-laughter; if serious, rancour, revenge, “a bad work,” “an outrage upon morality.” Feuerbach’s “healthy sensuality” once saved him; did hatred of life win, as in Flaubert? *The Case of Wagner* Postscript later *admires* *Parsifal* as seduction. This chapter keeps the earlier open question, then condemns the serious reading.

**How I Got Rid of Wagner.** Summer 1876, first Bayreuth festival: leave-taking in the soul. Double-faced; return to Germany; “even to anti-Semitism.” The triumphant man “fell helpless and broken on his knees before the Christian cross.” Then illness, tiredness, suspicion, solitude. He took sides against himself; that is the road to the mission. Illness answers when we doubt the mission or make things easy; return to health means a *heavier* burden.

**The Psychologist Speaks.** Select cases suffocate by sympathy; corruption of higher men is the rule; “too late.” Success is the greatest liar; the work invents the creator. Byron, Musset, Poe, Leopardi, Kleist, Gogol: torment once found out. Woman’s superstition that love can do everything. Profound suffering ranks and separates; Epicurism and science can be masks (Hamlet).

**Epilogue.** Indebted to the hardest years. “Everything necessary… one should *love* it.… *Amor fati*: this is the very core of my being.” To prolonged illness he owes a higher health “which grows stronger under everything that does not actually kill it” — “even my philosophy.” Great suffering emancipates by suspicion; makes deeper, not better. Second taste; convalescent art, only for artists; cheerfulness. No more unveiling truth at all costs. Greeks “superficial — from *profundity*.” Truth perhaps a woman / *Baubo*. (Wording tracks the 1887 *Gay Science* Preface.)

**Levy appendix (1878).** Bayreuth as death-agony of the last Art; he had depicted an ideal monster; Catholic revival after hoping German myth would disgust decaying Christianity; either one still *requires* Wagner’s art or one does not. Do not fold these into the 1888 spine.

## Whole-Work Assessment

The missing companion *The Case of Wagner* named. CW is the 1888 *case*; this is the *dossier* — earlier pages selected to show the opposition was not a late whim.

What it adds that CW does not:

1. **Genre.** Compilation and abridgment. Date-stamps (1877, 1876 Bayreuth) are load-bearing.
2. **Physiology as method.** Aesthetic formulae are clothing; the *petit fait vrai* is breath, foot, viscera. “Unending melody” overthrows music’s physiological first principle.
3. **Admiration with a restriction.** Orpheus of misery / master of the bar-long piece — “wherever he sets *himself* to music.”
4. **Two sufferers / antipodes.** Same distinction as *GS* §370, now the title of a Wagner chapter. He admits the early misreading.
5. **1876 scene.** Anti-Semitism and the Cross, not only Schopenhauer and *Parsifal*.
6. ***Amor fati* named in 1888** as core, joined to illness — selected from the 1887 Preface, not a new coining. See [[Concepts/Amor Fati (Love of Fate)]].

*Parsifal* is still a question here; CW’s Postscript is the later admiration-as-seduction. Do not flatten. *Birth of Tragedy* remains the uningested early praise.
