# Parerga and Paralipomena, Volume 2 — Arthur Schopenhauer (1851; Payne, Oxford 1974/2000)

> The *paralipomena*: stray yet systematically arranged thoughts for a second visit. Thirty-one short essays that presuppose *The World as Will and Representation*. Payne, not Roehr/Janaway.

## Argument Spine

The unpublished draft preface (quoted in Volume 1’s Cambridge apparatus) assigns most of Volume 2 to the *paralipomena*: supplements that presuppose the system. Payne’s title-page subtitle is “Short Philosophical Essays.” The volume motto is Seneca: *Eleusis servat quod ostendat revisentibus* — “Eleusis keeps something that it can disclose only on a second visit.” This is not a second system and not a recantation. It deepens method and intellect; restates the thing-in-itself / phenomenon cut; dismisses pantheism as a euphemism for atheism; adds ethics and politics; then the cluster that reception flattened into “pessimism” (indestructibility, vanity, suffering, suicide, affirmation and denial of the will-to-live); then religion as allegory; aesthetics; the intellectual life (thinking, reading, style); and the notorious “On Women.”

This is **Volume 2 only**, E. F. J. Payne’s 1974 Clarendon translation, reissued 2000. Converted from the OCR PDF. Distinctive Payne renderings to keep in quotations: **will-to-live** (Roehr/Janaway: *will to life*); **idea** / **representation** mixed; **phenomenon** for *Erscheinung*. Do not create a Payne thinker page. Do not treat this file as Volume 1 (already ingested, Cambridge 2014). Do not ingest *WWR* II (MOBI still in `raw/`).

He still does not call his own philosophy “pessimism.”

## Whole-Work Assessment

The *paralipomena* are what the unpublished draft preface said they were: supplements for a second visit, not a second system. Four things reception had flattened are now sharp.

(1) **Compassion is the moral incentive; eudaemonology was the accommodation.** Volume 1’s Aphorisms remain a lower art under a false standpoint. Volume 2 Chs. VIII–IX give the higher ethics: *Mitleid*, *tat tvam asi*, right as negation of injury. Do not flatten them.

(2) **Suicide is not denial.** Ch. XIII’s only valid objection is ascetic: suicide substitutes an apparent exit for the real one (*nolle*). Ch. XIV restates *WWR* IV: denial is not annihilation of a substance. Generation is the world-knot.

(3) **Religion is allegory.** Ch. XV’s dialogue (Demopheles / Philalethes) is two faces, not a verdict. The author’s voice: no true religion *sensu proprio*; the real cut among religions is optimism vs pessimism. Kant’s moral theology appears in Demopheles’s mouth as a practical hypothesis.

(4) **“On Women” is a primary rant.** Record it. Do not sanitize. Do not let it swamp thinking-for-oneself, noise, or the porcupine.
