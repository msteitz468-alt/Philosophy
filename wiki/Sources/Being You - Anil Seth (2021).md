# Being You — Anil Seth (2021)

## Argument Spine
Consciousness science should not try to solve Chalmers's hard problem head-on, nor sweep experience under the carpet. The **real problem** is to explain, predict, and control the *properties* of conscious experience — level, content, and self — in terms of embodied, embedded brains. Perception of the world and of the self are both **controlled hallucinations**: Bayesian best guesses designed for utility, not accuracy. Their origin and primary function is not representation but the control and regulation of the living body. We are conscious selves *because* we are **beast machines** — living bodies that predict and control their own physiological condition. Consciousness tracks life more than intelligence; that reorientation pulls us closer to other animals and farther from fleshless AI.

## Whole-Work Assessment

*Being You* is the vault's first primary for **Anil Seth** and the missing life-first voice in the predictive-processing cluster. Clark gave the mechanism and the extension; Hohwy gave the systematic PEM and the seclusion; Seth gives the *beast machine*: consciousness tracks being alive more than being intelligent, and the self is a control-oriented perception of the body's essential variables. The shared slogan "controlled hallucination" is now primary from both Sussex colleagues, with a documented owner-story split (Seth: Frith / Jain, not Clark).

The methodological contribution is the **real problem** — explain, predict, and control properties of experience until the hard-problem intuition evaporates (vitalism analogy). That is a third dissolve route alongside Clark's meta-problem attack and Dehaene's access-is-the-hard-one, and it is now a dedicated contradiction with Chalmers. IIT is kept as an I+I insight and refused as Φ-as-identity. Functionalism, left open in chapter 1 (Go vs weather), is later a "suspicious agnosticism": a silicon PP beast machine would "probably not" be conscious, but life has not been *demonstrated* necessary.

The Argument Spine held. Corrections of emphasis: (1) "controlled hallucination" is not Clark's gift to Seth; (2) FEP is explicitly *not* a theory of consciousness; (3) Nagel is adopted as the mark of consciousness in chapter 1 and then unnamed in the animal chapter; (4) Searle is a structural cousin on life-as-fire-in-the-equations, not a cited source.

*Surfing Uncertainty* (Clark 2016) and a Tononi/Koch IIT primary remain outstanding. *Descartes' Error* (Damasio) is still in `raw/`. Dehaene's GNW ingest completed in parallel; this book does not create or rewrite his pages.
