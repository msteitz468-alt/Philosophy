## Argument Spine

Wolff's formula is kept as the most general expression: *Nihil est sine ratione cur potius sit quam non sit* — nothing is without a reason why it is rather than it is not. That formula is an abstraction (law of homogeneity) from **four** ground-consequent relations, each a synthetic a priori law governing one class of objects for the subject:

1. **Becoming** — law of causality (alterations of real objects)
2. **Knowing** — truth of judgments (reason / concepts)
3. **Being** — position in space and succession in time (pure intuition; mathematics)
4. **Acting** — law of motivation (the one object that is one's own will)

Subject and object are correlative; the principle never applies to a subject or to a thing-in-itself. It is the mother of all sciences and the principle of all explanation — *inside* the world as representation. To ask for a proof of the principle already assumes it. The 1847 book exists to stop philosophers (including Kant) from saying "ground" *per se* in a transcendent sense.

## Whole-Work Assessment

The vault already had *WWR* Vol. 1 and a thinker page that treated PSR as "space, time, and causality" in one breath, then a contradiction page that said Schopenhauer holds "No Sufficient Reason." This 1847 book is the correction.

Four things are now sharp. (1) PSR is **four** a priori laws from subject–object correlativity, Wolff's formula only as homogeneity's abstraction. (2) It **cannot be proved** without circularity and **cannot be applied** to the world as totality or the thing-in-itself — that is the real contrast with Leibniz, not a denial of reasons inside science. (3) Everyday intuition is **intellectual** (§21); Kant's Second Analogy is the wrong proof of a fact already secured; animals have understanding. (4) 1813 ≠ 1847: categories and causal will belong to the dissertation; the Cambridge text is the rewrite after *WWR*.

The Argument Spine held. Correction to the stub: Hegel-abuse is already in the 1847 Preface and §34, not only later; Will-as-noumenon is **not** argued in FR (motivation as inner causality is; body=will is pointed to *WWR*).
