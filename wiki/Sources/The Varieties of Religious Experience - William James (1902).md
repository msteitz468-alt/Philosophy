# The Varieties of Religious Experience — William James (1902)

## Argument Spine

The Gifford Lectures on Natural Religion (Edinburgh, 1901–1902) study **personal** religion from documents, not institutions or systematic theology. James first describes "Man's Religious Appetites" (the planned second course, their "Satisfaction through Philosophy," was postponed). Origins — neurosis, sex, the "medical materialist" diagnosis — do not settle value; **fruits** do. Religion for these lectures is "the feelings, acts, and experiences of individual men in their solitude, so far as they apprehend themselves to stand in relation to whatever they may consider the divine." Two temperaments divide the field: **healthy-mindedness** (once-born) and the **sick soul** (twice-born). Conversion, saintliness, and mysticism are the extreme documents of the second type. Theories and creeds are secondary; feelings and conduct are the constants. The common nucleus is an **uneasiness** plus a **solution**: the higher part of the self is "conterminous and continuous with a MORE of the same quality" outside it. On the hither side that MORE is the **subconscious / subliminal** continuation of conscious life; on the farther side James's own **over-belief** is **piecemeal supernaturalism** — God (or a larger-than-conscious power) produces real effects in nature. Religious experience does **not** settle that the power is infinite or unique. Checked against Lecture XX and the Postscript: the Preface did not misrepresent the argument.

## Whole-Work Assessment

*Varieties* is the vault's first James primary that is not a theory of truth, and the missing source *Pragmatism* itself cites for the hypothesis of God. Its lasting contribution is a **method plus a typology plus an over-belief**. Method: existential origin does not decide spiritual worth; fruits do ("on the whole"). Typology: healthy-mindedness and the sick soul (Newman's once-born / twice-born) — *not* the 1907 tender/tough table. Over-belief: the common nucleus is uneasiness + deliverance via a MORE continuous with the higher self; hither side = Myers's subliminal; farther side = piecemeal supernaturalism, a larger power that produces real effects and need not be infinite or unique.

Three flattenings blocked. (1) **1907 into 1902**: cash-value and Peirce's principle *are* already in Lecture XVIII; "the true is the expedient" and the hotel corridor are not. Meliorism in IV–V is evolutionist progress-religion, not the 1907 midpoint. (2) **Mysticism into Neoplatonism**: four marks are a psychological classifier; Plotinus and Dionysius are instances of a monistic drift James then withdraws. (3) **Conversion into Augustine's grace**: the garden is a document of the divided will; the subliminal is a door, not prevenient grace.

What the book does *not* do: settle immortality; defend polytheism (only keeps it inside the testimony's bounds); replace living religion with a science of religions; write *The Will to Believe*. *Principles of Psychology* remains outstanding. The religion-as-philosophy leg is opened from a thinker already present; SCHEMA's active leg is still AI & Cognitive Science.
