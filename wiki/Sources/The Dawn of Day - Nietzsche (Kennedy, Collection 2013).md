# The Dawn of Day — Friedrich Nietzsche (1881; 1886 Preface; J. M. Kennedy)

> The missing free-spirit book between *Human, All Too Human* I–III and *The Gay Science*: a campaign that treats morality itself as a problem.

**Two voices.** 1881 is the mole: 575 thoughts on moral prejudices. 1886 Preface (same season as BGE and the HATH second preface) names the task after the fact — undermining *faith in morals*, morality as Circe of philosophers, *autosuppression of morals*, slow reading. Do not flatten 1881 into 1886.

## Argument Spine

The 1886 Preface states the whole: for thousands of years philosophers built on *faith in morals* as the safest foundation; this book tunnels under that faith and treats morality as a *problem* — which looks immoral only because morality has forbidden the question. Kant's critique of reason is diagnosed as a detour to protect a "moral kingdom"; the right target was never reason but the moral seduction of philosophers. The book's own "thou shalt" is not a new table of values but the last cry of morals still audible: we will not return to what is unworthy of belief. Formula: *the autosuppression of morals*. The 1881 books then do the mining — origin of moral feelings in custom, dreams, fear, and pity; the long prehistory that later *Genealogy* II will call morality of custom; pity as a danger well before *The Antichrist*'s technic; a free-spirit ethics of solitude, slow reading, and further flight (§575: other birds will fly farther).

## Argument Spine — Corrected After Reading (Step 3)

The Preface's claim holds: the book tunnels under faith in morals. Two corrections.

**The 1881 centre is custom and the feeling of power, not yet "genealogy."** Book I §9 (*Conception of the Morality of Custom*) is the load-bearing thesis — morality *is* obedience to tradition as command. Book II §103 denies morals as one denies alchemy. Books II–IV make the **feeling / desire / love / demon of power** the everyday psychology. None of this is *Genealogy*'s grey critique of the *value* of values, and none of it is will to power.

**Pity is already a campaign.** The 1888 *technic* of nihilism has an 1881 psychology (multiplier of suffering; comedy; suspicion of the neighbour's joy). Do not weld them.

The close completes the Preface's dawn as *ascent*: mole becomes aeronaut; other birds will fly farther; Phoenix §568 — "There is many a dawn of day."

## Key Claims (with verified absences)

- **Faith in morals** (1886 Preface): the philosophers' unexamined foundation; Circe; Kant's critique as cover for a moral kingdom; *autosuppression of morals*.
- **Morality of custom** (§9): morality = obedience to tradition as command; the free man is *immoral*.
- **Two denials** (§103): deny morals as alchemy (hypotheses); also deny *immorality* as a reason to feel immoral.
- **Feeling / love / demon of power** (§§23, 113, 189, 262): 1881 psychology. **Not** will to power.
- **Pity** multiplies suffering (§134); comedy (§383). Earlier than A §7.
- **Intellectual conscience** already named (§149, §298, §370).
- **Close** §573 / §575: cast the skin; other birds will fly farther.
