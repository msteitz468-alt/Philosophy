# Consciousness and the Brain — Stanislas Dehaene (2014)

> The vault's first primary for **global neuronal workspace**: consciousness is not a dualist residue but *conscious access* — global broadcasting of selected information across cortex — and the hard problem is a prescientific intuition that will evaporate once the "easy" functions are explained.

## Argument Spine
Descartes's challenge still stands: how can a machine report its thoughts and reason flexibly? Dualism was his *failure mode*, not his starting point — he already gave a mechanical model of body, sleep, and memory. The modern answer is experimental. Narrow consciousness to **conscious access** (reportable entry of attended information), not vigilance, attention, self, or ineffable "phenomenal awareness." Create **minimal contrasts** between seen and unseen stimuli; treat introspection as raw data, not a method. The brain then yields reproducible **signatures of consciousness** (late P3, ignition of a prefronto-parietal web, long-distance synchrony). These are explained by the **global neuronal workspace**: giant long-axon neurons broadcast a winning representation so it can be held, rerouted, and reported — Dennett's "fame in the brain." Unconscious processing is deep; consciousness adds duration, flexibility, and social sharing. The same signatures diagnose residual awareness in vegetative patients, exist (slowly) in infants, and appear in monkeys. Phenomenal consciousness distinct from access is a "slippery slope to dualism"; Chalmers's hard problem is mislabeled — the easy problem is the hard one, and qualia will go the way of vitalism. A machine with workspace, learning, and autonomy would be a "free-willing" machine: free will is autonomous deliberation, not quantum swerve.

## Whole-Work Assessment

Outstanding Sources asked for a GNW primary because Chalmers had already filed global workspace as a failed reductive program. This book is that primary. It does three jobs the vault could not do from Hohwy's citation or Chalmers's survey.

First, it *defines* the experimental object: [[Concepts/Conscious Access (Dehaene)]], carved off from vigilance, attention, self, and leftover "phenomenal awareness." The [[Concepts/Minimal Contrast Method (Dehaene)]] plus reports-as-data is the laboratory sibling of [[Concepts/Heterophenomenology (Dennett)]].

Second, it supplies the mechanism and the markers. [[Concepts/Global Neuronal Workspace (Dehaene)]] is Baars plus Changeux anatomy: long-distance pyramids, ignition as phase transition, a taxonomy of the unconscious, a restless Darwinian stream. [[Concepts/Signatures of Consciousness and Ignition (Dehaene)]] (P3, late gamma, brain web) are robust enough to change ICU practice and to show a slow workspace in infants and macaques. Hohwy's ignition page is no longer a second-hand loan.

Third, it answers Descartes's [[Concepts/The Two Tests and the Animal-Machine (Descartes)|two tests]] with [[Concepts/Functions of Conscious Broadcasting (Dehaene)]] (sample, hold, route, share) and then *grants* Descartes the composition-of-signs point as the uniquely human extra. Ch. 7 is also the first vault text that names Chalmers from the NCC side and refuses the hard problem as mislabeled vitalism.

The Argument Spine of Step 1 held. One correction of emphasis: Ch. 3 does not restate the two tests; it answers them functionally. Ch. 5 never names Multiple Drafts — the finish-line tension with Dennett is unmarked in the book and marked in the vault. Ch. 7's schizophrenia and anti-NMDA material is a speculative "disease of the workspace," not a completed theory.

Contradictions opened: [[Contradictions/Dehaene vs Chalmers - Access Consciousness vs the Hard Problem]] (new); finish-line tension with Dennett (flagged on both sides, not a new page); Hohwy keeps ignition and declines the hard problem Dehaene deflates. IIT (Tononi) remains outstanding as a *primary*; [[Thinkers/Anil Seth]] *Being You* now supplies the vault's first sustained treatment of Φ as measure-not-identity ([[Concepts/Phi and Integrated Information (Seth)]]). Dehaene's liberal functionalist machine criterion is a named foil in [[Concepts/Machine Minds (Seth)]].
