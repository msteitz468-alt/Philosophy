# The Antichrist — Friedrich Nietzsche (1888; Mencken trans., from the 2013 22-work collection)

> This book belongs to the most rare of men. Some men are born posthumously. The transvaluation of all values begins here.

## Identification

*Der Antichrist. Fluch auf das Christenthum* — written September 1888 in Turin, intended as Book I of the *Revaluation of All Values* (*Umwerthung aller Werthe*). *Twilight of the Idols* is dated 30 September 1888, “on the day when the first book of the Revaluation of All Values was completed.” Published 1895.

This English is **H. L. Mencken** (1920). Distinctive Mencken markers: the signed **Friedrich W. Nietzsche**; “the weak and the botched shall perish”; “Practical sympathy for the botched and the weak”; **transvaluation** (not Kaufmann’s *revaluation*); “sirocco to us.” Not Kaufmann, not Hollingdale, not the Ludovici/Levy line used for *The Case of Wagner* in the same EPUB.

## Argument Spine

The book is not a pamphlet against churchgoing. It is Book I of the Revaluation: a judgment on Christianity as the most fatal form of *décadence*, the practice of pity, and the priest’s inversion of the evangel of Jesus. The reader is restricted in advance (Preface): mountain-tops, intellectual integrity to the verge of hardness, indifference to whether truth is a profit or a fatality, contempt for politics and nationalism. §1: we are Hyperboreans; the formula of happiness is “a Yea, a Nay, a straight line, a goal” (same formula as *Twilight*, Maxims §44). §2 states the new table: good = what augments the feeling of power / the will to power / power itself; evil = what springs from weakness; happiness = the feeling that power increases. Christianity is then read as the religion that made the contrary type — the herd animal, the sick brute-man — into the only type willed. Pity is not a side-vice; it is the practice of nihilism. The middle of the book splits Jesus from Christianity: the Redeemer is a physiological type (Dostoevsky’s missing case; Mencken writes “idiocy,” not Kaufmann’s “idiot”), a Buddhist-like inwardness that does not resist; Paul is the genius of hatred who falsifies that type into a doctrine of sin, judgment, and immortality. The close condemns the church as parasite and dynamite (“equality of souls before God”), then dates a new era from today: the transvaluation of all values.

## Section-by-Section Summary

**Preface.** This book belongs to the most rare of men; perhaps none of them is yet alive. They may be among those who understand *Zarathustra*. “Some men are born posthumously.” Conditions of understanding: intellectual integrity to the verge of hardness; mountain-tops; politics and nationalism as gabble; indifference to whether truth is profit or fatality; courage for the forbidden; seven solitudes; new ears, new eyes, a new conscience; reverence for self. The rest are “merely humanity.” Signed **Friedrich W. Nietzsche**.

**§§1–4. Hyperboreans and the table.** Modernity made them ill: lazy peace, cowardly compromise, the “virtuous dirtiness of the modern Yea and Nay.” Formula of happiness: “a Yea, a Nay, a straight line, a goal.” **§2** is the table of the Revaluation — see [[Concepts/Formula of Values (Nietzsche)]]. First principle of “charity”: the weak and the botched shall perish; one should help them to it. The problem is not what shall replace mankind (man is an end) but what *type* must be bred. The more valuable type has appeared as a lucky accident, “even a sort of superman,” never as deliberately willed. “Progress” is a modern idea; today’s European falls below the Renaissance.

**§§5–7. Christianity as war on the higher type; pity as technic.** Christianity has waged war to the death on the higher type and made an ideal of antagonism to the instincts of sound life. “Rottenness” is *décadence*, not a moral accusation: an organism is corrupt when it prefers what injures it. Life is instinct for growth, accumulation of forces, power; when will to power fails, disaster. The highest values now are *décadence*-values / nihilism under holiest names. Pity is Christianity’s practice: a depressant that multiplies suffering, thwarts selection, preserves the botched. Schopenhauer was right that by pity life is denied: “pity is the technic of nihilism.” See [[Concepts/Pity as Practice of Nihilism (Nietzsche)]].

**§§8–15. Theologians, Kant, imaginary world.** The enemy is the theological instinct (also in “idealists”). Faith = closing one’s eyes on incurable falsehood. Criterion: whatever a theologian regards as true must be false — a *transvaluation* that calls what damages life “true.” Theological blood ruined German philosophy; Protestantism is its *peccatum originale*. Kant’s success is theological: the “true world” and morality as the world’s essence are “the two most vicious errors.” Impersonal duty / “good for its own sake” is decay; each must invent his own virtue, his “own categorical imperative.” “Practical reason” dresses a dearth of intellectual conscience; the philosopher is a development of the priest. Free spirits *are* already a transvaluation of “true” and “not true.” Man is among the beasts, “the most botched… and the sickliest”; consciousness / “pure spirit” is a symptom of organic imperfection. Christian morality and religion have no contact with actuality — imaginary causes and effects from hatred of the natural. Preponderance of pains over pleasures is “the formula for *décadence*.”

**§§16–19. Degeneration of the god-type.** A self-believing people projects power into a god who can benefit *and* injure (religion as gratitude). Gods are either will to power (national) or incapacity for power (must be “good”). The Christian god is low-water of the god-type: contradiction of life, nothingness deified, “will to nothingness… made holy.” Northern Europe failed to kill him — “not a single new god,” only “Christian monotono-theism.” This is *not* the GS madman. See [[Concepts/Death of God (Nietzsche)]].

**§§20–23. Buddhism vs Christianity.** Both are nihilistic *décadence* religions, but Buddhism is “a hundred times as realistic,” already “beyond good and evil”: not a struggle with *sin* but a struggle with *suffering*. Two physiological facts; Buddha treats depression by hygiene; “egoism is a duty.” Prayer and asceticism are not included. Christianity is the religion of the subjugated: sin-talk, grace, hatred of body, hygiene, intellect, senses, joy. Among barbarians it masters “beasts of prey” by making them ill. Buddhism is for closing, over-wearied civilizations; Europe is “not yet ripe.” Christianity’s three ingenuities: faith, hope, and charity.

**§§24–27. Israel and the priest.** Christianity is “not a reaction against Jewish instincts; it is their inevitable product.” Jews are “the very opposite of *décadents*”; they *use* décadence; for the priestly class “décadence is no more than a means.” Israel’s history is a type of “denaturiz[ing] all natural values”: Jahveh from national power-god to clerical weapon; a “moral order of the world” inverts cause and effect. The priest is a parasitical variety of man; “sins” are his reliable weapons. Christianity is the priestly instinct pushed to deny even “Jewish reality” / “the church.” The Gospel Jesus is a “saintly anarchist” / “political criminal” who “died for his *own* sins,” not others’.

**§§28–35. Psychology of the Redeemer.** Neither Renan’s “genius” nor “hero.” Unevangelical = hero. Key: “resist not evil!” Two physiologies: instinctive hatred of reality, and exclusion of all hostility, from extreme pain-susceptibility → “a religion of love,” “sublime super-development of hedonism.” The type reaches us distorted. Against Renan’s *impérieux* fanatic: “recrudescent childishness”; an “anti-realist” who “speaks only of inner things.” “It is greatly to be regretted that no Dostoyevsky lived in the neighbourhood of this most interesting décadent.” Gospels lack guilt, punishment, reward; “sin” abolished. Not a new *faith* but a “way of life.” He died not to “save mankind” but “to show mankind how to live.” “The ‘kingdom of heaven’ is a state of the heart—not something to come ‘beyond the world’ or ‘after death.’” See [[Concepts/Psychology of the Redeemer (Nietzsche)]].

**§§36–41. The church out of denial of the Gospels.** World-historical irony: the church is built from *denial* of what the bearer of glad tidings put behind him. From the death on the cross, Christian history is a “progressively clumsier misunderstanding of an original symbolism.” Priestly inventions — the other world, last judgment, immortality of the soul — are instruments of torture by which the priest remains master. **§39:** “at bottom there was only one Christian, and he died on the cross. The ‘Gospels’ died on the cross.” What was then called Gospels was a *Dysangelium*. Not faith in salvation but the *way of life* is Christian. The disciples did not take the example of dying free of *ressentiment*. Revenge demanded recompense and judgment; the already-fulfilled kingdom was recast as a future last act. “The One God and the Only Son of God: both were products of *ressentiment*.” “God gave his son as a sacrifice” ends the gospels (pagan sacrifice of the innocent).

**§§42–47. Paul the dysangelist.** The cross ended an effort to found a “Buddhistic peace movement” and happiness on earth. Paul is the genius for hatred who nails the Saviour to *his* cross and shifts the centre of gravity to the lie of the “risen” Jesus. He needed the *death*, not the life; he willed power. Personal immortality as reward lures “the botched.” “Equal rights for all” is forged from *ressentiment* of the masses against the pathos of distance; “the gospel of the ‘lowly’ lowers.” The Gospels already show decay in the primitive community; Paul, “with the cynical logic of a rabbi,” only concludes it. “The Christian is simply a Jew of the ‘reformed’ confession.” “Paul was the greatest of all apostles of revenge.” Only Pilate is honourable: “What is truth?” **§47:** “We deny that God is God.” Formula: *deus, qualem Paulus creavit, dei negatio.* Faith vetoes science. See [[Concepts/Paul as Falsifier of the Evangel (Nietzsche)]].

**§§48–55. Faith vs science; the holy lie.** Genesis is priest-psychology: science is the forbidden *per se*, the original sin. Guilt, punishment, and the “moral order of the world” were set up against cause and effect so the priest can rule through the invention of sin. “Faith makes blessed: *therefore*, it lies.” One is not converted to Christianity — “one must first be sick enough for it.” “Faith” means the will to avoid knowing what is true; philology is *ephexis* in interpretation. Blood is the worst testimony to truth (quoting *Zarathustra*). Great intellects are sceptical; the believer is a means, not a goal. Convictions can be more dangerous than lies. The “holy lie” (God, revelation, law) is the priestly type — Jews, Christians, Confucius, Manu, Mohammed, Plato; Kant’s categorical imperative is on the same road.

**§§56–61. Manu, Rome, Islam, Renaissance, Luther.** Manu is intellectually superior to the Bible because nobles, philosophers, and warriors keep the whip-hand. Caste ratifies a natural order of three physiological types; inequality of rights is the condition of any rights; the anarchist and the Christian have the same ancestry. Report the caste language as diagnosis of rank; do not expand it into a program the vault endorses. *Twilight* already has Manu as breeding vs Christian taming; this book adds the science/priest war and preserve-vs-destroy as two *ends* of the holy lie. The *imperium Romanum* was destroyed by Christian vampires, not by Teutons. **Epicurus** had already fought subterranean “Christianity” (guilt, punishment, immortality) until Paul. “Nihilist and Christian: they rhyme in German, and they do more than rhyme.” Christianity also destroyed the Moorish/Islamic harvest. Frederick II: war with Rome, peace with Islam. The Renaissance was the transvaluation of Christian values — even “Cæsar Borgia as pope.” Luther restored the church; Protestantism is the uncleanest, most incurable Christianity. Germans: Reformation, Leibniz, Kant, the war of “liberation,” the empire — every time a futile substitute.

**§62. Condemnation.** “I condemn Christianity; I bring against the Christian church the most terrible of all the accusations that an accuser has ever had in his mouth.” Greatest of imaginable corruptions; every value into worthlessness, every truth into a lie. It lives by distress and creates distress. The worm of sin is its gift. “The ‘equality of souls before God’ — this fraud… this explosive concept, ending in revolution… — this is Christian dynamite.” Parasitism as the only practice; the beyond as the will to deny all reality; the cross as conspiracy against health, beauty, well-being, intellect, kindness of soul — against life itself. “I call Christianity the one great curse, the one great intrinsic depravity, the one great instinct of revenge… the one immortal blemish upon the human race.” Mankind reckons time from the *dies nefastus* of the first day of Christianity. “Why not rather from its last?—From today?—The transvaluation of all values!…”

## Whole-Work Assessment

The vault’s first primary of Book I of the Revaluation, and the book *Twilight* was written as recreation *from*. Three things reception and the earlier Nietzsche pages had flattened:

1. **The §2 table is published doctrine, not a notebook slogan.** Good / evil / happiness as power is here a *value-formula* for the Revaluation, not *Zarathustra*’s Life-must-surpass-itself and not BGE’s morphology of philosophers. Pity is then the *practice* that inverts the table.
2. **Jesus ≠ Christianity.** The Redeemer is a physiological type (inner kingdom, non-resistance, way of life, no guilt). Paul is a later falsifier who needed the death and the risen Jesus. Do not dump *Genealogy*’s “Jewish priest” onto either figure without this split.
3. **“God is dead” is not this book’s claim.** A denies *this* God as a crime against life and as *dei negatio* (*deus, qualem Paulus creavit*). GS remains the sequence (shadows → madman → still pious toward truth). *Zarathustra* remains the pity-death and the murder of the witness.

What it adds to pages already on disk: [[Concepts/Will to Power (Nietzsche)]] gets the published triad; [[Concepts/Decadence (Nietzsche)]] gets Christianity as the complete *décadence*-religion (and Jews as *users* of décadence, not décadents); [[Concepts/Ressentiment (Nietzsche)]] gets Paul as “greatest of all apostles of revenge”; [[Concepts/Intellectual Conscience (Nietzsche)]] gets faith as the will to avoid knowing; [[Concepts/Morality as Anti-Nature (Nietzsche)]] gets Manu vs Bible as two *ends* of the holy lie; [[Thinkers/Epicurus]] gets the last published reference (A §58). The notebooks compiled as *The Will to Power* remain notebooks; this book is the published first strike of the Revaluation they announced.

The Argument Spine written in Step 1 holds. Mencken’s “transvaluation” is Kaufmann’s “revaluation”; one doctrine, two English words.
